# 🏢️ For Businesses — Why xyzos

A short, honest brief for decision-makers. Everything here is either real
today (harness-proven) or explicitly marked as roadmap. No vaporware. 🚫

## 🧩 What you're actually buying

**xyzos is a local operating culture, not another Electron app.** Programs are
built from a proven recipe (project + widgets + toolbar), identity is real
per-user filesystems, and **every fact lives in a file**.

- 📁 **File-truth** — state is on disk. Audit, replay, backup, AI-test, and
  recover are all just *file reads*.
- 🔒 **Local-first** — no cloud dependency, no account lock-in, no network
  required. Your data never leaves your machine.
- 🧪 **Harness-proven** — "works" means a key-injection harness went green in
  `proof/`, not a demo video.
- 🛠️ **Deterministic installs** — the installer is a one-way copy + in-place
  compile. Same source → same `~/xyzos/`, every time.
- 👤 **Real identity** — uuid-keyed per-user filesystems (`xyzfs`). Users can
  log out and in and *keep their files*.

## 📈 The distribution story

`@.app-store/` in the house = the **dev catalog** (what's available).
`~/xyzos/app-store/` = this user's **installed ledger** (what they have).
An install op copies a catalog entry into `~/xyzos/apps/<name>/` and registers
it — modeled on the proven per-user-provisioning op
(`ops/userpal_create_account.c`).

## ⚠️ Honest limits (read before committing)

- 🧊 **No cloud / multi-machine sync yet** — by design. Local-first.
- 🕹️ **One real app pair today** (login + avatar). The game/editor apps are
  mature programs but not yet packaged into `~/xyzos/apps/`.
- 🌳 **Versioning is designed, not shipped** — the gitlet strategy doc is the
  plan; wiring it is an open task.
