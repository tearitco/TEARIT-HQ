## 1. The rules of the world (the parts every book leans on)
### 1.1 Clones (READ + SAID)
- **What:** manufactured people. They are **labour, soldiers and guards** (identical armed "clone guards" patrol the Architect's Nexus in book 1; clone soldiers fill Lunaria in book 0; clone slums and pits on Mars; clone slaves on Venus).
- **Control and cost:** a new clone is wiped and fitted with **control chips** ("obedience routing"); in the Red White & You zine "the hum you hear is the conditioning" (READ). In book 0 **clone souls are the SolPen's power source** (chapter 8 and 11: "clone souls as power", the "cloning process" and "ethical questions"); in the zine the infection **the Red Mass** changes the host.
- **Not all clones obey:** **clone rebels** hold Mars (book 0 ch13: "clone rebels", the Black Queen and Princess Megan8r); the **clone-merpeople** are a peaceful mutant-clone faction the UN wants to eliminate (book 3); Gem's persuasion plants "a seed of doubt, a hint of something beyond their programmed existence" in clone guards (book 1).
- **Gem is a clone** (SAID; READ book 0 ch9: "subtle reveal of Gem's true nature as a **disguised clone and spy for SOL**"; later she turns and is loyal to the Martian throne, ch16). In the Metal Sun book **Riley is Gem's clone son** and Sol is his father ("He's your son, Sol. And mine"): Sol's "obsession with his clone sons" is a theme (READ).
- **The question the franchise asks:** "is a clone just property, or do they have a soul worth fighting for?" (stated in the Venus beat sheet; DOC: it is the theme of every clone story, and of TSOTS's possess-and-capture loop).
### 1.2 Time clones / time twins (SAID + READ)
- **SAID:** Lucky and Sol are **time clones**.
- **READ (Centroid session plan 3-10+, `4.d&party-centroid`):** "**When Sol goes back in time, he creates a time clone of himself. His future self still exists in the future. The past and future versions are separate entities. Both are real. Both matter.**" The **Stable Loop**: the past versions must reach the Centroid and enter the trap, because that is what creates the loop. Sol wears a **temporal body suit**; bits of him flicker because of his "**time twin connection to Lucky**". **Past-Lucky, past-Astra and past-CHARA** are abducted and trained on SSOL; their **future selves are still trapped** (Session 9). The **Melting Red Wax Time Police** hunt unauthorized time travelers.
- **READ (book 0 outline):** "**Sol (Future Lucky)**"; chapter 19: Lucky appears cloned: "it's actually SOL, who is Lucky from the future".
- **READ (book 11, Footrace Fu):** a **Time Twin** is a learned technique: a translucent temporal echo that fights and navigates beside you, later nearly as solid as the original; the Red Wax Time Police detain Lucky for time-twinning too fast and warn about recursion; Chinese skill name 时间分身 (shí jiān fēn shēn, "time clone"). In the FFU D&D notes, Sol-Supreme's Engineering Time Fu uses "time clone" to build "multiple Circuit duplicates".
- **DOC, canon mapping (now confirmed by the owner, section 5 item 9):** (a) a **clone** is made by manufacture; (b) a **time clone** is made by time travel and keeps the same identity line; both can have a soul; (c) Lucky, Sol (and, per the owner's theology, Asa) are one identity line, **not** David/Solomon/Jesus.
### 1.3 Carcazods (also spelled Carcazoids, "Carc-Zilla" for the big one) (READ)
- A **bio-engineered plague/monster**: a "virulent, bio-engineered scourge" that swept the world; infected beings become Carcazods; the plague scarred humanity ("the memory of the Carcazod plague").
- **Origin (book 1 outline):** **the Architect orchestrated the apocalypse, creating the Carcazoids and clowns, to seize control of the world and sell the cure.** Defeating him yields **the Mutagen**, which can be engineered into a virus that wipes out Carcazoids; it can only be processed in the Mars labs.
- **Later:** Lucky **cures the plague** (book 3 start, sells Cerberus Inc. to the UN); a **Carcazod bio-scan** at an orbital barrier shows infection (book 2); in book 2 a **larger, intelligent, evolving Carcazod** appears and the hidden bio-marker is Carcazod, revealing the Architect's influence; **"Carc-Zilla"** is the UN's secret weapon in book 3. **Carcazod bio-weapons** are used by the clowns (book 0 ch15: Gem is infected, Lucky fixes her heart on the ship).
### 1.4 Clowns (READ)
- **Rebels and thieves with painted faces** who run "Clown World": they **hijack vessels and fill them with Carcazod bio-samples**, ambush survivors (the clown thieves in book 1), and by book 0 ch15 are loose on Earth ("the US is buckling under the force of the clowns"), chased by everyone on Mars to the space docks.
- The Architect creates "his most grotesque mutant clowns and Carcazoids" (book 1 ch13). The **Clean Zone** (e.g. "000010001000") is the safe zone Sol builds on Earth (book 0 ch12, 20). DOC: clowns are the human-scale face of the plague and the plague's weapon-users; Carcazods are the biology.
### 1.5 XVS (SAID + READ)
- **SAID (owner):** XVS = the **expanse maps** from the RMMV games (map064 Ocean Expanse, 065 Space Expanse, 066 Mars Expanse).
- **READ:** folder `101.XVS++[FLEXOPEDAI]00.05` is a **series of seven worlds** (`XVS_0.AETERNA_MEDUSA`, `1.DETRITUS_PRIME`, `2.CRABFOLD`, `3.NEARTH`, `4.DARK`, `5.FUNGI`, `6.LUX`) with an **Artifacts Bible** and a **Planets Atlas**. The atlas file is titled "**XVS: The Hydroverse Expansion Atlas**": the **Hydroverse is a region of XVS**, navigated by a tri-state coordinate [H hydrogen density, T thermal variance, P pressure]; planets include **Puffara-Kai** (puffer-folk, the "Switzerland of the Deep"), **Venom-Spire** (lion-fish aristocracy, secretly aligned with the Angler Empire) and **Carchar-Industrial** (shark-kin forge, "the XVS Strategic Partner"). The Angler Empire book opens with "**The XVS Briefing**" and the books mention "XVS police". The Centroid folder name carries "xvsp".
- **DOC:** XVS is the **expanse of worlds** (the multiverse map) and also the command/police that governs it; the RMMV expanse maps are its playable map type. Confirm with the owner.

## 5. Inconsistencies: RESOLVED by the owner (canon proposals adopted 2026-11-05)
Status: these answers were written as **canon proposals** and adopted by the owner ("update docs with these answers"). They are **not yet in any book**; the books are drafts and **the owner's beat sheets and answers win where they differ**. Earlier sections of this doc describe what the books say; this section says what canon now is.
1. **The Architect is a time clone of Lucky from a failed loop.** **Sol is the Stable Loop**: the future Lucky whose past self reached the Centroid and closed the circle. **The Architect is the Open Loop**: a Lucky-line time clone who never closed it and chose profit over the fall. This explains the "evil twin" (book 3) and why he claims **"Supreme Emperor Sol by Divine Right"** (book 6): he usurps a title that belongs to the version that worked. The **Melting Red Wax Time Police** hunt him as an unauthorized time traveler, which is why they exist.
2. **Red Queen vs Black Queen: one dynasty, two titles.** The **Black Queen (Sophia)** leads the underground clone rebels and **Megan8r** is one of her daughters. **The Red Queen** is the title after the rebels take the throne. Red stands for the blood banks and the vampire guards, so the zine says "don't trust the Red Queen".
3. **Who cures the plague: all three, at different layers.** The Architect owns the **Mutagen** and sells the cure; **Lucky engineers the anti-Carcazod virus in the Mars labs** and takes the public credit; the cure **fails against the evolved strain in book 2**, which is why the plague keeps coming back.
4. **Spelling: "Carcazod" is canon** (supported by "Carc-Zilla"); **"Carcazoid" is the UN's clinical term.**
5. **Three Elaras: one soul, three echoes** (the Martian Viper, the Glitch World anomaly, the Accidental Witch). The Soul Pen reuses names the way the loop reuses people; "souls are a resource" covers it.
6. **XVS police = XVS expanse.** XVS is the expanse of worlds **and** the authority over it, as the Time Police govern time. The Carchar-Industrial line "XVS Strategic Partner" fits.
7. **The Venus DLC (6.7) is a "meanwhile" story during Hellride**, while the Metal Sun pushes humanity underground. Venus is where the **Metal Mafia ships its clone labour**, so it needs no Lucky or Sol.
8. **Gem's clone reveal: keep book 0 chapter 9 as the single reveal.** Later books treat it as known; **"Spy-K" is just her cover name.** She is a clone with her own soul: the franchise question answered in miniature.
9. **Time-clone vocabulary:** **"time clone"** = the Centroid scholars' word; **"Time Twin"** = the dojo (Footrace Fu) word; **"future self"** = plain speech. One identity line, both versions real, the loop must close. A **manufactured clone** is a different thing, though Sol's clone sons mix the two.
10. **Canon or drafts:** the books are drafts of a canon; beat sheets and owner answers win.
