# TSOTS: what it means and the player loop (2026-11-05)

Labels: **SAID** = the owner said it to an agent on 2026-11-05. **READ** = in a source file (named). **DOC** = reasoning, not canon.

## 1. The definition (SAID)
> "When we speak of TSOTS we mean playing through levels of the Bible, doing missions, capturing Bible characters as main character 'cursword' and using them as pals in battles / trading. We will be making levels and collecting characters and entities from both the Bible and the Soul Pen (Soul Pen lore is meant to pick up after the Book of Revelation)." "The story mode ... uses most of those maps + Soul Pen lore."

So TSOTS = **The Sword of the Spirit** as a game with three layers:
1. **Bible levels**: levels built from the Bible, played in order, each with missions.
2. **Capture and pals**: the player's main character is **"cursword"**. Characters (and entities) from the Bible are **captured** and become **pals** used in battle and in **trading**.
3. **Soul Pen levels**: the Soul Pen franchise (see `SOUL-PEN-LORE-SUMMARY-2026-11-05.md`) is the **continuation after Revelation**; its characters and entities are also collectible.
The RPG Maker maps (98 imported, `@.apps/piececraft-hq/pieces/system/maps/tsots/`, `map071` = the classic 4-player board) are the story mode's levels.

## 2. How this fits the owner's older notes (READ: TSOTS SECRETS, `TSOTS-SECRETS-VISION-SUMMARY-2026-11-05.md`)
- The old notes call the game "The Sword of the Spirit": the sword is the tool of the two eternal guardians **Lucky and Sol**; everyone else who wants that power draws on a **lyfebook** with a **soul pen**. In the Soul Pen lore the same word appears as an object: **Sol Pen / SolPen** (a 3D "printer" powered by clone souls) and **Tsots (SoulPen)** (READ: `103=+T&T.local.sum`: Lucky "begins using his Tsots (SoulPen) to write code directly onto the world").
- The old notes' mechanics apply to capture and pals: **loyalty** decides whether a piece can be "captured, bought or turned" (a disloyal clone becomes a clown below ~30%), pieces are captured by loyalty rather than by arrest, pals keep **crime**, **stress**, **hunger**, jobs and skills, and trading is through contracts and the market.
- The old story mode is a mission ladder (20 beats). The new framing replaces its fiction (Pakistan, the Mars mission) with **Bible missions then Soul Pen missions**; the mission-ladder shape and the rule "levels matter from Beat 4" can be kept (DOC).

## 3. The player loop (DOC, derived from the definition; owner to confirm)
1. Enter a **Bible level** (a map) as **cursword**.
2. Do the level's **missions**; meet **Bible characters and entities** (people, angels, creatures, objects).
3. **Capture** them (loyalty / mission outcome; the capture rule is not defined yet) and add them to the **roster** (the 22-piece army idea in the old notes).
4. Use them as **pals**: party battles (manual, auto or hybrid), **trading** between players, skills from schools.
5. After the Bible levels (ending with Revelation), **Soul Pen levels** begin with the Soul Pen cast and creatures.
6. The **Centroid University** is a place in the Soul Pen world (lore: "The Centroid: School of the Multiverse") where pals can be trained (see the vision doc).
