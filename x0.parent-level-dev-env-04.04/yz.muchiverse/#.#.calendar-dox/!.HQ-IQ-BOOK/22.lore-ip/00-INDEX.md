## Rules for this chapter
- **Canon is the owner's.** Mark every claim: SAID (the owner said it to an agent), READ (found in a source file, with the file), DOC (an agent's reasoning). Never present DOC as canon.
- **Say how much was read.** The lore is about 1.1 million words of books plus 2,004 audio files and about 5,000 chapter source files; a summary says exactly which parts it read (each doc has a "Coverage" section).
- **IP care.** This is the owner's unpublished franchise. Do not paste it into outside services or public repos; the XO folder is the owner's own export route.
- **Adult and crude content** exists in the sources (violence, sex, drugs, slurs in drafts). Summaries paraphrase neutrally and do not carry such items into game design unless the owner decides.
- Lore changes need an owner answer: add a line to a doc's "open questions" instead of choosing.
