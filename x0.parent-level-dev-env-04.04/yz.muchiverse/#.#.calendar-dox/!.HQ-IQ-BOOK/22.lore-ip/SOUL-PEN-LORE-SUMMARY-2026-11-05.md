## 2. The core cast (READ unless marked)
- **Lucky** (Lucky Shepherd, Lucky Starr, Dr. Lucky): the recurring lead across volumes, at ages 17 (Footrace), 25 (Death Kryo), 30s, 37 (Sol Pen), 40s. A billionaire / tech genius / mercenary / Centroid student depending on the volume.
- **Astra**: his partner, genius inventor and scientist (27 in Sol Pen), goth style with plush keychains.
- **Sol** ("Sol (Future Lucky)", masked leader of the lunar society, wielder of the SolPen; READ). Master list: "asa = lucky = sol".
- **Gem**: friend, artist, a clone and spy for Sol; later injects herself with Carcazod blood to get past clown bio-scans; "acting strange" aboard the Metal Sun.
- **Loona Twilight** (leader of the moon crew), **Kael** (Halo body, Glitch World, leads the team in Eden Recursion), **Lyra** (a cartographer of the multiverse at the Centroid; a creator of life in Children of Origin), **Horli** and **IQabella** (SSOL crew in the tabletop story), **Luna**, **Spike**, **Queen Sophia** (a mermaid), the **Black Queen** and **Princess Megan8r** (Mars rulers who hate the clowns), **Elara** (a rogue on Mars), **Agent Thompson** (CIA), **Kaito** (Shadow Syndicate), **the Architect** (builder of the Metal Sun and the Icarus), **Commander Jax** (Angler), **Professor Aurora**, **Piper**, **Atlas**, **Zephyr** (Centroid people), **the Nihilator**, **the Melting Red Wax Time Police**, **Zick and Zack**, **Elara Veyn and her Inkling**, **Chara, Giana Nguyen, ANON, Agent Klondike** (Charanon), and **Asa and Ava** (section 4).

## 5. The Centroid (the lore behind Centroid University)
READ (screenplay, first 100 lines) and T&T summary:
- **The Centroid: School of the Multiverse.** Lucky and Astra (ancient, "hundreds of thousands of years old" yet looking like teenagers) get a package of admission: report to **Spiral Alley**. Principle: the **Golden Ratio** is "the fundamental principle governing the universe" and an energy signature; classes teach creation by its proportions.
- Places: **Spiral Alley** (orientation), **Diagonal Alley** (a quarter of eccentric shops: **Mystic's Muse** run by Zephyr, selling "Essence of Creation"; **The Celestial Cartographers** run by Lyra; the café **The Fountain of Eternal Youth**), the **Soul Bank** (an ancient repository of knowledge), a **library** where the **Soul Pen** is found, a **grand hall** with a great tree, an inter-class exhibition with a secret guest judge.
- Tools and subjects: **Soul Crafting** with the **Soul Pen** ("taps the collective unconscious"), **Tear It Cards** (write an intention, tear the card, release its energy), **Flexbox Engineering** and **Angler Theory** (T&T), time and temporal physics.
- People: Professor Aurora, students Piper and Atlas. Geography: the Centroid is **a cluster of asteroids orbiting a "shooting star"**; ships dock at the **Sky Port**; the journey there runs through **Wormhole X** and the **Phoenix Way** (T&T).
- For the game (DOC): this is the lore skeleton of the University: orientation street, a shop quarter (physical shops), a bank of souls, a library, classes with a creation skill tree, exhibitions and a guest-judge event.

## 7. Canon rules that recur (READ / SAID)
1. **Loops and recursion**: Sol is Lucky's future self; time loops must stay stable; revealing yourself can split a person (so Lucky guides Sol anonymously). "Recursion is an atom, not a ring."
2. **Souls are a resource**: clones and deaths power ships and the SolPen; the Nihilator consumes souls.
3. **Clowns, clones and Carcazods** are the antagonist ecology of the Soul Pen volumes; the **Nihilator** is the void's agent.
4. **The Metal Sun / Icarus** is a recurring structure built by the Architect (and, in the owner's theology, tied to Noah's ship and Eden).
5. Lucky's identity changes with the era (age 17 to 40s); treat volumes as different timelines of one soul unless the owner says otherwise.
