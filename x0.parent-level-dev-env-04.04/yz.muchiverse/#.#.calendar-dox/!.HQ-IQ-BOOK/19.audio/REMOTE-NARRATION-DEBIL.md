# Narration (Kokoro TTS) on debil over ssh (set up 2026-10-14)
**Why**: local Kokoro narration took ~1.8-2.5 s of compute per second of audio on this weak machine, loaded the desktop, and the local run died silently twice at about line 20 of NIGHT 50 (cause not found; log was lost the first time). Policy: big jobs go to debil or the Mac.
**Measured**: NIGHT 50 (45 lines, 653 s of audio) rendered on debil in **244 s** (6 threads, nice 10, 68-70 C) versus 481 s for the first 20 lines locally. Whole local run would have been ~20+ min.
**How**: `1-1.HARNECIENT.SMOL/convert_night_to_audio_remote.py start|status|fetch <NIGHT_xx.txt>`: parses the script here (same parser, same voices as `convert_night_to_audio_local.py`), uploads a job file, starts `remote_tts/render_lines.py` on debil in the background, `fetch` copies the wav back and makes `audio-book/<name>_debil.mp3` (96k, never overwritten) with local ffmpeg. Per-line parts on debil make it resumable.
**Debil setup (no sudo, nothing system-wide)**: `~/house-tts/` with `kokoro-v1.0.int8.onnx` (92,361,271 bytes) and `voices-v1.0.bin` (28,214,398 bytes), checksums matched the local copies (sha256 prefixes 6e742170d309016e, bca610b8308e8d99); `lib/` (177 MB) from `pip --target` (kokoro-onnx, soundfile, numpy, onnxruntime 1.31.0, numpy 2.4.6); `render_lines.py`. The venv route failed (no ensurepip); debil has no ffmpeg, hence wav out and mp3 here. Python 3.11.2 there.
**Check (our own listener)**: lines 1, 2 and 4 of the debil-made audio transcribed with whisper tiny.en (`hear`) and scored with `wer`: line 1 (19.5 s, bm_george) 1 error in 61 words (0.016, "said" heard as "set"), line 2 (bf_emma) 0 of 11, line 4 (am_michael) 0 of 7. This shows the audio is intelligible English to our recogniser; it says nothing about how pleasant it sounds. **Not listened to by a person.**
**Gotchas fixed in the orchestrator**: a backgrounded remote job held the ssh session open (now `ssh -n` + `setsid -f`); a tuple bug in `status`.
**Not done**: the same for the Mac, running other TTS jobs (pets' say cache) there, GPU use. The local `convert_night_to_audio_local.py` is unchanged.
