## 3b. Language rule: one voice, the OTHER language as subtitles, never both (SAID 2026-11-18)
The company has English and Japanese customers. A deck is made as a pair: **English voice with Japanese subtitles** (`-en-voice-ja-subs`) and **Japanese voice with English subtitles** (`-ja-voice-en-subs`). Never a voice and subtitles in the SAME language (redundant). The manifest caption is the subtitle text, so a deck needs two manifests (one per subtitle language) and one narration script per voice language; keep the two text sets side by side so they stay the same slides.
PITFALL (2026-11-18, RAN): the caption font is DejaVuSans, which has NO Chinese or Japanese glyphs: a CJK caption renders as empty boxes (seen in the first Teario deck). Japanese subtitles need a CJK font (Noto Sans CJK or WenQuanYi, both searched by the engine glyph code in `bv_cjk_glyph.c`) added as a fallback in the maker before any ja-subs deck.
NOT built or verified: `make_presentation_video.py` has no language option yet, and a Japanese Kokoro voice (needs the Japanese text front end for `kokoro-onnx`) has not been tried here; check it with a recorded level test before promising a Japanese deck. Today the caption strip shows the caption and the narration reads the same caption, which is exactly the redundant case to avoid.

## 4b. A fight proof is two views of the same moment (2026-11-21)

The Draw Battle flame cut (`XO/31.Nov/nov-21/1.draw-battle-flame-en-ja/`) failed this. The ledger and the bar said two flames at 38 hp and a bolt on the cell between them. The frames, opened, showed one sprite and no walk. Do not ship a bar reading as the picture of a fight.

Why the perspective view collapsed: `bv_render_3d.c` stands each `events.txt` row up as a column on its cell (a person is 2.6 cells tall, a `!` sheet is 1.9). Three rows on one line, cells `5,7` `6,7` `7,7`, are three neighboring columns. At the camera distance used that night they read as one figure. The flat view does not do that: `bv_render_2d.c` `draw_map_events` blits each row on its own cell, and an earlier flat dump in this same window showed separate charset sprites.

A `!` sheet is only a sprite in perspective when `frames/<name>_<index>_<dir>_<pattern>.rgba` exists (16 by 24). The bolt row is `!Flame 0 2 1`. That file was missing, so the column stayed the flat red box. It was cropped from `!Flame.png` (index 0, down, step 1) on 2026-11-21: 1536 bytes, 140 opaque pixels. The next perspective frame of that row uses it. No camera edit.

A fight video is the same paused ledger twice. Key `0` toggles the view. Shoot the flat frame, then `0` again and shoot the perspective frame. Do not send `f` (it resets the view). Do not edit the camera equations. If the perspective frame still shows one figure, the bodies are still on a line that points at the camera: spread them so their cells are not neighbors along that line, and shoot again. Open both frames before the voice is recorded. A caption that claims two fighters while the picture has one is the failure this section exists to stop.

What moves the perspective half from about 60% to 80% is one opened frame, not a camera edit. The columns overlap when the cells share a line aimed at the lens (the failed proof was `5,7` `6,7` `7,7`, one row). Put the two bodies on a diagonal, at least three cells apart in both x and y, pause the ledger, shoot, and count silhouettes. Two bodies and the bolt, each on its own cell, is the 80% observation. Until that frame has been opened, the perspective half stays at 60%. Do not send `f` and do not write the camera state to force a closer view.

## 7. ANIMATED game footage (2026-11-18, the Teario deck; `make_presentation_video.py` is STILLS ONLY)
Owner 2026-11-18, about a deck of stills: "VIDEO PRESENTATION SHOULD SHOW FRAMES ANIMATED, SHOW HERO JUMPING, KILLING ENEMY, CURRENTLY IT ALL LOOKS STILL". A game deck shows the game MOVING. RAN, with scratch scripts (they are not in the repo; a C op is the right home if this is repeated):
1. **Record:** play the run through the game's own buttons (`game_core ctl <verb> <games dir>`, inputs at the harness ticks) while a second loop dumps the editor window with `dump_frame_png_op.+x` (about 2.8 frames a second, niced) and appends `<n> <epoch seconds> <game tick>` per frame to a log. At normal speed (100 ms a tick, seen about 7 ticks a second) that is one frame per 3 ticks: choppy; at the SLOW speed button (250 ms a tick) it is about 0.75 frames per tick: smooth enough. 175 frames for the 241-tick Meadow clear.
2. **Cut by game tick, not by clock:** a scene is "the frames with tick a..b"; each frame is held for the real gap to the next frame (divided by a speed factor; the waiting stretch was 4x). A scene is held as long as its narration plus half a second (speed it up to 2.2x at most, then hold the last frame): that is how there is never silence.
3. **Subtitles and voice:** one line per scene, English voice with Japanese subtitles (rule 3b), burned in with ffmpeg `drawtext` and `/usr/share/fonts/opentype/noto/NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc` on a Tear-it purple strip under the picture (gold text, red rule). Kokoro clips (`pet_tts_local.py --voice en-US-AnaNeural`) padded to each scene length; verify with `volumedetect` per scene (every scene read about -23 dB mean).
4. **Traps that cost runs:** the speed button and the human seat are PREFERENCES that PERSIST between games, so a blind `human_next` or `speed_next` cycles them to the wrong value (seat dropped to a pet, speed to fast): press until the feed label says `you play: p1` and `speed: slow`. The game ends on its own at the goal, so inputs at fixed ticks only work with the right speed and a human seat. A `pkill -f` or `ps | grep` with the pattern in your own command line kills your shell.
5. **The result:** XO nov-18 `11.teario-animated-en-voice-ja-subs/` (52 s: seven scenes of the real run, then the Make stamp bank).

## 9. A VOICED deck (English voice + Japanese subtitles), made 2026-11-19 after the owner asked "why is the last presentation silent? make sure it's not silent next"
**Why the section 8 deck was silent:** I ran `make_presentation_video.py ... --no-tts` on my own (the script speaks the caption it burns in, so it cannot do English voice with Japanese subtitles; the CJK font is not in it). A deck for the owner is voiced by default; `--no-tts` only on request. The old silent deck is `16.teario-goals-deck-silent-en-captions`; the voiced one is XO `31.Nov/nov-18/17.teario-goals-deck-en-voice-ja-subs/Teario-goals-and-3D-findings-en-voice-ja-subs.mp4` (11 slides, 116.7 s, 1280x854 h264 + aac).
**The recipe that RAN (no new Python, no repo script; the build commands were a scratch `sh` file, kept out of the repo; a C op is the right home if this repeats):** folder = `snapshots/NN.png`, `narration_en.txt` (one line per slide, written to be SPOKEN: no symbols, numbers as words, each line UNDER 240 characters because `pet_tts_local.py` cuts longer text), `sub/NN.txt` (the Japanese subtitle, line-broken by hand at about 36 full-width characters because `drawtext` does not wrap), `audio/`. (1) TTS: `cpu_gate <house> run deck-tts --wait 120 -- nice -n 15 sh -c 'loop: @.apps/pet-trainer/ops/pet_tts_local.py --voice en-US-AnaNeural --text "<line>" --write-media audio/NN.wav'` (one clip at a time, in the background, 11 clips took about 4 minutes). (2) CHECK THE SOUND before building: `ffmpeg -i audio/NN.wav -af volumedetect -f null -` per clip, mean about -22.5 dB; silence reads far lower or the file is missing. (3) One `ffmpeg` call per slide (gated, niced): `-loop 1 -framerate 30 -t HOLD -i NN.png -i NN.wav -filter_complex "[0:v]scale=1280:664,pad=1280:854:0:0:color=0x3a245c,drawbox=x=0:y=664:w=1280:h=4:color=0xc82030:t=fill,drawtext=fontfile=/usr/share/fonts/opentype/noto/NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc:text='N/11':fontcolor=0xc4aaf0:fontsize=22:x=18:y=676,drawtext=fontfile=<same>:textfile=sub/NN.txt:fontcolor=0xffd75a:fontsize=28:x=18:y=708:line_spacing=8[v];[1:a]apad,aresample=44100[a]" -t HOLD -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 30 -c:a aac`, with `HOLD = clip length + 0.8 s`; colours are the `tearit` theme (bg 58,36,92 = 0x3a245c, text 255,215,90, label 196,170,240, rule 200,32,48). (4) join: `ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i list.txt -c copy out.mp4`. (5) VERIFY (all RAN): `ffprobe` (one video + one audio stream, duration), `volumedetect` on the whole file (mean -22.8 dB, peak -2 dB) with a CONTROL on the silent deck (`Output file #0 does not contain any stream`), and two frames extracted with `ffmpeg -ss` and OPENED (Japanese renders as text, not empty boxes; counter and strip present). A `sh` script run by `cpu_gate` must not use `$((10#01))` (dash): strip the zero with `sed`.
