## 2. What you can rely on today (all verified this session unless marked)
* **`views_to_voxel`** (`44.xyz.01.00/&.widgits/_shared-lib/ops/`, build `sh build_views_to_voxel.sh`, binary `+x/views_to_voxel.+x` is gitignored: rebuild after a clone). `views_to_voxel <out_model.txt> <front> <side> <top> [--solid] [--max-vox N] [--min-consistency F] [--bg R,G,B]`; each view a `.png` (alpha ≥ 128 is filled, or differs from `--bg`/the top-left pixel when opaque) or a `.txt` grid (`.`/space empty, anything else filled, `# legend <char> <r> <g> <b>` for colour).
