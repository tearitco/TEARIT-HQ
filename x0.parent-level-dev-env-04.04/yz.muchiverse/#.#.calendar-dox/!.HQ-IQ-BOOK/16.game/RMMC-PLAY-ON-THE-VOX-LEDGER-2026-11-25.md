## 2. Verbs (event pages; GAME_ARG carries the argument)

`spawn x z` (on top of the column; 11 over sky or off the map, 18 if already spawned), `walk n|e|s|w`, `face n|e|s|w`, `jump`, `reset` (to the spawn point), `seat human|harness`. `mine place pick` are in the manifest (`page=no`) and the engine answers exit 2 "not wired yet": NOT VERIFIED, owner priority 3.
Walk rules (numbers in `vox/rules.pdl`): step up 1 (2 when climbing out of water, `step_up_wade`), drop at most 3 (`fall_max`), water at most 1 deep (`water_max_depth`), `jump_height` 1, `move_max` 4 (a walk to another level is one MOVE row of length 1 + |dy|). A refusal writes nothing: 11 off the island or over sky, 18 blocked, 10 unknown actor, 14 hand-edited ledger, 15 no ground for a jump, 24 not your seat. The result is on `H/@.apps/rmmc/play/result.txt` as `ok` or `refused: <reason> [exit N]`.

## 3. The pose rule (`pose.pdl`, data)

`MOVE_ODD` 1 and `MOVE_EVEN` 2 alternate per ground step of the actor; `MOVE_LEVEL` 3 (a jump's rows, a step up, a drop); `DAMAGE`/`MINE` 4; `PLACE` 5; `FACE`, `RESET`, `IDLE` 0. Direction: the last move delta or the last FACE (n 8, e 6, s 2, w 4). `--idle` writes 0: the expiry belongs to the DRIVER (`idle_after_ms` 400 in `keys.pdl`; it re-projects with `--idle --no-marker`), never to a ledger row. `cells.marker` grows by one byte on every projection (`old mod 4000 + 1`); `--no-marker` skips it (an events-only refresh, offered in case a full desk reload per step hitches; the engine re-reads events every frame).

## 4. The two controllers and the seat

The seat is a ledger row (`SEAT | asa | human|harness`; no row = harness). The beat op and the verb engine (env `RMMC_CALLER`) refuse with exit 24 and write nothing when the seat is not theirs. Human keys: left 1000, right 1001, up 1002, down 1003, jump 32 (GAMEKEY rows of `rmmc-pchq/pieces/system/keybinds.pdl`, verbs in `keys.pdl`). The driver reads `<host>/state/interact_relay.txt` lines `<key> <ms>` by a byte cursor that starts at the END (history is not replayed), writes its UI feed only when the bytes change, and exits when `--owner-pid` is gone (also when the owner is a zombie).

## 5. Commands for the lead (house `H`; the editor and the engine are NOT touched by any of them)

```
H=.../44.xyz.01.00        # built binaries are in @.apps/rmmc/ops/+x and @.apps/vox/ops/+x (gitignored; rebuild with the gcc line in each header)
export RMMC_LEDGER="$H/@.apps/rpg-edit-hq/state/games/rmmc/game/ledger.txt"   # the default; rmmc_verb makes the folder
"$H/@.apps/rmmc/ops/+x/rmmc_beat_op.+x" "$H" "$H/@.apps/rmmc/beats.pdl" check       # no writes: beats=10 ok
nice -n 15 "$H/@.apps/rmmc/ops/+x/rmmc_beat_op.+x" "$H" "$H/@.apps/rmmc/beats.pdl" next    # ONE beat (the spawn); repeat for beat 2, 3 ...; `all` plays the ten
# the human path (In: ON, cursor on Asa, Enter; then keys). Take the seat and start the driver (register it with kh_spawn/proc_reg_op when started for real):
GAME_ARG=human RMMC_CALLER= "$H/@.apps/rmmc/ops/+x/rmmc_verb.+x" seat
"$H/@.apps/rmmc/ops/+x/rmmc_driver.+x" "$H" "<host>/state/interact_relay.txt" --feed "<ui feed path>" --owner-pid <pid of the window process>
```
Each verb rewrites `rmmc-0001/{cells.txt,map.txt,events.txt,cells.marker}`; the engine shows Asa only after a reload (marker size changes) and with `event_lift=1` she stands on her column. `play/beat.txt` is a bookmark (PREFERENCE) for `next`. The Blueprintz pause is never cleared: with `paused=1` the verbs run directly and the message says so.
