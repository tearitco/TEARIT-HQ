## 2. Using tear-on (normal use)
1. **Open it:** network menu > `tear-on (rpg-edit board)`. The op `H/&.hq-apps/tear-on/ops/+x/tear_on_op.+x --open` projects both pages, saves tear-on as the editor's last book, then switches a RUNNING editor through its inbox (`CONFIRM_START_MAP:tear-on`, `CONFIRM_SET_DESK:ranch`) or starts the editor (`button.sh run`, which opens the last book). RAN on a running editor (3D ranch and arena seen); the start-from-closed branch is NOT verified live.
2. **Empty chain?** `tear_on_op demo <H>` seeds five pets and an arena as ROWS (refuses a chain that already has rows). Reset = delete `H/@.apps/tear-on/ledger/web_chain.txt`.
3. **Play** (Player > Play in the editor; RAN live 2026-11-24, it replaced the owner's Teario-Play popup, backups in `/home/no/tear-live-backups/2026-11-24-before-play/`): starts `@.apps/tear-on/ops/+x/tear_on_driver.+x`, which draws a game bar with FIVE TABS so every button fits the popup: **[Care]** Hatch a pet, Next pet, Pet faucet, Feed bread / meat, Rest, Refresh board; **[Train]** Train sprint / lift / study, Next ranked fight; **[Walk]** up / down / left / right; **[Arena]** Open 1v1 / 2v2 / free for all, Join, Fill with defaults, FIGHT, Leave, Next arena; **[Wallet]** cap, reserve, sale lock, Next pet; and Close the game on every tab. Every click is ONE ROW mined into the chain; pages are re-projected. The editor's `running` switch is the only pause (commands wait while it is 0). Verified by `tear_on` case `driver-plays-the-book-like-the-editor-does` with a scratch editor AND live: the Arena tab and FIGHT! (winners and scores on the bar, chain verifies), walking (the rules refuse an occupied cell by name). **Live motion, no page switch (fixed 2026-11-24 night, SEEN live):** the engine reloads a page when the SIZE of its `cells.marker` changes (owner standard 2026-10-25) and draws only when a frame is requested. The projection (`tear_chain_op desk`) wrote a constant one-byte marker, which is why five walks gave a zero image difference; it now grows the marker by one byte after `cells.rgba` (capped at 4000, then wraps), and the driver nudges the engine (`bv_keys <session> ~~`) after every row. Arena actions still switch the board to the arena page, hatch and walking to the ranch page, only when it is on the other one. **[Care] > Redraw board** is just the nudge. Harness: `tear_on` checks the stamp grows even when the chain did not change.
4. **Wallet options per pet** (rows `WOPT|pet|option|value|owner|ts`): `cap` (most one spend may cost, 0 = none), `reserve` (coins the pet must keep), `sale` (0 = locked from sale, clears a listing). Enforced at every spend: feed, train, upgrade, items, cup entry, arena entry, buying a pet. The game bar cycles cap through 0/10/25/50/100 and reserve through 0/50/100/250/500.
5. **Who you are:** `@.apps/tear-on/play/owner.txt` (one word, default `you`).

## 3. The arena (rows ARENA JOIN LEAVE FILL FIGHT)
1v1 (2 fighters), 2v2 (4, two teams), free for all (3 to 8). Entry 20 from the pet into a pot, 10 percent rake to the ranch pool, free for all pays the top three 50 / 30 / 20. FILL lets the arena owner put default fighters in empty chairs (strength = a hash, never a roll at fight time). Score = power - tired/2 + a roll from the pet seed and the arena name, so replaying the chain replays the fight. A fight tires the pet and ages it one week.
