## The shared founder-lore (spans most outlets)

- Overarching flavor: **SF / NY / (Japan) founders & devs**, targeting
  single asian females, cyberpunks, and "redheads" (ties to a
  "red-head vampire clone" lore thread).
- The founder appears across outlets as a recurring character — a
  "Tenchi Muyo"-style ensemble of familiar, friendly female-assistant
  characters around him. This is **branding and marketing**, not a
  product claim.
- Persona ladder for the founder himself:
  - `JB.EZ` — grounded "mr. robot" flavor (hacker alter-ego, fights
    crime, humble-brag, real networking).
  - `LUCKY_SOL_PEN` — the same person taken to the lore extreme:
    celebrity/rapper energy, space travel, time travel, Mars clone
    army, "man of mystery". Most game lore derives from here
    (`LUCKY-SOL-PEN/`).

## The strategic question the founder wants answered

**When to lean INTO the branding/lore, and when to distance from it.**
Examples the founder raised:
- On a sales call — lore on or off?
- Depends on the demographic / which outlet the lead came through?
- Warm/insider vs. cold audiences?

→ This feeds `../STRATEGY/sales.md`, `../STRATEGY/marketing.md`, and
`../STRATEGY/ethics.md`. Draft a simple decision table:
`context × audience → lore level (none / light / full)`.

## Cross-promotion

Outlets are public-facing as **independent entities**. Only a few
cross-promote, and only when relevant. Keep a short "who may reference
whom" list here once decided.

## Ethics flags to think through (not judgments — just the open questions)

- Demographic targeting language ("single asian females", occult
  fashion, etc.) — where's the line between audience focus and
  something the founder won't put his name to? → `../STRATEGY/ethics.md`
- "Slop posting" / high-volume low-effort gif posting (`ROBOT-TRAP-HOUSE`,
  `TEMPT`) as a growth tactic — acceptable on which accounts, never on
  which (e.g. `JBM` academic, `JB.BLOCKROACH.EZ` services)?
- Secret / impersonal accounts vs. accounts tied to the founder's real
  name and services — keep the separation explicit.
