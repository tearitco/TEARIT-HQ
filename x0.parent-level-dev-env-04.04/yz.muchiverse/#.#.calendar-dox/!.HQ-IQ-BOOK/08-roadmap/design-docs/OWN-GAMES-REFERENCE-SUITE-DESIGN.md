## 3. How each maps onto the house blocks (all as events; harness first)
- **Ring-board core** (being built in alpha as the neutral engine: dice, loop board, cash, ownership, rent, bankruptcy, save/restore, in-progress `ring_board_core`): **Time-Fu: Buffet** is a data change: `board.pdl` = the 24 named buffets with chute/ladder rows, `rules.pdl` = Time Coins/Food/HP, rent = "1 Food or lose 1 HP" (a **choice** page), buffet prices = a proposal I must ask the owner for, since none are given. The race mode adds ninjas (HP/class) and combat pages.
- **Footrace Fu Race/Brawl** needs the combat primitives (RPG Maker parameters `mhp/atk/def/agi/luk`, `change_hp`, states, skills), **action tokens** (a variable reset each turn), a **dice/coin op** (shared with the ring board: `dice_roll`, a coin flip), and a **teleport** page (the existing `transfer_player`-style move on a track). Classes (Chrono-Weaver, Blade Dancer, Ward Sentinel, Ranged Mystic, Shadow Step) are RPG Maker **classes** with HP/move/ability rows; their once-per-game or cooldown abilities are variables and switches. The draft's own fairness guardrails (max total HP 20, max 2 action tokens, teleports limited to 2 per ninja) become tunables in `rules.pdl`.
- **Booty Land** is a grid game with **coin-flip combat tables** (data: `COMBAT | attacker | defender | heads_needed`), a **capital** entity per player (tax/produce/invest as menu rows, i.e. the DSR bank/store pattern), unit entities with move allowances, and a **treasury**; fits `GRID` pages + economy ledger. It is the natural second game after the ring board because its rules are mostly **data tables**.
- **B.O.T.S.** is a chess-like **8x8** with an economy layer (Blood Tax, Market). The grid and turn-with-3-actions machinery are the same as Booty Land; the open items are the piece rules. It also fits DSR's banks/stocks (the **Market** can read the same price formula as DSR/WSR).
- **TSC** is built (TSC_ELO) and is the **sessions/seats/ratings** pattern's first consumer (`GAME-SESSIONS-SEATS-RATINGS-LOBBIES-PATTERN.md`). The remaining proof there: a duel to a winner and the Elo write-back.
- **TSOTS** version: a **collection** layer on top of TSC (characters as entities with weights/skills/word banks, i.e. the entity designs), cast through the same duel loop; needs the owner's rules.
All five reuse: the clock (turn timer or End Turn), save/rewind (snapshots), the seat/lobby/rating pattern, the dice/coin ops, and the entity menu/event machinery. **KISS order:** (1) ring-board core (in progress) -> (2) **Time-Fu: Buffet v0** as board/rules data on it -> (3) **Footrace Fu race mode** (ninjas, action tokens, combat roll-off) -> (4) finish **TSC** proofs -> (5) **Booty Land** (tables) -> (6) **B.O.T.S.** -> (7) **TSOTS** after the owner supplies rules.

## 5. Copyright and naming caution (practical, not legal advice)
- These games are the owner's own inventions, which is the right basis. Keep **third-party brands out of shipped text and file names**: do not use **"Monopoly"** (a registered mark); the FFU file also says **"Chutes and Ladders"**, which is a Hasbro mark: use the traditional generic **"snakes and ladders"** or an invented name ("slides and climbs") in anything shipped. "Time-Fu: Buffet" as a variant name is already the owner's.
- **Art:** the FFU guide pages are AI-generated images; check the terms of the tool that produced them before commercial use. **Third-party assets in the pallets** (Mineclonia/Pixel Perfection, CDDA UltiCa) carry CC licenses with **attribution requirements** (the CREDITS item in the backlog).
- **Bible text** in TSC/TSOTS: use a **public-domain translation** (e.g. KJV) and record which; **other texts** (Tao Te Ching, I Ching translations) vary, so record the translation and its license.
- **Names to clear before selling:** Time-Fu / Footrace Fu, Booty Land, Armor of War, B.O.T.S. / Battle of the Sovereigns, True Swords Clash, TSOTS: I cannot search trademark registries; the owner should run a trademark search before publishing.
