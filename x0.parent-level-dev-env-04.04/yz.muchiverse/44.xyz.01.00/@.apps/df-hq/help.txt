df-hq help: digging
The castle has a block of rock. Pets dig the
cells you mark.

Help tab: Open help shows this panel.
This panel is the full help: Help > Next page.
---
1. Mark a cell
Turn INT on. Face the rock and use Designations
> Mine (or give x y to the dig verb).
A marked cell shows an X; when a pet has
claimed it the X turns into a diamond.
---
2. Mark an area
Designations > Mine area: 1st use = a corner,
second use marks every rock cell between the
two corners (up to 64).
Designations > cancel the mark in front removes
a mark.
---
3. What happens
A free pet in the room walks next to a mark and
digs it. The rock cell becomes floor and the
digger gets one stone (chat scroll:
pip: gets stone). The note line under the coins
shows who dug what.
---
4. Save and test
Player > Save / Load keep the dug cells and the
marks. Player > New game starts a fresh one
(your game goes to slot 0; Undo new game).
Player > Load test fortress loads a ready-made
castle with 9 marked cells.
