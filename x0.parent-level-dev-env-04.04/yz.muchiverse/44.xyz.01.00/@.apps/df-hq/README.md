## FORTRESS-TODO (2026-10-16, sprint step D1: dig)

Built (claude): rock (`STONE | room | x | y | w | h` rows in `rooms.pdl`, solid until dug), the **Dig** verb (Menu row "Dig: mark the tile in front", or `df_hq.+x dig <x> <y>`), a mark list (`state/jobs.txt`: `DESIGNATE|ts|room|x|y`, `DONE|ts|room|x|y`), and a free pet in the same room that takes the nearest mark, walks to a free tile beside it and digs (`DIG|ts|actor|room|x|y|stone` appended to `state/events.txt`). Both files are append-only and read by byte cursor. A cell that has no free tile beside it (inside a block) stays marked until a neighbour is dug. Pets work only in the room they stand in.
Overlay and marks (claude, same day): the window shows the last dig message (`state/note.txt`, `ui.txt` `note=`, a green line under the coins). A marked cell is an X; once a dwarf takes it (`CLAIM|ts|actor|room|x|y` in `jobs.txt`) it is drawn as a diamond and no other dwarf in that room takes it (a dwarf that left the room gives it up). Menu row "Dig: cancel the mark in front" (`undig`, `CANCEL|ts|room|x|y`). Menu row "Dig area" (`dig_area`): the first use stores a corner (`CORNER|ts|room|x|y`, drawn as a marker tile), the second marks every rock cell of the rectangle between the corners and clears the corner (job list holds 64). A mark with no free tile beside it no longer blocks the reachable ones. Not built: priority numbers, other designations (stairs, channel), drag-select with the mouse.
Save/load/reset (claude): slots now include `jobs.txt` and `events.txt` (streamed; events.txt is far over the 64 KB read buffer the other files use); Reset house appends `RESET|ts` to both ledgers (rock back, marks cleared, history kept); a load appends a line to `state/world_epoch.txt` so the running daemon re-reads both ledgers from the start. Left empty on purpose, for grok (rules): how long a dig takes, which tool, stone and ore kinds, what drops and where it is hauled (`haul`/`take`/stockpile), labor preferences and skills (who may take the job), mood, and digging down (z: stairs stay a `DOOR` row until a slice writes `current_z`).
Proof: `XO/df-presentation/df-hq-dig-20261016/` (real window, arrow keys and menu taps, state-file checks, old-code control). The running `df_hq.+x` daemon must be restarted to pick up the job logic; the verb and the menu row work as soon as the binary is replaced.
