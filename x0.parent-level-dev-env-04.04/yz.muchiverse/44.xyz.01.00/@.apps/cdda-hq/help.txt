cdda-hq help: crafting, the cellar, one zed
You pick who acts with Menu > Control (Tab).
Every pet and the player has its own bag.

Craft > Help is a one-line tip.
This panel is the full help: Help > Next page.

Everything that happens is a line in the chat
scroll (Menu > Chat show / hide).
---
1. Items and crafting
Craft > Scavenge a rag gives one rag. It is a
stand-in until a real item source exists.
Craft > Craft bandage: 1 rag gives 1 bandage
and costs 1 minute of game time.
Craft > Bag and Recipes list what you have and
what you can make (recipes.pdl).
---
2. The cellar
The Castle has a second door in its left wall:
the stair. It leads to the Cellar page, and the
Cellar door leads back. There is no height
level yet; it is an ordinary door.
---
3. The zed
One zed lives in the Cellar. It acts only in
INT (turn) mode, after each of your turns:
next to you it hits you, otherwise it takes one
step toward you. It never leaves the Cellar.
Craft > Strike the zed beside me hits it when
you stand next to it.
---
4. Wounds
Six parts: head, torso, arm_l, arm_r, leg_l,
leg_r. Each starts at max HP / 6 (Rex 34 gives
5, the zed 18 gives 3). One hit takes 1 from
one part. Craft > Wounds lists who is hurt.
A leg at 0 makes walking "slow" but you still
move one cell. Nobody dies yet.
