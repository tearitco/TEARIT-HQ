mcl-hq help: blocks and crafting
Blocks stand on the Overworld map and stop
walking. You can mine them, carry them, place
them and craft with them.

Every pet and the player has its own bag.
The actor is whoever Menu > Control (Tab)
has selected.

Menu: Blocks > Help is a one-line tip.
This panel is the full help: Help > Next page.
---
1. Face a block
Turn INT on (the INT tab). An arrow key into a
block turns you to face it; you cannot walk
into it.

2. Mine it
Blocks > Mine block ahead takes the block in
front of you into your bag.
The chat scroll says: rex: mines stone
If nothing is in front: "no block at x,y".
---
3. Place a block
Blocks > Hold next block picks which block of
your bag Place uses (it cycles).
Blocks > Place block ahead puts it on the free
cell in front. It costs one from the bag.
It refuses on a block, water, scenery, a door
cell, or a cell where someone stands.
---
4. Craft
Blocks > Craft planks: 1 log gives 4 planks.
Without a log: "cannot craft planks".
Recipes are rows in recipes.pdl in the game
folder (RECIPE | id | grid | output | count).

Blocks > Bag lists what the actor carries.
---
5. The log
Every mine, place and craft (and every refusal)
is one line in the chat scroll.
Menu > Chat show / hide opens or closes it.
The scroll shows the last ten lines.

Save, Load and New game cover the blocks and
the bags (they are rebuilt from events.txt).
New game puts the starting blocks back.
