
Grade 8 math focuses on building a strong foundation in linear equations, functions, and the Pythagorean theorem. Students learn to solve for unknowns in simple and complex equations, identify key features of functions, and apply the Pythagorean theorem to find lengths of sides in right triangles. Additionally, they learn about slope, including how to calculate it and understand its significance in real-world situations. With these concepts, students can better understand and solve problems in math and other subjects.
