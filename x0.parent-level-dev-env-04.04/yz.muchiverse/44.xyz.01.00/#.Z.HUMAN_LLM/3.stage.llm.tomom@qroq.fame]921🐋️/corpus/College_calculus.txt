
College calculus is a foundational subject that encompasses a wide range of concepts and techniques. Derivatives, the study of how rates of change, form a crucial component of calculus, with applications in optimization, physics, and other fields. Integrals, which represent the accumulation of quantities over time or space, are also central to the subject. Limits, the concept of approaching a value without actually reaching it, provide the mathematical framework for understanding these concepts. Differential equations, which describe how quantities change over time or space, further expand upon this foundation. Multivariable functions, which involve the study of functions of multiple variables, offer a more nuanced understanding of calculus. Additionally, series and applications, such as Taylor series and integration by parts, serve to deepen the student's comprehension of the subject matter.
