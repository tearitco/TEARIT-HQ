
High school biology encompasses a wide range of topics, including the structure and function of cells, photosynthesis, DNA replication, evolution, genetics, and biochemistry. Students learn about the various organelles present in cells, such as mitochondria, ribosomes, and lysosomes, and how they contribute to cellular processes. They also explore the mechanisms of photosynthesis and DNA replication, and how these processes are crucial for life on Earth. Additionally, students delve into the study of evolution and genetics, understanding how species evolve over time and the role of genes in inheritance. Finally, they examine the biochemical processes that occur within cells, including metabolic pathways and protein synthesis. Through this comprehensive study, high school biology lays the foundation for further scientific inquiry and exploration in the field of biology.
