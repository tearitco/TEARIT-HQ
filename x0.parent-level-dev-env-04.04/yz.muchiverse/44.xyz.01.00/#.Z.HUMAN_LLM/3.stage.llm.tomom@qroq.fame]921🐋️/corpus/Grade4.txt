
In fourth grade, students broaden their horizons in various subjects. In math, they delve deeper into multi-digit multiplication and division, refining their skills to solve complex problems. They also explore factors and multiples, understanding the relationship between numbers and how they can be used to simplify calculations. Additionally, they learn about fractions and decimals, mastering the conversion of one form to another. Geography comes alive as students learn about the different regions of the United States, including their unique features and attractions. Historical figures like explorers are also introduced, providing a glimpse into the past and the achievements of these brave individuals. To enhance their reading comprehension skills, students learn various strategies to better understand texts, such as identifying main ideas and making inferences.
