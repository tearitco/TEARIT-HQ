
Second graders are learning some exciting new skills! They're practicing their addition and subtraction up to 20, and getting good at telling time to 5 minutes. They're also learning about different shapes, like squares and circles, and reading tricky sight words. They're discovering the elements of stories, like characters and plots, and learning to identify them in books. Plus, they're learning about the weather and seasons, and even planting their own seeds! It's a lot to take in, but they're handling it like pros.
