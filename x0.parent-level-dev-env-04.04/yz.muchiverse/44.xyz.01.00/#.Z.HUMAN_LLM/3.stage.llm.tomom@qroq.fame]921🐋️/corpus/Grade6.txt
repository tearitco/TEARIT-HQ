
In sixth grade, students delve into various exciting topics that enhance their mathematical, scientific, historical, and literary understanding. Ratios and proportions are a significant part of the math curriculum, where they learn to compare and relate quantities using fractions, decimals, and percentages. Negative numbers are introduced to help students comprehend the concept of absolute value and its applications. Basic algebra expressions, such as simplifying equations and solving problems, become more complex and challenging.

In Earth systems, students explore the different types of rocks and their properties, including their formation, composition, and classification. Ancient civilizations like Egypt and Rome are studied to gain insights into their cultures, beliefs, and contributions to modern society. Poetry and literary devices are also part of the literary curriculum, where students learn to analyze and interpret various forms of poetry and prose. Throughout these topics, academic language skills are developed and refined to enhance overall comprehension and communication.
