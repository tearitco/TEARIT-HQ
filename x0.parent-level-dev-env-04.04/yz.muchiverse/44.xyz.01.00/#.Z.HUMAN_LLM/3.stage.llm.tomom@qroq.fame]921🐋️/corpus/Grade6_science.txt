
At Grade 6 level, students learn about the fascinating world of cells and how they work. They discover the different parts of a cell and how they function together to keep living things alive. In Earth Systems, they explore the interconnectedness of our planet's systems and how they impact each other. They also learn about the difference between physical and chemical changes, and how motion can be described using simple laws. Through engaging experiments and activities, students deepen their understanding of these fundamental concepts in science.
