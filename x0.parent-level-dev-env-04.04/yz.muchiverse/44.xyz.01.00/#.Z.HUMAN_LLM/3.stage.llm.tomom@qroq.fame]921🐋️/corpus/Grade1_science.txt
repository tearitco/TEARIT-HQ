
Grade 1 level science is all about exploring the world around us! We learn about the different types of weather, like sunny and rainy days. We discover the four seasons and how they affect the environment. We also learn about plants and animals, their needs and how they interact with each other. Our senses help us experience the world in new ways, like seeing, hearing, and touching. Finally, we learn about solids and liquids and how they behave differently. Through hands-on activities and experiments, we gain a deeper understanding of these fascinating topics!
