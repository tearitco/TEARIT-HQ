
Grade 4 math involves complex calculations with multi-digit numbers, understanding factors and divisors, working with fractions and decimals, and developing problem-solving skills. Students learn to multiply and divide numbers with ease, exploring the relationship between multiplication and division. They also delve into the world of fractions, learning to convert improper fractions and mixed numbers, and understanding the concept of equivalent fractions. Additionally, they become proficient in working with decimals, including rounding and comparing decimal values. With these skills, students are well-prepared for more advanced math concepts in upper grades.
