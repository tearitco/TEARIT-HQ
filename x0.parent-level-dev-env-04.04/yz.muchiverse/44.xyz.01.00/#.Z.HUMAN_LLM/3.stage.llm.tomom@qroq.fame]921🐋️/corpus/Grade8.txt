
In eighth grade, students delve into a variety of engaging and challenging topics. In mathematics, they learn to solve linear equations and functions, applying their knowledge to real-world problems. The Pythagorean theorem is also a crucial concept as students explore right triangles and trigonometry. Additionally, they dive deeper into chemistry, covering bonding and reactions, and explore the intricacies of the US Constitution and Bill of Rights. In English, they analyze transcendentalist literature, examining themes of individualism and nonconformity. Furthermore, they learn about the pressing issue of climate change and its impact on the environment and society as a whole. Throughout these topics, students develop their advanced academic language skills, honing their ability to comprehend complex ideas and communicate their thoughts effectively.
