
During breaks, students engage in peer learning, a valuable opportunity for collaboration and mutual support. They share knowledge and help each other with homework, working together to solve problems and complete assignments. Friendly discussions and group study sessions foster a sense of community and social learning, where everyone can contribute and learn from one another. Peer learning during breaks promotes a positive and inclusive classroom environment, where students feel comfortable asking questions and seeking help when needed. Through these interactions, they develop important skills such as communication, teamwork, and problem-solving, setting them up for success in their academic and personal pursuits.
