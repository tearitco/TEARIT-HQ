
In first grade, students learn a variety of fundamental topics to develop their skills and knowledge. They start by counting to 20 and practicing addition to 10. They also learn about basic colors, such as red, blue, yellow, and green. Reading simple words becomes easier with the help of phonics and sight-word recognition. Students also learn about days of the week and months of the year. They get introduced to their family members and learn about different animals, both real and fictional. Finally, they start to familiarize themselves with the school setting and the people around them. All these topics are taught in a simple and easy-to-understand language, making learning fun and enjoyable for young students.
