
Grade 8 science covers exciting topics like atoms and elements, which are the building blocks of matter. Students learn about the structure of atoms and how they combine to form elements. They also explore Newton's laws of motion, which explain how objects move and interact with each other. In chemistry, students learn about bonding and the different types of chemical reactions. Additionally, they examine the impact of climate change on our planet and how it affects us. By the end of Grade 8, students have a solid foundation in these core scientific concepts.
