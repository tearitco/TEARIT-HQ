
At Grade 4 level, students learn about different types of ecosystems, including forests, grasslands, and deserts. They discover how energy is transformed from one form to another, such as photosynthesis and cellular respiration. They also learn about the Earth's rotation and how it affects the movement of objects on our planet. Additionally, they explore the water cycle and understand how water moves through different forms, such as liquid, ice, and vapor. Through hands-on activities and experiments, students develop a deeper understanding of these complex concepts in an elementary academic language.
