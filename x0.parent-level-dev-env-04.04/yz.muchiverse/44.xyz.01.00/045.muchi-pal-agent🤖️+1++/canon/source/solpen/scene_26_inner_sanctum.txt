INT. MARTIAN COLONY - BLACK QUEEN'S INNER SANCTUM - DAY

SOUND of a low, rhythmic hum, the clinking of crystal flutes, a tense silence

Lucky, Astra, and Gem stand before the BLACK QUEEN. The Queen, tall and imposing in her dark exo-suit, listens with focused intensity. LUCKY, his platinum blonde high-top fade sharp under the purple lights, gestures to the data on Astra's pad.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Your aborted project wasn't destroyed. The clowns stole the samples and the blueprints. They're breeding Carcazods at the hospital—the "First Nest."

The Queen’s eyes flare with anger.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> I should have known my own research clones would turn. They have no respect for the balance of power.

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (leaning forward, asymmetrical hair swaying)
> They plan to launch them to Earth. They want to finish what you started.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> (to the room)
> Then we stop them. We retake the hospital. We purge the infection once and for all.

Suddenly, a hidden panel in the ceiling SHATTERS. A CLOWN SPY, his face a manic grin of red and white, drops down, an energy blade aimed directly at the Queen's throat.

<center>CLOWN SPY</center>
> Death to the architect!

Gem reacts with superhuman speed, intercepting the spy with a mid-air kick. The spy's blade misses the Queen by centimeters, carving a glowing line into the dais.

Lucky fires a SolPen blast, but the spy rolls, using a localized cloaking device to flicker out of sight.

<center>CLOWN SPY (O.S.)</center>
> You're too late! The launch sequence has already begun!

The spy bursts through a secondary exit, his laughter echoing down the hallway as he escapes to warn the main rebel forces.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> (drawing her own weapon)
> No more talk. We fight!

FADE OUT.
