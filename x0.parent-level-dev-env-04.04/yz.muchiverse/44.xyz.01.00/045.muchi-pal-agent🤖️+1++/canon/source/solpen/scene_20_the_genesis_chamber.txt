EXT. MARTIAN COLONY - DAY

SOUND of whistling Martian wind, the hum of subterranean machinery, the rhythmic thumping of large mechanical hearts

The red dust of Mars settles over a cluster of pressurized domes and industrial spires. This is the main colony, a hub of activity where clone soldiers and imported humans move with purpose.

LUCKY stands on a docking platform, his platinum blonde high-top fade vibrant against the rusty sky. He's wearing his Light Exo Suit, gold chains gleaming. He looks at his hands, his caramel skin flushing.

ASTRA stands beside him, her asymmetrical hair (left buzzed, right dark wavy) catching the wind. She's covered in plushie keychains—a neon pink monster and a small blue alien dangle from her belt. She clutches a data pad.

GEM, in her SOL agent tactical gear, hands them each a translucent pouch of thick, crimson liquid.

<center>GEM</center>
> Drink up. We're outside the SSOL's atmosphere now. You won't last ten minutes on Martian air without this.

Lucky takes the pouch, eyeing the viscosity.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Type C blood. PFC-based, right?

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (nodding)
> Perfluorocarbons. They dissolve twenty times more oxygen than plasma. On Mars, your stomach is your lung. The intestinal lining absorbs the PFC molecules directly into your bloodstream.

Lucky drinks. The taste is coppery and sweet, like high-octane fuel. He feels an immediate rush—a surge of energy and clarity. He let's out a long, heavy breath.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (burping softly)
> That's the CO2 venting, I assume?

<center>ASTRA</center>
> Exactly. You'll need to "vent" frequently. If you feel lightheaded, it means your blood is turning acidic. Drink more.

They walk through the colony streets. The economy is visible everywhere. Clones exchange small vials of blood for goods. Large "BLOOD BANKS" dominate the corners—reinforced bunkers with networks of pulsing, organic tubes running between them. LUCKY sees a rhythmic pulse in the translucent pipes: actual cloned hearts, oversized and mechanical-hybrid, pumping the Type C blood through the city's grid.

Suddenly, a loud SIREN blares. A group of CLONES with painted white faces and red smiles—CLOWNS—burst out of a nearby building, carrying crates of blood vials. They fire chaotic energy blasts into the air, laughing maniacally as they scramble into a modified rover.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Bank robbery?

<center>GEM</center>
> Clowns. They're addicted to the rush of pure Type C. They raid the banks to stay high and sabotage the Queen's supply.

Lucky watches the rover disappear into the dust.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Looks like we've got a problem with the local residents.

<center>GEM</center>
> The Queen is waiting. We need to move.

FADE OUT.
