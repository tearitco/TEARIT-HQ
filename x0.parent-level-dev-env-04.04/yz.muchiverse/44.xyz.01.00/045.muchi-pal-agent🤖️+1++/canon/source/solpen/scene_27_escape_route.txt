EXT. MARTIAN DESERT - DAY

SOUND of roaring engines, high-pitched energy blasts, the screaming of wind-blown dust

The chase is on. LUCKY and the BLACK QUEEN’S forces are in heavily armored, modified "Mad Max" style futuristic rovers, hurtling through the streets and out into the vast Martian desert.

LUCKY, his platinum blonde high-top fade whipping in the open-top vehicle, mans a mounted energy turret. ASTRA, her asymmetrical hair full of dust and her plushie keychains clattering against her armor, handles the navigation.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (Into comms)
> There's the spy! He's heading for the hospital!

Dozens of CLOWN ROVERS—jagged machines covered in spikes and neon paint—burst from the dunes, flanking the Queen’s convoy. The desert becomes a battlefield. Energy beams cut through the red dust, flipping rovers and vaporizing tires.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> (From the lead rover)
> Call for backup! We need all units at the hospital!

They reach the hospital—the "First Nest." A massive battle ensues as the Queen’s elite clones clash with the clown swarm.

LUCKY and ASTRA burst into the maternity ward, weapons ready. But the room is empty. No Clown Queen. No Carcazod Brutes. Only a massive, pulsing DEVICE in the center of the room.

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (checking her HUD)
> It's not a nest anymore. It's a BOMB! It's a trap!

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (Into comms)
> Fall back! Everyone OUT!

They scramble into their rover and floor it just as the hospital EXPLODES in a blinding white flash. A massive cloud of red dust and debris billows into the Martian sky.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (coughing)
> They used the hospital as a diversion.

<center>GEM</center>
> (Scanning the horizon)
> I've got them! Multiple signatures at the Space Port! They're launching!

Lucky’s jawline sets. He adjusts his gold chains.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> To the Space Port. We can't let those ships reach Earth.

FADE OUT.
