INT. CLEAN ZONE "000010001000" - DIAMOND MINE - DAY

SOUND of energy barriers humming, the distant clinking of gold bars, the rhythmic drilling of automated mining equipment

Clean Zone "000010001000" is a paradise of marble and light, a super-advanced civilization protected from the clown-led chaos outside. Deep beneath its surface, within a massive, glittering DIAMOND MINE, a empire of hoarding is being built.

LUCKY, ASTRA, and the real GEM stand on a high balcony, looking down into the mine’s main cavern. LUCKY, his platinum blonde high-top fade sharp under the crystal lights, stares in disbelief.

Below, thousands of crates are being filled. Not with diamonds, but with massive quantities of GOLD BARS and ADVANCED WEAPONS.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (whispering)
> He's not just hiding. He's preparing for an eternal war.

Standing at a central console, overseeing the loading, is FAKE LUCKY—a man who looks identical to Lucky Starr, down to the platinum blonde fade and the gold chains. But this man's expression is cold, centuries of burden in his eyes.

Beside him is another GEM—or perhaps the original, who stayed behind. She’s checking a shipment of heavy pulse rifles.

<center>FAKE LUCKY</center>
> (to the clone guards)
> Faster. The Silent Shadow doesn't wait for paperwork.

The Fake Lucky turns, his eyes meeting the real Lucky's from across the cavern. He doesn't look surprised.

<center>FAKE LUCKY</center>
> (Into comms)
> Welcome home, Lucky. I've been waiting for you to see what’s necessary.

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (clutching her plushie keychain)
> He’s used the clowns to hunt us down just to bring us into his vault.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> He hasn't stolen my identity. He's fulfilling it.

The Fake Lucky—SOL from the future—smiles sadly as he gestures to the vast wealth of resources.

<center>FAKE LUCKY</center>
> You call it hoarding. I call it the only chance humanity has left.

FADE OUT.
