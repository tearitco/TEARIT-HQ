# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 10

### The Mothers

**1** And it came to pass after the First Nest had awakened and after the ancient voice had spoken unto Lucky, saying, "Welcome Home," that none among the company knew how to answer, for fear and wonder wrestled together within their hearts, and every certainty they had carried into the depths of Mars now seemed fragile as glass.

**2** Then Astra stood before Lucky with weapon drawn, though she knew not whether she defended him from the chamber or the chamber from him, and the others likewise prepared themselves, for when ancient things recognize a man whom they should not know, wisdom counseleth caution.

**3** Yet the chamber did not attack them, neither did the sleeping creature arise from its cradle, but instead the lights within the structure brightened gradually, revealing carvings, symbols, and records hidden for ages beneath layers of dust and silence.

**4** And the voice spoke again, saying, "Authorized lineage detected. Historical archives unlocked. Maternal records available."

**5** Then Mara said, "I dislike every word of that sentence."

**6** And Astra answered, "For once, we are in complete agreement."

**7** Therefore Lucky approached one of the illuminated pillars, and as he drew near the Soul Pen shone brighter still, whereupon symbols flowed from the Pen into the structure and from the structure into the Pen, so that neither device nor man appeared wholly separate from the other.

**8** Then images appeared throughout the chamber, not upon screens fashioned by human hands but within the air itself, and the company beheld visions from an age so ancient that no history of Earth contained record thereof.

**9** They saw a red world before the coming of mankind, when the deserts of Mars stretched untouched beneath foreign stars and when great cities stood where no human foot had yet trodden.

**10** And these cities were not built of stone nor steel but seemed grown according to patterns both biological and mathematical, as though living organisms and architecture had become one thing.

**11** Then the visions changed, and they beheld vast beings moving among those cities, beings so immense that towers reached only unto their knees, yet whose movements were graceful and deliberate, as gardeners tending a cherished field.

**12** Therefore Cassian fell to his knees once more and whispered, "The Mothers."

**13** And all understood that the sleeping creature before them belonged unto the same race.

**14** Yet the vision continued, revealing that the Mothers were neither gods nor beasts but makers of worlds, travelers between stars, and keepers of knowledge beyond human comprehension, who carried within themselves the ability to shape life according to design and purpose.

**15** For wherever they journeyed, life followed after them, and barren worlds blossomed, while dead worlds awakened and flourished beneath their care.

**16** Then Astra said, "If these things are true, why are they gone?"

**17** But before anyone could answer, the vision itself provided reply.

**18** For darkness appeared among the stars.

**19** Not the ordinary darkness of empty space, but another kind, moving with intention and spreading like a disease through entire systems.

**20** And worlds vanished.

**21** Great structures burned.

**22** Civilizations collapsed.

**23** While the Mothers fled before an enemy not fully shown.

**24** Then the company watched as the surviving Mothers gathered together and devised a final plan, planting Nests upon distant worlds and entering long cycles of sleep, hoping that one day suitable heirs would awaken them when the danger had passed.

**25** Thus the First Nests were established.

**26** Thus the Mothers entered their slumber.

**27** And thus countless ages passed.

**28** Then the vision faded.

**29** Yet many questions remained.

**30** Therefore Mara asked, "What hath this to do with Mars?"

**31** And immediately new images appeared.

**32** The surface of Mars was shown.

**33** The First Nest was shown.

**34** Then came the words:

**35** "PRIMARY COLONIAL PLATFORM."

**36** "WORLD-SHAPING FOUNDATION."

**37** "TERRAFORMING ORIGIN."

**38** At first none understood.

**39** Then the realization came.

**40** Slowly.

**41** Terribly.

**42** Therefore Lucky read the symbols aloud.

**43** And his voice trembled.

**44** For he understood before the others.

**45** Then he said, "The Nest was here before the colony."

**46** Yet the symbols continued.

**47** "MARTIAN BIOSPHERE GENERATED FROM PRIMARY FOUNDATION."

**48** "HUMAN SETTLEMENTS CONSTRUCTED UPON EXISTING STRUCTURES."

**49** "COLONIAL DEVELOPMENT UTILIZED PRECURSOR SYSTEMS."

**50** Then silence filled the chamber.

**51** For all understood.

**52** The First Nest was not built beneath Mars.

**53** Humanity had built Mars upon the First Nest.

**54** Then even Astra was unable to find words.

**55** And Mara sat heavily upon a broken platform, for much of what she believed concerning Martian history had collapsed within the span of moments.

**56** Yet Cassian appeared even more disturbed.

**57** Therefore Lucky asked him, saying, "What troubles thee now?"

**58** And Cassian answered, "Because the Blood Banks suddenly make sense."

**59** Then all turned toward him.

**60** And he continued.

**61** "The hearts."

**62** "The blood."

**63** "The circulation systems."

**64** "The Flesh Priests."

**65** "All of it."

**66** "We believed we were feeding the colony."

**67** "But what if the colony was feeding something else?"

**68** Thereupon a dreadful understanding spread among them.

**69** For the giant hearts beneath New Antioch were not random machinery.

**70** Neither were they symbolic decorations.

**71** They formed part of a biological support system.

**72** A life-support network.

**73** A feeding apparatus.

**74** A circulatory system extending throughout the colony.

**75** And all of it connected unto the sleeping Mothers.

**76** Then Astra looked toward the immense creature resting within the cradle.

**77** And she said, "The Queen knoweth."

**78** Mara answered, "She must."

**79** "No ruler could oversee systems this vast without learning their purpose."

**80** Yet another question remained.

**81** Therefore Lucky asked, "Then why doth she fear them?"

**82** At this the chamber responded once more.

**83** New records emerged.

**84** Some damaged.

**85** Some incomplete.

**86** Yet enough remained.

**87** And the company beheld images from the earliest days of human settlement upon Mars.

**88** They saw explorers discovering the Nest.

**89** Scientists studying the sleeping Mothers.

**90** Military officials securing the site.

**91** Corporations competing for access.

**92** Then they witnessed a decision.

**93** A terrible decision.

**94** Rather than awakening the Mothers, humanity chose to exploit them.

**95** Their biological systems were harvested.

**96** Their technologies reverse-engineered.

**97** Their circulatory networks integrated into colonial infrastructure.

**98** Their gifts transformed into industries.

**99** Their life-support systems converted into economies.

**100** Thus the Blood Banks were born.

**101** Thus the Heart Reactors were created.

**102** Thus New Antioch prospered.

**103** And as the centuries passed, the original truth became hidden beneath layers of doctrine, bureaucracy, commerce, and myth.

**104** Then Mara whispered, "We built a civilization atop sleeping giants."

**105** And none disputed her words.

**106** Yet one final archive remained unopened.

**107** For it bore markings unlike all the others.

**108** Then the voice announced:

**109** "AUTHORIZED HEIR ACCESS ONLY."

**110** And immediately every light in the chamber focused upon Lucky.

**111** Therefore Astra stepped beside him.

**112** And Mara likewise approached.

**113** Yet the archive refused them.

**114** For it recognized only him.

**115** Then Lucky hesitated.

**116** For he feared what further truths might emerge.

**117** Yet at last he raised the Soul Pen and touched the seal.

**118** Thereupon the archive opened.

**119** And a final image appeared.

**120** The image showed a human child.

**121** Yet the child stood among the Mothers.

**122** And the Mothers regarded him not as a stranger but as family.

**123** Then the archive identified the image.

**124** Saying:

**125** "GENETIC BRIDGE PROGRAM."

**126** "SUCCESSFUL."

**127** "HEIR DESIGNATION: LUCKY."

**128** Then every soul in the chamber became silent.

**129** For the words seemed impossible.

**130** And Astra immediately said, "That cannot be true."

**131** Yet the archive continued.

**132** "PURPOSE: FUTURE AWAKENING."

**133** "PURPOSE: COMMUNICATION."

**134** "PURPOSE: RECONCILIATION."

**135** "PURPOSE: SURVIVAL."

**136** Then the image faded.

**137** And the chamber grew dark once more.

**138** Yet before the lights fully vanished, the sleeping Mother moved again.

**139** This time more noticeably than before.

**140** One immense eye opened.

**141** Only partially.

**142** Yet within that eye shone intelligence older than civilizations.

**143** Older than Mars.

**144** Older than history itself.

**145** Then a single thought entered the minds of all who stood within the chamber.

**146** Not spoken.

**147** Not heard.

**148** Yet understood.

**149** And the thought was this:

**150** *"The Dream is ending."*

**151** Immediately the eye closed once more.

**152** The chamber fell silent.

**153** The lights dimmed.

**154** And the ancient systems returned unto dormancy.

**155** Yet none who stood there remained unchanged.

**156** For they had learned the truth of the Mothers.

**157** They had learned the purpose of the Blood Banks.

**158** They had learned why the Queen feared what slept beneath her kingdom.

**159** And they had learned that Lucky's connection unto the Soul Pen was no accident.

**160** But far above them, within the towers of New Antioch, other powers had begun to move.

**161** For the awakening of the Nest had not gone unnoticed.

**162** The Flesh Priests felt it.

**163** The Black Queen learned of it.

**164** And in places deeper still, where neither rebel nor ruler dared travel, something else awakened alongside the Mothers.

**165** Something older.

**166** Something wounded.

**167** Something that remembered.

**168** And its name was Carcazod.
