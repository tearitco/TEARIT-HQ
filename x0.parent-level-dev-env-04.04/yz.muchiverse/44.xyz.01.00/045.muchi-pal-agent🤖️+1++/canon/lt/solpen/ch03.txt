# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 3

### The Trial of the Moon

**1** And it came to pass immediately after the alarms had sounded throughout the station, that Sol led Lucky and Astra from the chamber of observation and into the corridors of the Moon Facility.

**2** And the lights of the station changed from white unto crimson, and warning symbols appeared upon every wall, and automated voices proclaimed the breach continually.

**3** And the sound of distant roars echoed through the halls, and many who dwelt within the facility fled toward secure sectors.

**4** Now among those fleeing were engineers, technicians, researchers, guards, and apprentices of the Pen, and fear was upon them all.

**5** For none knew the extent of the breach, neither did they know how many creatures had escaped.

**6** Then Astra said unto Sol, "Tell me truly: were those creatures always dinosaurs?"

**7** And Sol answered, "Once they were."

**8** But Astra was not satisfied, and she said, "And what are they now?"

**9** Then Sol remained silent for a time.

**10** At length he answered, saying, "Experiments."

**11** And Lucky said, "I dislike that answer."

**12** And Sol replied, "Most sensible people do."

**13** Then they came unto a security station where armed guards had assembled themselves behind barricades of metal.

**14** And the commander of the guards approached Sol and said, "Sector Seven is lost."

**15** And Sol asked, "Casualties?"

**16** The commander answered, "Twenty-three confirmed. Forty-two missing."

**17** Then even Astra grew silent.

**18** For she perceived that this matter was no mere training exercise.

**19** And Sol said unto the commander, "Evacuate the civilians and seal the lower laboratories."

**20** But the commander answered, "The lower laboratories no longer respond."

**21** And hearing this, Sol frowned.

**22** For the lower laboratories contained many things not intended to walk freely.

**23** Then the station trembled.

**24** And a mighty impact shook the walls.

**25** Dust fell from the ceiling.

**26** And a second impact followed.

**27** Then a third.

**28** And all who were present turned toward the blast doors at the end of the corridor.

**29** For something approached.

**30** Suddenly the doors burst inward.

**31** And fragments of steel flew throughout the chamber.

**32** Then there emerged a creature unlike any beast known upon Earth.

**33** Its form resembled that of a great raptor.

**34** Yet its flesh was transparent as glass.

**35** And beneath its skin could be seen organs, muscles, and bones moving continually.

**36** Its eyes shone with blue light.

**37** And strange symbols flickered beneath its flesh as though information itself had become part of its body.

**38** Then many guards opened fire.

**39** And the corridor was filled with flashes of light.

**40** Yet the creature moved with astonishing speed.

**41** It leapt upon the barricades.

**42** And three guards fell before they could retreat.

**43** Then Astra raised her weapon and struck the beast.

**44** And the creature stumbled.

**45** Yet it did not fall.

**46** Then Lucky cried out, saying, "How is that thing still alive?"

**47** And Sol answered, "Because life is no longer what it once was."

**48** Thereupon Sol drew forth the Soul Pen.

**49** And with a swift motion he inscribed symbols in the air.

**50** And a wall of crystal arose between the creature and the fleeing civilians.

**51** Then Sol placed the Pen into the hands of Lucky.

**52** And Lucky was astonished.

**53** For he had expected Sol to keep such a thing unto himself.

**54** But Sol said, "Use it."

**55** Then Lucky answered, "I have no idea how."

**56** And Sol replied, "Neither did the first man who ever lifted a sword."

**57** Then the creature attacked once more.

**58** And Astra narrowly escaped its claws.

**59** Therefore Lucky gripped the Pen and attempted to imitate the symbols he had seen Sol create.

**60** And though his hand trembled, he traced a pattern in the air.

**61** At first nothing happened.

**62** Then a burst of blue light appeared.

**63** And suddenly a barrier rose from the floor.

**64** The creature collided with it and was thrown backward.

**65** Then the guards rejoiced.

**66** And Astra laughed and said, "Well done."

**67** But Lucky stared at the Pen in disbelief.

**68** For he knew with certainty that he had not fully understood what he had written.

**69** Yet the Pen had understood.

**70** Then Sol said unto him, "The Pen is not merely a tool."

**71** "It is a language."

**72** "And every language seeketh a speaker."

**73** While they yet spoke, another alarm sounded.

**74** Then came a message from the station command.

**75** Saying, "Multiple breaches detected."

**76** "Sector Four compromised."

**77** "Sector Eight compromised."

**78** "Biological containment failure expanding."

**79** And the faces of the guards grew pale.

**80** For they understood what this meant.

**81** The creatures were spreading.

**82** Then Sol touched a panel upon the wall.

**83** And there appeared a map of the station.

**84** Now the Moon Facility was vast.

**85** For beneath its surface were many levels.

**86** Laboratories.

**87** Factories.

**88** Habitation sectors.

**89** Training halls.

**90** Storage vaults.

**91** And tunnels extending deep into the lunar rock.

**92** Yet upon the map there appeared many red symbols.

**93** And the symbols multiplied continually.

**94** Then Astra beheld them and said, "How many escaped?"

**95** And Sol answered, "Too many."

**96** Then Lucky perceived one symbol larger than all the others.

**97** It moved slowly through the lower sectors.

**98** And wherever it traveled, smaller symbols gathered around it.

**99** Therefore Lucky pointed unto it and asked, "What is that one?"

**100** Then Sol looked upon the symbol.

**101** And for the first time they saw concern upon his face.

**102** And he answered, saying, "That is not supposed to exist."

**103** Then Astra said, "That answer is becoming a habit."

**104** Yet Sol ignored her.

**105** For he continued looking upon the map.

**106** And at length he said, "The Alpha hath awakened."

**107** Then the commander of the guards stepped backward in fear.

**108** And several engineers exchanged troubled glances.

**109** For they knew the meaning of those words.

**110** But Lucky and Astra knew it not.

**111** Therefore Lucky said, "What is the Alpha?"

**112** And Sol answered, "The first."

**113** "The strongest."

**114** "The one from which the others were patterned."

**115** Then the station shook more violently than before.

**116** And lights flickered throughout the corridor.

**117** The map upon the wall distorted.

**118** Yet the large symbol continued moving upward.

**119** Level by level.

**120** Corridor by corridor.

**121** Toward them.

**122** Then Sol closed the display.

**123** And he looked upon Lucky and Astra.

**124** And he said, "The lesson is concluded."

**125** But Astra answered, "The battle hath barely begun."

**126** And Sol replied, "Precisely."

**127** Then he turned and began walking toward the lower sectors.

**128** And the guards followed after him.

**129** And Lucky took up the Soul Pen.

**130** And Astra checked her weapon.

**131** And together they descended into the depths of the Moon.

**132** For the Alpha Ghost Rex was rising from the darkness below.

**133** And the first true battle of the Soul Pen had begun.
