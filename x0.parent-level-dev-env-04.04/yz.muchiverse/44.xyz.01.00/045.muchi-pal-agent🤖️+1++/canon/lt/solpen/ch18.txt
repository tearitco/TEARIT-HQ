# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 18

### The Red Plague

**1** And it came to pass after the Silence of Mars, that the corruption which had touched the lower districts spread upward among the living quarters, the transit lines, and the supply wards, and people began to name it the Red Plague.

**2** For it appeared first as fever, then as dream sickness, then as a strange red bloom beneath the skin.

**3** Yet soon it became clear that the disease was no ordinary sickness.

**4** For it altered memory.

**5** It bent speech.

**6** It changed behavior.

**7** And it copied itself through imitation as much as through blood.

**8** Then the Queen ordered all remaining hospitals to isolate the infected.

**9** But the hospitals themselves became nests of fear, for many of the caregivers dreamed the same dream and awoke with the same hunger.

**10** Therefore the Black Queen descended from her throne chambers and went among the people openly, though her advisors begged her not to expose herself.

**11** And seeing her, many understood that the colony had entered a war she could no longer hide.

**12** Then she declared the truth.

**13** "Mars is not merely sick."

**14** "Mars is waking."

**15** "And what waketh beneath us may have already reached the blood of our world."

**16** Thereafter fear changed into action.

**17** Some fled.

**18** Some fought.

**19** Some prayed.

**20** And some turned upon their neighbors as the plague rewrote them from within.

**21** Then Lucky and Astra labored with the Soul Pen to seal infected corridors and separate the living from the changing.

**22** And they discovered that every time the Pen wrote a barrier, the plague seemed to think back against it.

**23** For the corruption was learning the shape of resistance.

**24** Then the Mother said, "It imitates what it hates."

**25** "Therefore beware the mask it wears."

**26** At that hour reports came from the orbital ports.

**27** Ships bound for Earth had departed.

**28** Cargo manifests were being altered in transit.

**29** And blood shipments had already left the colony under routine commercial cover.

**30** Then Mara said, "Then Earth shall not be spared."

**31** And Cassian answered, "Not spared, no."

**32** "Only delayed."

**33** Meanwhile the Queen ordered a final containment action.

**34** She commanded the old Bloodguard structures to wake.

**35** And deep beneath the colony great barriers began to rise, ancient and vast, designed to hold back what the earliest rulers had feared.

**36** Yet the barriers answered imperfectly.

**37** For the plague had already entered too many systems.

**38** Then the Mother looked at Lucky and said, "Thou must go."

**39** "Not deeper."

**40** "Outward."

**41** "For the wound no longer endeth on Mars."

**42** Therefore Lucky understood that the fight had changed.

**43** It was no longer only a Martian crisis.

**44** It had become a message sent toward Earth.

**45** And what answered that message would decide the age to come.
