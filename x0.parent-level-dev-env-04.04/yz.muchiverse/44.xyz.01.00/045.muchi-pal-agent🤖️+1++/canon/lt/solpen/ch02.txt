# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 2

### The Man Called Sol

**1** And it came to pass after Lucky and Astra had been taken from the Earth, that they knew not whether they slept or whether they were awake, for their senses were troubled and their minds could not comprehend what had befallen them.

**2** For they had been carried upward through the blue light and beyond the clouds of heaven, and they had seen the desert city become as a speck beneath them, and afterward all memory became uncertain.

**3** Then Lucky opened his eyes and found himself lying upon a floor smooth as polished silver, and above him was a ceiling unlike any work of man.

**4** And lines of light moved continually across the surface thereof, like rivers flowing through crystal.

**5** Therefore he rose to his feet and looked about him.

**6** And he beheld a great chamber whose walls curved gently upward, and whose windows revealed the darkness of space.

**7** Then fear came upon him, for through those windows he saw the Earth hanging in the heavens.

**8** And it appeared as a blue jewel suspended within an endless sea of darkness.

**9** Now Astra also awoke and perceived the same things, and she approached the glass and gazed upon the Earth.

**10** And she remained silent for a time.

**11** Then she said, "No aircraft of men can travel here."

**12** And Lucky answered, "Then either we are dead, or everything we believed concerning the world is incomplete."

**13** While they yet spoke together, a door opened at the far end of the chamber.

**14** And a man entered.

**15** His garments were dark.

**16** His face was partly concealed.

**17** His age could not be discerned.

**18** Yet his bearing was calm, as one who fears nothing.

**19** And the man regarded them for a time before speaking.

**20** Then he said, "Welcome to the Moon."

**21** And Astra laughed once, though there was little joy in it.

**22** And she answered, "Then I suppose the next thing you tell us shall be that all the old stories are true."

**23** But the man replied, "Many are true. Most are incomplete."

**24** Then Lucky stepped forward and said, "Who are you, and why have we been brought here?"

**25** The man answered and said, "Many names have been given unto me. Some know me as Sol."

**26** "And as for why you are here, the answer is simple."

**27** "Because someone wished to remove you from the Earth."

**28** Then Astra said, "Who?"

**29** And Sol answered, "That is a longer conversation."

**30** Now there was in the chamber a table formed of white stone, and Sol beckoned unto them.

**31** Therefore they approached.

**32** And as they drew near, symbols appeared upon the surface of the table.

**33** Images formed thereon.

**34** Mountains arose.

**35** Oceans appeared.

**36** Cities came into view.

**37** And at length the whole Earth was displayed before them.

**38** Then Sol stretched forth his hand.

**39** And the image expanded.

**40** The Moon appeared.

**41** Then Mars.

**42** Then worlds and stations unknown to mankind.

**43** And beyond these appeared structures so vast that entire cities might have rested within them.

**44** Then Lucky said, "What are these places?"

**45** And Sol answered, "The kingdoms beyond Earth."

**46** "Some are governed by men."

**47** "Some by machines."

**48** "Some by things which were never human."

**49** Then Astra felt unease and said, "Why have we never heard of them?"

**50** Sol answered and said, "Because knowledge is often hidden from those who are not yet ready to bear it."

**51** Then the image changed.

**52** And there appeared many scenes of war.

**53** Colonies burning.

**54** Fleets destroyed.

**55** Cities abandoned.

**56** And creatures unknown moving through darkness.

**57** Then Sol said, "A conflict is coming."

**58** "Its beginning hath already occurred."

**59** "Though few upon Earth yet perceive it."

**60** Then Lucky said, "What hath this to do with us?"

**61** And Sol looked upon him and answered, "More than you know."

**62** Thereafter Sol touched the surface of the table.

**63** And the images vanished.

**64** In their place appeared a single object.

**65** It was shaped as a pen.

**66** Yet no pen fashioned by man had ever resembled it.

**67** Its body shone like liquid silver.

**68** Symbols moved beneath its surface.

**69** Light flowed through it as blood floweth through living flesh.

**70** Then Astra said, "What is it?"

**71** And Sol answered, "The Soul Pen."

**72** "One of the oldest artifacts still remaining."

**73** "A tool of creation."

**74** "A weapon."

**75** "A key."

**76** "And perhaps the most dangerous object in existence."

**77** Then Lucky regarded the Pen and felt within himself both wonder and fear.

**78** For it seemed alive.

**79** And he perceived that the thing possessed a purpose beyond his understanding.

**80** Then Sol took the Pen and wrote a symbol in the air.

**81** And the symbol remained suspended before them.

**82** It shone with blue light.

**83** Then a wall emerged where previously there had been empty space.

**84** Stone formed itself from nothing.

**85** Mortar appeared.

**86** And within moments a complete structure stood before them.

**87** Then Astra stepped backward in astonishment.

**88** And Lucky was speechless.

**89** For neither had ever witnessed such power.

**90** Then Sol said unto them, "Matter obeyeth information."

**91** "Information obeyeth language."

**92** "And language obeyeth the Pen."

**93** Yet before either could answer, alarms suddenly sounded throughout the facility.

**94** Red lights flashed along the walls.

**95** Doors sealed themselves.

**96** And a voice echoed through the station.

**97** Saying, "Containment failure."

**98** "Containment failure."

**99** "Training Sector Seven breached."

**100** Then Sol closed his eyes for a moment and sighed.

**101** And Astra said, "That sounds bad."

**102** Sol answered, "It is."

**103** Then another alarm sounded.

**104** And distant roars echoed through the corridors.

**105** The sound thereof was unlike any beast of Earth.

**106** Yet it possessed something strangely familiar.

**107** Then Lucky said, "What escaped?"

**108** And Sol looked toward the sealed doors.

**109** And he answered, "Dinosaurs."

**110** There was silence.

**111** Then Astra said, "You expect me to believe dinosaurs are loose on the Moon?"

**112** And Sol replied, "Believe what you wish."

**113** "They are still loose."

**114** Then the station trembled beneath a mighty impact.

**115** And the walls shook.

**116** And a roar sounded again, nearer than before.

**117** Then Sol placed the Soul Pen upon the table before Lucky.

**118** And he said, "Your education begins immediately."

**119** For the Trial of the Moon was at hand.

**120** And beyond the Moon, beyond Mars, and beyond the sight of men, darker powers had already begun to stir.

**121** Yet their names were not revealed in those days.

**122** For the appointed time had not yet come.
