# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 11

### The Queen's Burden

**1** And it came to pass after the revelation of the Mothers and after the awakening of the First Nest, that alarms began sounding throughout many hidden systems beneath New Antioch, and signals long dormant awakened one after another, causing concern among rulers, priests, engineers, and soldiers alike.

**2** For though the common people knew it not, the sleeping mechanisms beneath Mars had begun speaking to one another across great distances, and messages passed through forgotten networks older than the colony itself, as though ancient organs had remembered their purpose after a long slumber.

**3** Then within the Citadel of Governance the Black Queen sat alone in her chamber, and before her stood displays numbering beyond count, each showing reports from different regions of the colony, and every report carried the same troubling conclusion.

**4** The Blood Banks were changing.

**5** The Heart Reactors were accelerating.

**6** The deep sensors were detecting movement.

**7** And the dreams had returned.

**8** Now the Queen was not ignorant concerning these matters, neither had she inherited her throne through chance, for every ruler of New Antioch received secret knowledge hidden from ordinary citizens, and among that knowledge was the history of the Mothers.

**9** Yet much had been lost over generations, and even the Queen possessed only fragments of the truth, like a woman holding torn pages from a greater book.

**10** Then there entered her chamber an elderly advisor named Voss, who had served three generations of Martian rulers and whose loyalty was considered beyond question.

**11** And Voss said unto her, "The disturbances increase."

**12** Then the Queen answered, "I know."

**13** And Voss continued, saying, "The First Nest hath activated."

**14** At these words the Queen closed her eyes briefly.

**15** Not from surprise.

**16** But from exhaustion.

**17** For she had feared this day for many years.

**18** Then Voss asked, "Shall we begin evacuation?"

**19** But the Queen answered, "To where?"

**20** "Mars containeth billions."

**21** "The stations contain millions."

**22** "The transport fleets cannot carry a fraction."

**23** "And Earth would never believe our warnings."

**24** Thus silence fell between them.

**25** For both understood the truth.

**26** There existed no evacuation plan.

**27** Only delays.

**28** Then the Queen arose and approached a sealed vault hidden behind the walls of her chamber.

**29** And placing her hand upon an ancient mechanism, she opened the vault and withdrew a small metallic object wrapped in protective cloth.

**30** Then Voss beheld it and grew pale.

**31** For few living souls had ever seen such a thing.

**32** And when the Queen unwrapped it, there appeared a pen.

**33** Yet it was not the Soul Pen.

**34** It resembled the Soul Pen.

**35** But its light was broken.

**36** Its surface was cracked.

**37** And portions of its structure appeared corrupted.

**38** Therefore Voss whispered, "The Shadow Pen."

**39** Then the Queen nodded.

**40** Saying, "My predecessors found it during the earliest excavations."

**41** "It was discovered near the deepest chambers."

**42** "Near the places where the records end."

**43** "Near the places they feared to enter."

**44** Then she looked toward Mars beyond the window.

**45** And she said, "This was our first warning."

**46** Now far beneath the city Lucky and his companions had not yet departed from the First Nest, for the revelation concerning the Mothers had shaken them greatly, and many questions remained unanswered.

**47** Therefore they searched the archives more thoroughly, hoping to discover knowledge concerning Carcazod.

**48** And after much labor they found records whose contents had been deliberately damaged.

**49** Yet fragments remained.

**50** And one fragment declared:

**51** "Containment successful."

**52** Another declared:

**53** "Dream activity reduced."

**54** Another declared:

**55** "Cross-species corruption halted."

**56** Then there appeared one final surviving sentence.

**57** Though incomplete.

**58** And it read:

**59** "Should Carcazod awaken, all barriers shall eventually..."

**60** There the record ended.

**61** Then Astra folded her arms and said, "I dislike documents that stop at the important part."

**62** Yet Cassian appeared frightened.

**63** More frightened than before.

**64** Therefore Mara asked him, saying, "What dost thou know?"

**65** And Cassian answered reluctantly.

**66** "Among the Flesh Priests there exist forbidden teachings."

**67** "Most believe Carcazod to be a god."

**68** "Others believe it to be the first Mother."

**69** "Others believe it to be the enemy of the Mothers."

**70** "Yet all agree concerning one thing."

**71** Then he paused.

**72** And none spoke.

**73** For they desired to hear the remainder.

**74** Therefore Cassian continued.

**75** Saying, "Carcazod dreameth."

**76** "And while it sleepeth, its dreams become reality."

**77** Then Lucky remembered the words spoken by the Mother.

**78** The Dream is ending.

**79** And a cold feeling entered his spirit.

**80** For he began to understand that awakening the Mothers might also awaken something else.

**81** Meanwhile, throughout New Antioch, ordinary citizens began reporting unusual occurrences.

**82** Workers claimed to hear voices within machinery.

**83** Children described cities they had never visited.

**84** Engineers dreamed of impossible geometries.

**85** Pilots awoke speaking languages unknown to mankind.

**86** And though these events appeared unrelated, they increased daily.

**87** Then there occurred a greater sign.

**88** For one of the giant Heart Reactors beneath the eastern district ceased beating for precisely seven minutes.

**89** During those seven minutes every system connected unto it experienced failure.

**90** Yet when the reactor resumed operation its rhythm had changed.

**91** And all those who heard it afterward said that it no longer sounded as a machine, but as a thing thinking in its sleep.

**92** Then the Queen received word of disturbances in the lower sectors, and she knew that time had passed beyond fear and entered the hour of decision.

**93** Therefore she placed the Shadow Pen within the vault once more and summoned her commanders, priests, and engineers, commanding them to lock the higher districts, hide the truth from the public, and prepare the colony for a war no citizen yet understood.

**94** But in her heart she knew that locks and proclamations were no match for what had begun beneath Mars.

**95** For the Nest had awakened.

**96** The records had begun to speak.

**97** The dreams had returned.

**98** And somewhere below the blood and stone, Carcazod had begun to turn toward the light.
