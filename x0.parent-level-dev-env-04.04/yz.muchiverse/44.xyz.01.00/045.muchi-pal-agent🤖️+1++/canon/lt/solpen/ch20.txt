# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 20

### The Beginning of the Last Age

**1** And it came to pass when Earth had received the gift of Mars and the boundaries between worlds had begun to fail, that the rulers of many nations gathered in fear and called their councils into session.

**2** Yet none among them possessed a single answer.

**3** For the scientists spoke of contagion.

**4** The generals spoke of containment.

**5** The priests spoke of judgment.

**6** And the merchants spoke of opportunity until even they grew ashamed of their own voices.

**7** Then Lucky stood among the survivors and looked upon the ruined logic of the age.

**8** And he understood that humanity had entered a new season.

**9** Not the age of exploration.

**10** Not the age of empire.

**11** But the beginning of the last age.

**12** Thereafter the Mother, weakened yet awake, spoke unto him and said, "Thou hast seen the root, the wound, the lie, and the bridge."

**13** "What remaineth is choice."

**14** "Shalt thou become what was prepared?"

**15** "Or shalt thou break the circle and bear the cost?"

**16** Then Lucky lifted the Soul Pen.

**17** And he looked toward the darkened heavens, where Mars still burned red above the world.

**18** And he remembered Sol, the Queen, the Mothers, the blood, the dream, and the warnings written across so many broken things.

**19** Then he answered, "If the world must be remade, let it be by truth and not by the Dream."

**20** And the chamber about him answered with silence, for silence was the only honest witness left.

**21** Then far away the first new fortifications rose on Earth.

**22** The first civilian sanctuaries opened.

**23** The first clean zones were marked.

**24** The first names were lost.

**25** And the first age ended in the same hour that the last age began.
