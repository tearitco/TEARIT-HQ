# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 16

### The Arena of New Antioch

**1** And it came to pass after the discovery of the Blood Banks, that the rulers of New Antioch sought to turn the hearts of the people away from the rebels, and therefore they proclaimed a great spectacle in the Arena, saying, "Come and behold the strength of the Colony, and witness the triumph of order over chaos."

**2** Now the Arena stood in the center of the city, and was surrounded by towers of black steel and great screens of light, so that its image could be seen throughout every district beneath the Martian domes.

**3** And many thousands gathered there, some willingly and some by force, for no citizen desired to draw suspicion upon himself by refusing the summons.

**4** And among the crowd were Lucky, Astra, and Gem, who had disguised themselves as laborers that they might observe the proceedings without being known.

**5** Yet their hearts were troubled, for they perceived that the gathering was not merely entertainment, but a display of power intended to strengthen the authority of those who served Carcazod.

**6** Then there entered into the Arena certain prisoners who had been accused of rebellion, and they were clothed in gray garments and bound with restraints of iron.

**7** And the announcers mocked them before the multitude, declaring them enemies of prosperity and destroyers of peace.

**8** But Astra looked upon them and said quietly unto Lucky, "These are not soldiers, neither are they terrorists; they are miners, workers, and ordinary people."

**9** And Lucky answered, "Then the purpose of this spectacle is fear."

**10** After these things there arose a great sound from beneath the Arena floor, and the earth shook beneath the feet of the spectators.

**11** The lights dimmed, and all eyes were turned toward the center platform.

**12** Then the platform opened, and from the depths there emerged a machine unlike any machine known upon Earth.

**13** Its height exceeded three houses, and its frame was covered with armor blacker than obsidian, and its eyes burned red as molten metal.

**14** And mounted upon its shoulders were engines of war, while within its chest there revolved strange mechanisms whose purpose none could discern.

**15** Yet the most curious thing was this: that before the machine there rolled a sphere of metal larger than a transport vehicle.

**16** And the people cried out in astonishment, for they knew the machine.

**17** And they called it the Ballistic Titan.

**18** For it had been fashioned for the Arena and for the suppression of unrest, and many who had opposed the Colony had perished before it.

**19** Then the announcer lifted up his voice and proclaimed, "Behold the Guardian of New Antioch, defender of order and servant of progress."

**20** And the crowd answered with uncertain applause, for some cheered sincerely while others clapped only because fear compelled them.

**21** Then the Ballistic Titan seized the great sphere and launched it across the Arena with terrible force.

**22** And the sphere shattered a stone barrier as though it had been made of glass.

**23** The fragments flew in every direction, and many spectators recoiled in terror.

**24** Then the machine launched a second sphere and a third, each striking with the force of artillery.

**25** And the announcers praised the machine, saying, "Who can stand against the Titan, and who can resist its might?"

**26** But Gem, having studied the hidden systems of the Colony, perceived that the spectacle concealed another purpose.

**27** For she observed that every movement of the machine was being recorded and transmitted to facilities beyond the city.

**28** Therefore she said unto her companions, "This is not merely a game, nor even a demonstration; they are testing weapons."

**29** And while she yet spoke, her eyes were opened unto another thing.

**30** For among the officials seated in the highest gallery she saw robed figures clad in crimson garments.

**31** And those figures neither cheered nor applauded, but watched in silence.

**32** And upon their robes were the symbols of the Flesh Priests.

**33** Then Astra said, "What concern have the Priests with a sporting exhibition?"

**34** And Gem answered, "When wolves gather, there is always prey nearby."

**35** After these things the restraints were removed from the prisoners.

**36** And the announcer declared that they would face the Ballistic Titan and prove their loyalty through combat.

**37** But the crowd murmured among themselves, for all understood that no man could survive such a contest.

**38** Then one of the prisoners, being an elderly miner, lifted up his voice and cried, "This is not justice."

**39** And another said, "We are condemned already."

**40** Yet before the execution could begin, a disturbance arose within the lower sectors of the Arena.

**41** For certain rebels, having prepared secretly for many weeks, activated devices hidden beneath the maintenance tunnels.

**42** And suddenly the lights failed.

**43** Darkness covered the Arena.

**44** The great screens went black.

**45** The crowd cried out in confusion.

**46** And alarms sounded throughout the city.

**47** Then Lucky perceived that the appointed hour had come.

**48** Therefore he drew forth the Soul Pen and began to write in the air.

**49** And symbols of blue fire appeared before him.

**50** And from those symbols there emerged barriers of light to shield the innocent from the coming battle.

**51** Then Astra cast aside her disguise.

**52** And many recognized her.

**53** And fear fell upon the servants of the Colony.

**54** For rumors of her deeds had spread throughout Mars.

**55** Then the rebels rose up from every section of the Arena.

**56** And workers, miners, smugglers, and fugitives joined themselves together.

**57** The guards rushed to suppress them, and battle erupted in every direction.

**58** Yet above the noise of the conflict there came another sound.

**59** It was deeper than thunder and older than any machine.

**60** The Ballistic Titan ceased its movements.

**61** The Flesh Priests fell silent.

**62** Even the rebels stopped for a moment.

**63** For the sound came not from the Arena, nor from the city, but from somewhere beneath the foundations of Mars itself.

**64** Then the crimson-robed Priests bowed their heads.

**65** And some among them began to tremble.

**66** For they recognized the voice.

**67** And one whispered unto another, saying, "The Sleeper stirs."

**68** Another answered, "The Seal weakens."

**69** And a third said, "Carcazod is awakening."

**70** Thus the battle of the Arena began, and thus was revealed the second sign of Carcazod, whose shadow was spreading across Mars and whose purposes remained hidden from the children of Earth.
