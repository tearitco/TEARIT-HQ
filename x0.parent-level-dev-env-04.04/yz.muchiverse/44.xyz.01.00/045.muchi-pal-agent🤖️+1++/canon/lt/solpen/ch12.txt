# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 12

### The Clown Archives

**1** And it came to pass after the Queen had learned of the Shadow Pen and after the colony had begun to tremble with strange dreams, that Lucky, Astra, Mara, Cassian, and the Free Colonists sought knowledge in places where the rulers of Mars no longer looked.

**2** Therefore they followed rumors of hidden vaults beneath the old civic districts, where confiscated records, outlawed recordings, and broken testimonies were said to be kept by those who trafficked in secrets.

**3** And Cassian led them unto a place known among the Priests as the Laughing Archive, though in truth it was a warren of tunnels, storage chambers, and sealed rooms hidden behind a defunct performance hall.

**4** Now upon the entrance there hung painted smiles and cracked masks, and the symbols thereupon had been altered so many times that none could tell whether the building was in truth a shrine, a prison, or a joke left behind by a madman.

**5** Then Astra said, "I trust this place less than I trust a locked grenade."

**6** And Mara answered, "Then trust it exactly as much."

**7** Thereafter they entered.

**8** And within they found shelves of hardlight tablets, stolen registers, children’s drawings, black-market sermons, and fragments of code stripped from colonial servers.

**9** Yet the strangest things were the clown relics.

**10** For there were masks with false teeth, handheld projectors that cast laughing faces upon walls, and cylinders of preserved biological material labeled in a hand both elegant and cruel.

**11** Then Lucky asked, "Who maintaineth this place?"

**12** And Cassian answered, "Those who know too much to remain loyal and too little to remain safe."

**13** While they yet spoke, a voice came from the dark and said, "A fair description of every survivor on Mars."

**14** And there emerged an old archivist wearing a patched red coat and a grin too tired to be sincere.

**15** Then Mara raised her weapon, but the man lifted both hands and said, "If I meant you harm, ye would already be dead."

**16** Therefore he introduced himself as Jester Vale, keeper of the Laughing Archive.

**17** And he led them to a vault whose door was covered in warning sigils, crossed-out prayers, and the face of a clown drawn so many times over that the lines had cut into the metal.

**18** Then he said, "Ye seek Carcazod, and I tell you plainly that the archives are angry about it."

**19** And Astra replied, "The archives may take a number."

**20** Thereupon he unlocked the vault.

**21** And within were reels of old footage, preserved interviews, and damaged witness logs from the earliest Martian years.

**22** Then Lucky viewed the first recording.

**23** And he beheld scientists at the edge of a cavern, speaking in frightened voices concerning a living structure beneath the colony.

**24** Then the second recording showed colonists waking from shared dreams, all reporting the same image of a face made of clouds, blood, and static.

**25** The third recording showed a party of explorers returning changed, their words twisted, their laughter wrong, and their skin marked by small red sigils.

**26** And the fourth recording showed the first clown ritual ever recorded upon Mars.

**27** For a group of laborers and failed performers had painted themselves in mockery of the colony’s authority, yet while they mocked power they were overtaken by a vision no one could explain.

**28** Then one of them spoke in a voice not his own, saying, "The dream below is not dead."

**29** Another answered, "It remembers the shape of us."

**30** And a third began to laugh and weep at once.

**31** Then the recording ended in static.

**32** Therefore Lucky turned toward Jester Vale and said, "What connection have the clowns unto all this?"

**33** And Jester answered, "Once, they were only rebels, fools, performers, and criminals."

**34** "Then the dreams touched them."

**35** "Some resisted."

**36** "Some embraced what they found."

**37** "Some were broken and remade."

**38** "From that breaking came the first clown archives."

**39** Then Mara asked, "Wherefore keep these records hidden from both Queen and Priests?"

**40** And Jester said, "Because both know enough to be dangerous and not enough to be honest."

**41** After these things he opened another cabinet and withdrew a cylindrical vessel sealed in black glass.

**42** And within it floated a fragment of living tissue, neither fully human nor fully monstrous.

**43** Then Cassian recoiled.

**44** And Jester said, "A sample from a prior corruption event."

**45** "Not the first."

**46** "Only the first one we survived long enough to name."

**47** Then the archived witness logs showed the very words no ruler wished repeated.

**48** Carcazod.

**49** The Mothers.

**50** The First Nest.

**51** The Blood Banks.

**52** The Shadow Pen.

**53** And beneath all these names a single phrase kept recurring, scratched out and rewritten by frightened hands:

**54** "Do not wake the Dream."

**55** Then Lucky asked, "Why was this hidden?"

**56** And Jester Vale answered, "Because the first rulers tried to use what slept below Mars."

**57** "Because the Priests inherited the machinery and called it holy."

**58** "Because the clowns were blamed for the scream that followed."

**59** "And because every time the colony neareth the truth, somebody decides it is simpler to burn the evidence."

**60** Then Astra held up one of the logs and said, "This is not merely history."

**61** "This is a warning."

**62** And Jester nodded.

**63** Saying, "A warning, yes."

**64** "And also a map."

**65** Then he played one final recording.

**66** And in it appeared a deep chamber beneath the colony, a chamber older than the blood system and older than the first domes.

**67** There stood a cradle.

**68** And within the cradle slept a thing with a crown of cables, a face like folded stone, and eyes that opened for only a moment.

**69** Then the thing looked directly into the camera.

**70** And every speaker in the archive emitted one word at once:

**71** "MOTHER."

**72** Thereafter the image flickered.

**73** And beneath the word there appeared a coordinate set.

**74** Then Cassian said, "That is not one of the public sectors."

**75** And Jester answered, "No."

**76** "It is where the Queen forbade all travel."

**77** "It is where the true archives lie."

**78** "It is where your answers continue."

**79** Therefore Lucky took the coordinates.

**80** And with them came a burden greater than any he had yet borne.

**81** For the clowns had not merely been mocking a broken kingdom.

**82** They had been guarding the memory of its first lie.
