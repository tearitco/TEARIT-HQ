# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 17

### The Silence of Mars

**1** And it came to pass after the battle of the Arena and after the fall of the lower wards, that Mars entered a silence stranger than any fear.

**2** For the public channels failed in many places.

**3** The market screens went blank.

**4** The transport hymns ceased.

**5** And across great portions of New Antioch there arose no music, no commerce, and no public proclamation, only the sound of distant vents and the uneasy beating of hidden reactors.

**6** Then those who had survived the first corruption hid behind locked doors and asked one another whether the colony still belonged to human hands.

**7** Yet the answers were no comfort.

**8** For the rulers had withdrawn.

**9** The Priests had split into factions.

**10** The rebels had gone underground.

**11** And the Mother’s chamber remained sealed behind layers of emergency protocols.

**12** Thereafter Lucky and his company moved through the lower engine corridors, guided by the Soul Pen and by the Mother’s fragmentary warnings.

**13** And they discovered that the old support systems of Mars were no longer obeying their intended masters.

**14** Some gates opened to them.

**15** Others refused.

**16** Some halls repeated the same footsteps no matter how they advanced.

**17** And some walls appeared in places where no wall had stood before.

**18** Then Cassian said, "The colony dreameth while awake."

**19** And Astra answered, "Then let us wake it properly or burn it in the attempt."

**20** Yet before they could proceed further, the Mother in Lucky’s care fell silent.

**21** And all the lights in her portable chamber dimmed.

**22** Then she whispered, "It hath gone deeper."

**23** "It knoweth our approach."

**24** Therefore they hastened onward.

**25** And in a chamber beneath the oldest heart of the city, they found records indicating that the original seal on Carcazod had been maintained not by steel alone but by repeated acts of memory, ritual, and biological management.

**26** Thus the colony had not merely housed the Dream.

**27** It had fed it, restrained it, and mistaken its containment for prosperity.

**28** Then Lucky asked, "What shall break it?"

**29** And the Mother answered, "One part fear, one part hunger, and one part invitation."

**30** Then the floor beneath them trembled.

**31** And from the dark beyond the archive came a voice that sounded like many mouths speaking in agreement.

**32** And the voice said, "The bridge returns."

**33** Therefore Cassian fell back.

**34** And Mara drew her weapon.

**35** But Lucky stood still, for the Soul Pen had begun to write of its own accord upon the air.

**36** Then symbols assembled themselves into a warning:

**37** "DO NOT COMPLETE THE CIRCLE."

**38** And beneath it another line appeared:

**39** "TO SEE IS TO BE SEEN."

**40** Then the chamber opened.

**41** And within the blackness beyond there moved a city-sized shape in the slow manner of a sleeper turning in bed.

**42** Yet no one there was given the mercy of seeing it whole.

**43** For the lights failed before the vision could complete.

**44** And the silence that followed was not absence, but listening.
