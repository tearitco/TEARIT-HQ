# place_and_move.txt - three ways to place a thing, then drive a pet and give it an event. Needs the editor window open.
# Run: python3 &.widgits/_shared-lib/presentation/present_harness.py &.widgits/_shared-lib/presentation/scenarios/place_and_move.txt
book | rpg-edit-hq
clockto | 12
verb | phase day
wait | 6
expect | state/daylight.txt | day
shot | start | We start in the editor's own book, in the Store room. Nothing has been placed yet.
# way 1: arm a palette tile, click a cell with a real mouse click, press Enter
armtile | cdda | pngs_normal_32x32 | furniture | 265
shot | armed | Way one. We arm a palette tile: the CDDA fire barrel. The palettes bar says armed.
clickplace | 560 | 500
expect | state/voxel_log.txt | placed 30gal_firebarr as #1
shot | placed1 | We click a floor cell with the mouse, then press Enter. The barrel is placed exactly where we clicked, and the voxel log says placed.
# way 2: the place verb moves a placed item by its number, no mouse
verb | place 1 5 9
expect | state/house.txt | MOVE\p1\p
expect | state/house.txt | \p5\p9
shot | placed2 | Way two. The place verb takes the barrel's number and a new x and y, and moves it to nine, nine. No mouse at all.
# way 3: drive the controlled character to another room (a visible move), then give it an event that brings it back by itself
mark | before_drive
verb | goto bank
wait | 4
moved | before_drive
shot | moved | Way three is movement. We drive the character: the goto verb sends it to the Bank, and the position changed.
mark | before_event
event | demo_automove | ui
wait | 4
moved | before_event
shot | auto | Now we give it an event. The auto move page sends the character back to the Store by itself. Same spine, same events.
