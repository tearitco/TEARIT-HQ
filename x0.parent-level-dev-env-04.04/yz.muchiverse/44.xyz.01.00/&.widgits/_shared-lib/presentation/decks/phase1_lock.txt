TITLE | Phase one: lock what we built | Harness cases and roll-outs, about one day | Phase one is about locking in what we have already built, so it cannot quietly break.
SLIDE | Why now | Today's fixes were found by driving the real window, and several have no harness case; The dropdown thumb, click to place and lamps could regress unnoticed; The editor-only fixes have not reached the other games | Several things we fixed today were found by driving the real window, and none of them has a harness case yet. The dropdown thumb, click to place and lamps could regress without anyone noticing, and the other games do not have those fixes.
SLIDE | What we build | Harness case: the book dropdown shows no thumb through its rows; Harness case: arm a tile, click, press enter, the item lands on the clicked cell; Harness case: lamps on at dusk, off at dawn, brightness measured; Harness case: save and load restores the clock on every skin; Roll the click placer and lamp system into the other games | We add a harness case for each fix. The dropdown must not show a thumb through its rows. Click to place must land on the clicked cell. Lamps must come on at dusk and go off at dawn. Save and load must restore the clock on every skin. Then we roll the placer and lamp system out to the other games.
SLIDE | How we prove it | Each case has an expectation that can fail, tested once with a deliberate wrong value; Each run restores the desk and reports OK or FAIL with its step log; Narrated video of the run is saved next to the report | Each case checks something that can actually fail, and we test that once with a deliberate wrong value. Every run puts the desk back and writes an OK or FAIL report, plus a narrated video.
SLIDE | Risks | Engine sessions keep their own binary copies, so a rebuild needs a relaunch; The machine runs hot, so the gate waits and runs take longer; Other games may differ in layout, so a skin may need small changes | The risks are small. Engine sessions keep their own copies, so a rebuild needs a relaunch. The machine runs hot, so the gate sometimes waits. And a few games may need small layout changes.
SLIDE | Done means | Every fix from today has a passing case and a negative test; Eleven games rebuilt and the fix verified in each binary; Report in the book and a copy in the nov-4 folder | Phase one is done when every fix has a passing case and a negative test, all eleven games are rebuilt with the fixes verified, and the report is in the book and in this folder.
