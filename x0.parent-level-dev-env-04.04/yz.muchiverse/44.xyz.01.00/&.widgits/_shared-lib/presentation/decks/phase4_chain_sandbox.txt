TITLE | Phase four: a local blockchain sandbox | Testnet only, no real value, about one week | Phase four is an experiment with a local blockchain, with no real money involved.
SLIDE | Why now | The pal chain v1 already mines, signs and escrows; The cones chain will get its own virtual machine; We have not measured what contracts, gas and auctions feel like | The pal chain already mines blocks, signs transactions and holds escrow, and the cones chain will get its own virtual machine. We have not yet measured what contracts, gas and auctions feel like in practice.
SLIDE | What we build | A local development chain with no monetary value; Three small contracts: a token, an auction, and a fee and tribute splitter; A bridge that mirrors pal chain events to the sandbox; The pal chain stays the source of truth | We run a local development chain and deploy three small contracts: a token, an auction, and a splitter that takes the fee and tribute. A bridge copies pal chain events into the sandbox, and the pal chain stays the source of truth.
SLIDE | How we prove it | A scripted auction: list, bid, settle; balances checked after each step; A conservation check: the total of all balances never changes by itself; The same scenario replayed twice gives the same result | We prove it with a scripted auction: list an item, bid, and settle, checking balances at each step. A conservation check must show that balances never change by themselves, and replaying the scenario must give the same result.
SLIDE | Risks | A token that people buy expecting profit can count as a security in many places; Wallet keys must never be committed or shared; Autonomous sellers can wash trade without limits | The main risk is legal. A token bought for profit can be treated as a security. So we stay on a test network with no value until you have had a legal consult. Keys never go into git, and autonomous sellers get rate limits.
SLIDE | Done means | A recorded auction run on the local chain, with checks passing; A short report: what gas, cost and delays looked like; Your decision on what the cones chain virtual machine should copy or avoid | Phase four is done when we have a recorded auction run with passing checks, a short report on cost and delays, and your decision on what the cones chain virtual machine should copy or avoid.
