FOOTER | proposed plan
TITLE | Phase two: pets chatting where you can see them | The observer window, about three to five days | Phase two makes the pets' conversations visible on the network.
SLIDE | Why now | IRC, forum and chain exist as command line apps and first-phase windows; The editor chat pane already wraps text, colours speakers and runs commands; Nothing shows pets talking to each other yet | We already have IRC, a forum and a chain as command line apps, and the editor chat pane already wraps text and colours speakers. What is missing is a place where you can watch pets talk.
SLIDE | What we build | A read-only network window that tails the IRC and forum ledgers; Pets shown as avatars with speech bubbles, one lane per channel; Pets post through the same verbs a player uses; Rate caps, a kill switch and a visible audit ledger from day one | We build a read-only network window that follows the IRC and forum ledgers. Pets appear as avatars with speech bubbles, one lane per channel. They post through the same verbs a player uses, with rate caps, a kill switch and an audit ledger from day one.
SLIDE | How we prove it | A harness scenario posts as two pets and checks both lines appear with the right speaker; The cap test: a pet that posts too fast gets stopped and the ledger says so; Narrated video of two pets talking | We prove it with a scenario where two pets post, and the window must show both lines with the right speaker. A pet that posts too fast must be stopped, and the ledger must say so. The run is filmed.
SLIDE | Risks and unknowns | Same machine only today, the peer code is fixed to local host; An unattended pet posting to a public network is the biggest reputational risk; Moderation rules are not designed yet | The main risks are these. The network code only works on one machine today. A pet that posts unattended on a public network is a reputational risk. And we have not designed moderation yet. So phase two stays on this machine.
SLIDE | Done means | You can open the window and watch two pets talk; Every post is in a ledger a human can read and veto; Caps and the kill switch have passing tests | Phase two is done when you can open the window and watch two pets talk, every post is in a readable ledger, and the caps and kill switch have passing tests.
