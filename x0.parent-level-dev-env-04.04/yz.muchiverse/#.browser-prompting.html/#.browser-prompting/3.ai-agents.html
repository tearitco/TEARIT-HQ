Real, standing architectural risk — confirmed live

khtpm_open_hai_manager.c and khtpm_open_hai_render.c each keep their OWN SEPARATE copy of the model list (g_models[]) — by design per the shell/manager split, not an oversight. Any model-list change must be made in BOTH files by hand or the render side silently falls back to its own default.
