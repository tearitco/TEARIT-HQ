<module>${module_path}</module>. After: 2 new files, byte-identical
content to pal/main_loop_chtpm.pal, one per screen:
    pal/game_module.pal
    pal/info_test_module.pal
game.chtpm's <module> line now reads
    <module>system/prisc+x pal/game_module.pal</module>
info_test.chtpm's now reads
    <module>system/prisc+x pal/info_test_module.pal</module>

clear_saved_active_index() (PITFALL 66) ported into this project's own
local system/chtpm_parser_pal.c, inserted after build_path_malloc(),
called at 6 real transition sites (lines 1766, 1793, 1883, 3059, 3326,
3361 after insertion - found via the same `focus_index = 0;` +
following-parse_chtm() grep pattern used across this whole rollout).
Debug-spam printf lines were already absent before this work.

Applied identically (verified byte-for-byte identical chtpm_parser_
pal.c between the two before AND after this edit) to this project's
own sibling copy, 300.rtp-xyz.
