    remote trainer may keep running. Before/after any train run, check and
    kill leftovers so we never leave orphan procs on the Mac:
      ssh ... "pgrep -fl 'tomom-qroq' || echo clean"
      ssh ... "pkill -f tomom-qroq && echo killed"   (no matching procs = no-op, safe)
