B.O.T.S. (Battle of the Sovereigns)
Theme: A blood-based sci-fi economy set on Mars involving biotech, vampires, and corporate law firms in a "Wild West" setting.

Game Setup
- Board Size: 8x8 Square Grid.
- Total Pieces: 32 (16 per player).
- Starting Resources: 
    - Money/Blood: 100 credits.
    - Bullets: 0 (must be purchased or produced).
    - Stocks: 0 (purchasable at the Market).
- Piece Placement: Players set up on the two rows closest to them (Rows 0-1 and Rows 6-7).

Pieces (Per Player)
1. King: High-health leader with a powerful melee attack.
2. Queen: High-priority piece. Can "Convert" enemy units to your side.
3. Nurse: Support units that can heal or revive dead pieces.
4. Pistol Gunner: Standard ranged units. (Consumes 1 Bullet).
5. Auto Gunner: Rapid-fire unit. (Consumes Bullets).
6. Sniper: Long-range specialist. (Consumes 1 Bullet).
7. Grenadier: Area-of-effect attacker. (Consumes 1 Bullet).
8. Engineer: Can build/fix Robots and "Work" at Factories to produce bullets.
9. Artist: Support unit capable of converting enemies.
10. Lawyer: Uses "Litigation" to revive or handle legalities of dead units.
11. Archer: Ranged attacker (2-space range).
12. Robot: Powerful mechanical unit; if damaged, must be repaired by an Engineer.
13. Factory: Stationary structures (built during play) used to produce bullets.

Core Rules
- Turn Structure: Each player gets 3 Actions per turn.
- Actions: Moving, Attacking, Buying Items, or using a Special Skill (Heal, Litigate, Work) each count as 1 action.
- The Blood Tax: Every unit requires "blood" to survive. If your bank hits zero and you cannot pay the tax, you lose immediately.
- The Market: Players can spend an action to check the current price of Stocks or Bullets. Buying low and selling high is a key strategy for maintaining your "Blood" bank.
- Combat: Most attacks have a range and a success probability. Some units (Gunners) require Bullets to attack; others use "Barehanded" attacks as a last resort.

Win State
- Assassination: Capture/Kill the enemy Queen.
- Bankruptcy: Deplete the enemy's money until they can no longer pay their "Blood Tax."
