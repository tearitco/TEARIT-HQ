# 🥷 FOOTRACE FU / TIME FU  
*Official Rulesheet v0.1 (Collaborative Draft)*

## 🎯 Game Overview
- **Players**: 2–4 (expandable)
- **Objective (Standard Race)**: Be the first player to move **any one ninja** from Start to Finish.
- **Secondary Objective (Buffet Variant)**: Be the last ninja standing while managing economy, healing, and territory.
- **Core Loop**: Roll → Move → Act → Resolve → Pass.

---

## 🧰 Components (Suggested)
| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Ninja Cards** (10+ unique) | Track name, class, HP, abilities, cooldowns |
| **Action Tokens** (3 per player) | Limit actions per turn (prevents spam) |
| **HP Trackers** | Paper, beads, or dice rotated to show health |
| **2d6 + 1d4** | Movement, combat, magic resolution |
| **Coin** | 50/50 fate checks (teleport safety, tiebreakers) |
| **Board Options** | Grid (10×10), Chutes & Ladders track, or *Time-Fu: Buffet* board |
| **Food Tokens** (Buffet only) | Healing currency earned via property control |

---

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics

### 🔄 Turn Structure & Actions
- **Standard**: Each player gets **1 turn per round**.  
- **Actions per turn**: **1–3 Action Tokens** (recommend starting with **2** for balance).  
- **Each action can be ONE of**:
  1. **Move** (roll 1d6, move that many spaces)  
  2. **Cast Ability** (spend token + meet cooldown)  
  3. **Attack** (melee or ranged)  
  4. **Block/Defend** (prepare for incoming attack)  
  5. **Heal** (Buffet only: spend Food token)  

> 🔍 *Question for you*: Should moving always cost an action, or should every ninja get 1 "free step" per turn to keep the race feeling fast?

### ⚔️ Combat Resolution (Mathematically Fair)
- **Trigger**: When a ninja enters a space occupied by an enemy (melee) OR uses a ranged ability within line-of-sight.
- **Roll-off**: Attacker rolls 1d6 + Attack Bonus; Defender rolls 1d6 + Block Bonus.
  - **Higher roll wins**: Loser loses 1 HP.
  - **Tie**: Both lose 1 HP (clash!) OR flip a coin for who blinks first.
- **Ranged/Magic**: Requires line-of-sight; may ignore terrain but cost an Action Token.
- **Death**: At 0 HP, ninja is **eliminated** (Standard) or **returns to last Buffet owned** (Buffet variant).

> 🎲 *Probability Note*: Using 1d6 vs 1d6 gives ~16.7% tie rate. Adding small static bonuses (+1 for class) keeps outcomes swingy but not random. Want to test this with a quick sim?

### 🌀 Magic & Teleport Rules (Fair & Fun)
| Ability | Cost | Effect | Fairness Guardrail |
|---------|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Time Skip** | 1 Token + roll doubles | Move forward 1d4 spaces | Max 2 uses per race |
| **Warp Ally** | 1 Token + coin flip | Teleport friendly ninja up to 3 spaces | Cannot teleport past Finish |
| **Banish Foe** | 2 Tokens + roll double 6s | Send enemy to Start OR (coin flip) to Finish* | *If sent to Finish, they win immediately—high risk/high reward! |
| **Chrono Shield** | 1 Token | +2 Block for one combat | Cooldown: 2 turns |

> ⚠️ *Design Check*: Should teleporting an enemy to the Finish be a possible "oops I helped them win" moment, or should we cap it at "send to Start only" for safety?

## 🏁 Winning Conditions
- **Standard Race**: First ninja to **land exactly on Finish** wins. Overshooting? Bounce back or lose turn (your choice).
- **Army Brawl**: Last player with any ninja alive wins.
- **Buffet Variant**: Last ninja standing + most Food tokens = winner (tiebreaker: who controls most Buffets).

---

## 🔁 Variants & Scaling ("The Toys")
| Variant | Setup | Win Condition | Best For |
|---------|-------|---------------|----------|
| **Duel Mode** | 1v1, 3 ninjas each | First to Finish OR eliminate all | Quick play, strategy focus |
| **Squad Race** | 2–4 players, 5 ninjas each | Standard Race | Balanced chaos |
| **Epic Buffet** | 2–4 players, 4 ninjas each + economy | Last standing + economy | Long sessions, negotiation |
| **Blitz** | Any, 2 ninjas each, 5x5 board | First to Finish in 10 rounds | Fast, high-variance fun |

> 📏 *Fairness Standard Recommendation*:  
> - Max starting HP per army: 20 total  
> - Max Action Tokens per turn: 2 (Standard), 3 (Epic)  
> - Teleport abilities: max 2 uses per ninja per game  
> This keeps probability curves tight and prevents runaway leaders.

## 🍱 APPENDIX A: Time-Fu: Buffet (Monopoly-Style Variant)

### 🎲 Core Twist
- Board is a loop of **24 themed properties** (see below).  
- Players **buy "Buffets"** (properties) using **Time Coins** earned by landing on unowned spaces or winning combats.  
- Owning a Buffet lets you **collect Food** when opponents land on it.  
- **Food** = healing currency (1 Food = restore 1 HP to any ninja on a Buffet you own).

### 🗺️ Buffet Board Spaces (Named & Themed)
1. Start: **Gateway of Moments**  
2. **Dumpling Dojo**

11. **Udon Uplift** (Ladder: climb to #19)  
12. **Mochi Maze**  
13. **Yakitori Yard**  
14. **Edamame Expanse**  
15. **Teriyaki Terminal**  
16. **Gyoza Grove**  
17. **Onigiri Outpost**  
18. **Soba Summit**  
19. **Tofu Temple**  
20. **Katsu Keep**  
21. **Panko Pass**  
22. **Mirin Marsh** (Chute: to #8)  
23. **Dashi Delta**  
24. Finish: **Feast of Eternity**

### 💰 Economy Rules (Simplified Monopoly)
- **Buy**: Land on unowned Buffet → pay listed price → collect Food when others land there.  
- **Rent**: Opponent lands on your Buffet → pays you 1 Food OR loses 1 HP (their choice).  
- **Heal**: Spend 1 Food on your turn to heal any ninja on a Buffet you control.  
- **Bankruptcy**: If a ninja dies on a space you don't own and you have 0 Food, they're out.  
- **Win**: Last player with any ninja alive AND at least 1 Buffet controlled.

> 🤔 *Your vision check*: Should players be able to trade Buffets/Food mid-game? Or keep it competitive-only?
