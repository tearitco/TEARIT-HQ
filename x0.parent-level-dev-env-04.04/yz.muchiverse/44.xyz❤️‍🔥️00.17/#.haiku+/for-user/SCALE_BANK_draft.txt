SCALE_BANK — Entity-Domain-Fidelity Configuration Layer
(Section 4.6 of zest-er-summary.txt — prep work, not yet built)

================================================================================
CORE CONCEPT
================================================================================
Scale is PER-ENTITY, PER-DOMAIN, not one global dial. A car in a driving game
might need deep "large scale" simulation of its physics domain (tires, gas,
engine mechanics, combustion) while needing almost nothing for "conversation"
domain (cars don't talk). An NPC might need large-scale fidelity for
driving-knowledge but only small-scale cheap fidelity for conversation.

Without SCALE_BANK, a project either (a) hardcodes one fixed fidelity level
for everything (wasteful/inflexible), or (b) builds one-off fidelity switches
per feature (not reusable). SCALE_BANK makes "how much detail does THIS need"
a first-class, swappable, reusable config decision.

================================================================================
DRAFT SCHEMA
================================================================================
Plain pipe-delimited registry file (same format as model_list.txt, npc_bank.txt,
terrain_registry.txt):

  entity_type | domain | scale_level | module_id
  car | physics | large | tire_engine_combustion_full_sim
  car | physics | small | simple_speed_accel_abstraction
  car | conversation | small | none
  npc | driving_knowledge | large | full_driving_rules_module
  npc | driving_knowledge | small | basic_speed_direction_only
  npc | conversation | small | random_words_or_light_fsm
  npc | conversation | large | muchi_pal_agent_instance
  employee | job_skills | large | full_job_training_module
  employee | biology | small | none
  animal | biology | large | full_animal_lifecycle_sim
  animal | conversation | small | none

Keys:
  - entity_type: what kind of thing (car, npc, employee, animal, etc)
  - domain: which subsystem/behavior (physics, driving_knowledge, conversation, job_skills, biology, etc)
  - scale_level: fidelity tier (large, small — 2 tiers for now, add more if needed)
  - module_id: which actual generator/behavior module implements THIS combo (op, script/fsm/rl entry, wordbank, etc)

Per-project/game config file then picks ACTIVE scale_level for each (entity_type, domain) pair:

  simple_cheap_racing_game.config:
    car | physics | small
    car | conversation | small
    npc | driving_knowledge | small
    npc | conversation | small

  hardcore_sim_racer.config:
    car | physics | large
    car | conversation | small
    npc | driving_knowledge | large
    npc | conversation | large

Same SCALE_BANK rows, different active selections per game/simulation.

    corpus | coding | small | simple_function_stubs.txt

  WSR (future — humans/employees/customers, organisms/animals/plants/microbes):
    (same bank shape, totally different content per domain)

  GAME LEVELING/SCALE (section 4.5):
    (terrain generators at different granularities, evolution rules, etc)

The pattern is identical everywhere. Only the domain content changes.

================================================================================
BUILD ORDER (Design-Doc-First, Pilot First)
================================================================================
DO NOT build SCALE_BANK as a generic cross-project system yet. Prove ONE
concrete case end-to-end first:

  1. DESIGN: Write SCALE_BANK-standard.txt (design doc, no code)
     - Why we need it
     - How it slots into existing registry+switch pattern
     - Relationship to model_list.txt, npc_bank.txt, etc
     - Schema and key structure (refine the draft above)

  2. PILOT: Pick ONE real entity type with genuine two-tier need
     - Candidate: mutaclsym's AI (section 4.4 work) — cars or NPCs have both large/small fidelity needs
     - Write SCALE_BANK rows ONLY for that entity type
     - Wire ONE config file to pick between large/small
     - Verify both tiers actually work end-to-end

  3. GENERALIZE: Once pilot is proven, roll out to other projects
     - Apply to other entity types (more NPCs, animals, employees, etc)
     - Extend to other domains (not just AI, but physics, biology, etc)
     - Turn per-project configs into a shared pattern
