🐛 IQABOD ROOT-CAUSE BUG — FOUND + FIXED + PROVEN
  - The bug: training loop fed the whole corpus position into the KV cache
    with NO seq_len guard (generation caps at 32, training didn't). Any
    corpus >32 tokens = heap corruption = "free(): invalid pointer" crash.
  - That's why iqabod had only ever learned ≤32-token toy corpora AND why
    loss always plateaued at the random floor. Real conversations = crash.
  - FIX: sliding-window training — pos = i % seq_len + zero KV cache at
    window boundary (generation_module.c, one targeted change).
  - Recompiled on the Linux node → the 80-token conv-g1 corpus now trains
    clean, no crash.

⚙️ SECOND BOTTLENECK — LEARNING RATE (also fixed)
  - Default lr=1e-5 sat on the random floor (4.38 = ln 80). Nothing learned.
  - Store config.txt now: learning_rate=0.1, epochs=100 → loss fell
    4.38 → 0.0002. iqabod genuinely learns now. 🎉
