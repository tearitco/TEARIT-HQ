INT. MARTIAN VESSEL - CONTINUOUS

SOUND of heavy breathing, the hum of life support, the crackle of static

The flag-ship has turned back, lurking in the shadow of a Martian moon. The mood inside is grim. The BLACK QUEEN sits on her temporary command chair, her exo-suit damaged.

LUCKY and ASTRA stand before her. GEM is at a medical station, holding a vial of glowing, iridescent CARCAZOD BLOOD.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> The clowns have a bio-scan at the barrier’s edge. If we’re not 'infected', we don't pass.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> I will not turn my people into monsters just to chase them.

<center>GEM</center>
> (stepping forward)
> I'll do it.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Gem, no. That stuff rewrites your soul.

<center>GEM</center>
> I'm a SOL agent. I was built for this. If I inject a controlled dose, I can trick the sensors into thinking this is a hijacked clown ship. We can pass the blockade and get to Earth to warn them.

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (her dark expressive eyes wide)
> It will attack your heart, Gem. The PFCs in your Type C blood will react with the Carcazod entropy.

<center>GEM</center>
> Then Lucky will have to use the SolPen to fix me.

She looks Lucky directly in the eye.

<center>GEM</center>
> It’s the only way.

Gem takes a pneumatic injector and presses it to her neck. She gasps as the glowing blood enters her system. Her veins immediately begin to darken, a web of shadow spreading across her caramel-toned skin.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> (to the pilot)
> Heading back to the barrier. Full speed.

FADE OUT.
