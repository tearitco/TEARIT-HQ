INT. MARTIAN COLONY - RED QUEEN'S CASTLE / ARENA - DAY

SOUND of a cheering crowd, the rhythmic squeak of sneakers on a high-tech floor, energy pulses

Lucky, Astra, and Gem are guests of the BLACK QUEEN and PRINCESS MEGAN8R. The Queen, tall and imposing in her ornate dark exo-suit, watches from a royal box.

LUCKY stands beside her, his platinum blonde high-top fade glinting under the arena lights. ASTRA, her asymmetrical hair full of colorful beads and her plushie keychains jingling, leans over the railing, fascinated.

The arena is a massive, pressurized court. On the floor, a high-stakes BASKETBALL GAME is underway.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> Mars needs order, Lucky. And Mars needs entertainment.

On the court: a team of AFRICANS—stunning athletes imported from Earth to manage the clone labor force—play against a team of CLONES. The athletes move with superhuman speed, their reflexes enhanced by Type C blood.

Suddenly, the game shifts. The clones are replaced by sleek, bipedal ROBOTS. The pace intensifies. The Africans pivot and jump with gravity-defying flair, keeping the lead.

Finally, a HUGE BALL-SHOOTING BOSS ROBOT rolls onto the court. It’s a literal tank with dozens of pneumatic arms, firing balls with lethal velocity. The athletes dodge and counter-dunk in a display of breathtaking skill.

The buzzer sounds. The athletes win. The crowd erupts.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> (smiling coldly)
> And now, for the main event.

The court floor retracts, revealing a series of tracks. The boss robot rapidly constructs a structure: THE LONG WINDED GOODBYE. It’s a high-speed execution roller coaster.

Several captive CLOWNS, their faces painted in mocking grins, are strapped into the cars. They scream—not in fear, but with chaotic laughter.

The coaster launches. The cars hurtle through a loop and dive directly into a FLAMING SPIKE BOX. A flash of light, a puff of smoke, and the clowns are gone.

Lucky watches, his mixed heritage features tight with conflict.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Efficient.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> Necessary. The clowns are a cancer. They want chaos; we give them a spectacular end.

The Queen gestures toward a grand hallway.

<center>BLACK QUEEN</center>
> Now, to the after-party. We have much to discuss regarding the Carcazod threat.

Astra catches Lucky's eye, her dark expressive eyes full of unease. They follow the Queen into a lavish ballroom filled with the Martian elite, drinking vintage Type C blood from crystal flutes.

FADE OUT.
