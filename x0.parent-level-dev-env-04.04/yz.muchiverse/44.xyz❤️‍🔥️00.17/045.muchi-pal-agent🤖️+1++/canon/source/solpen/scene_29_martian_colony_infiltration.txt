EXT. SPACE - MARS ORBIT

SOUND of sirens echoing through the hull, the muffled boom of energy cannons, the hum of the orbital barrier

The space battle reaches its peak. LUCKY’S interceptor banks hard, dodging a cluster of mines launched from a hijacked freighter. Ahead, the orbital barrier pulses with a sickly green light.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (Into comms)
> The barrier's being manually overridden!

The clown fleet—dozens of mismatched, spiked ships—reaches the energy mesh. Instead of crashing, the barrier PARTITIONS, allowing the clown vessels to pass through unharmed.

The BLACK QUEEN’S flag-ship, following close behind, prepares to cross. But the barrier snaps shut, the energy lines solidifying into a impenetrable wall.

<center>BLACK QUEEN (V.O.)</center>
> (Furious)
> Open the barrier! I am your Queen!

<center>CLOWN REBEL (V.O.)</center>
> (Laughing over the channel)
> Long live the chaos! The Queen stays on Mars while the circus goes to Earth!

Lucky watches the clown fleet accelerate away, heading for the deep void toward Earth.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (to himself)
> They've taken the entire defense system.

<center>GEM (V.O.)</center>
> Lucky, the barrier's feedback loop is draining our shields! We have to turn back or we'll be vaporized!

<center>LUCKY</center>
> We can't let them go. We have to find another way through.

FADE OUT.
