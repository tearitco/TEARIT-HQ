INT. QUEEN'S TRANSPORT SHIP - SPACE

SOUND of a high-pitched bio-scanner ping, the tense silence of a crew holding its breath

The transport ship approaches the orbital barrier. Ahead, two massive clown frigates—covered in jagged armor and neon graffiti—flank the energy mesh.

LUCKY stands behind the pilot’s chair, his platinum blonde high-top fade illuminated by the red emergency lights. ASTRA, her asymmetrical hair full of beads, clutches a plushie keychain of a three-eyed alien for comfort.

<center>CLOWN REBEL (V.O.)</center>
> (Through comms)
> Transport 409, state your purpose. Why are you lagging behind?

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (Into comms, disguised voice)
> We had a containment leak. The Queen’s guards are dead. We’re bringing the rest of the samples to Earth.

<center>CLOWN REBEL (V.O.)</center>
> Stand by for bio-scan.

A green laser sweeps the ship. At the medical station, GEM lies on a table, her skin a network of dark, pulsing veins. Her dark expressive eyes are wide with pain, but she remains silent.

The scan PINGS.

<center>CLOWN REBEL (V.O.)</center>
> Positive for Carcazod signature. You’re one of us. Barrier opening. Get to Earth and start the carnival.

The energy mesh PARTITIONS. The transport ship glides through, accelerating into the void between Mars and Earth.

Lucky exhales, his muscular frame finally relaxing.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> We're through.

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (Running to Gem)
> Lucky, she’s crashing! The infection is spreading too fast!

<center>LUCKY</center>
> (To the pilot)
> Get us as far from Mars as possible. I have work to do.

FADE OUT.
