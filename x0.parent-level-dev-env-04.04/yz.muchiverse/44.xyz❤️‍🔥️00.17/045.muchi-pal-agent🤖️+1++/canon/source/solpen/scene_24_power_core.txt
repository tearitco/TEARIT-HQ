INT. MARTIAN COLONY - POWER CORE - DAY

SOUND of a deep, resonant hum, the crackling of energy arcs, the skittering of thousands of small legs

Lucky and Astra stand on a catwalk overlooking the pulsing heart of the colony. LUCKY, his platinum blonde high-top fade vibrant in the reactor’s glow, adjusts his gold chains. ASTRA, her asymmetrical hair full of colorful clips, grips her energy weapon tightly.

Below them, a group of CLOWN REBELS are attempting to sabotage the cooling conduits.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> Halt! You're overloading the system!

The clowns look up, their painted faces splitting into wider, impossible grins. One of them, a lanky rebel in spiked leather, laughs.

<center>CLOWN REBEL</center>
> You can't catch what you can't see!

Suddenly, the rebel’s body SHUDDERS and collapses inward. His skin folds and hardens, his limbs shrinking into multiple jointed legs. In a blink, the human is gone, replaced by a ROACH the size of a cat—but with a tiny, human-like clown face on its thorax.

The other clowns follow suit, a grotesque transformation echoing through the chamber.

<center>ASTRA</center>
> (horrified)
> It's a new mutation! They're shifting biological states!

The ROACH-CLOWNS swarm a fallen technician, their tiny clown mouths tearing into the body. As they consume the flesh, they rapidly grow, their forms expanding and straightening back into human shapes.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> They use biomass as a catalyst for the shift.

One of the clowns, now fully human again and wiped clean of blood, points a jagged energy blade at Lucky.

<center>CLOWN REBEL</center>
> We are the many! We are the swarm!

Lucky activates his SolPen, creating a rapidly spinning energy barrier to intercept the charging roach-clowns.

<center>LUCKY</center>
> We need to seal the core before they turn the whole city into a nest!

FADE OUT.
