# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 1

### The City Beneath the Dust

**1** And it came to pass in the latter days, when the nations of the Earth were troubled by wars and rumors of wars, that there stood a city in the desert beyond the mountains of Afghanistan, hidden among ancient roads and forgotten empires.

**2** For the city was old, and many kingdoms had risen and fallen around it, yet it endured; and merchants passed through its gates, and soldiers marched through its streets, and men spoke of peace while preparing for conflict.

**3** In those days there dwelt a man called Lucky, who had seen much of war and trusted few men, for he had witnessed the promises of rulers and the failures of governments, and his heart had become cautious.

**4** Yet though he carried weapons and walked among dangerous places, there remained within him a desire to do what was right, even when he could not clearly discern the path before him.

**5** And there was with him a woman called Astra, whose spirit was fierce and whose courage failed not in the face of danger, and many who underestimated her soon regretted their folly.

**6** And Astra was skilled in matters both seen and unseen, and often she perceived patterns which others overlooked, and dangers which had not yet revealed themselves.

**7** Now there was unrest throughout the region, and many factions sought power for themselves, and alliances were formed in secret and broken in secret, and the common people suffered while greater forces struggled for dominion.

**8** Therefore Lucky and Astra journeyed toward the desert city, having received word that a transaction of unusual importance was soon to take place there.

**9** And the matter concerned a weapon whose destructive power was beyond that of ordinary arms, and many sought either to possess it or to prevent its use.

**10** Thus they entered the city under the cover of evening, while dust blew through the streets and the merchants prepared to close their stalls.

**11** And the city was crowded with travelers, traders, soldiers, refugees, and men whose purposes were concealed behind friendly words.

**12** Yet among all who walked its streets there moved others whose presence was scarcely noticed, for spies and informants watched continually, gathering knowledge for masters both near and far.

**13** And there was a warrior named Kaito, who commanded many loyal men, and his reputation was known throughout the region.

**14** For some regarded him as a protector, while others regarded him as a criminal; yet all agreed that he was dangerous when opposed.

**15** And Kaito had also come to the city, for he had learned of the weapon and desired to secure it before it fell into the hands of his enemies.

**16** Thus many paths converged upon the same place, though few understood how their destinies were about to become intertwined.

**17** Now as night fell, Lucky met with certain informants in a marketplace near the western district, and they spoke quietly concerning shipments, routes, and guarded compounds.

**18** And one of the informants warned him, saying, "There are forces at work here greater than the ambitions of warlords and greater than the schemes of governments."

**19** But Lucky answered, "Many men speak in riddles when they possess no facts."

**20** And the informant replied, "Then may God preserve thee when the facts arrive."

**21** After these things Astra observed strange lights moving among the mountains beyond the city.

**22** At first she supposed them to be aircraft.

**23** Yet their movements were unlike any aircraft known unto her, for they changed direction suddenly and moved without visible engines.

**24** Therefore she reported the matter unto Lucky.

**25** But neither of them understood the meaning of what they had seen.

**26** And in the days that followed, rumors spread among the inhabitants of the city.

**27** Some spoke of secret weapons.

**28** Others spoke of hidden armies.

**29** Others still spoke of visitors from the heavens.

**30** Yet most dismissed such reports, for men often reject the truth when it first appears among them.

**31** Then came the appointed day.

**32** The buyers arrived.

**33** The sellers arrived.

**34** Armed men occupied rooftops and alleyways throughout the district.

**35** And Kaito's soldiers took their positions.

**36** While hidden observers watched from afar.

**37** And the atmosphere of the city became tense, like the stillness before a storm.

**38** Then the exchange began.

**39** And cases were opened.

**40** And codes were verified.

**41** And payments were confirmed.

**42** Yet even as these things were taking place, there appeared in the heavens above the city a light brighter than any star.

**43** And many looked upward.

**44** And some pointed in astonishment.

**45** Yet others continued with their business, believing it to be some military craft.

**46** But the light increased.

**47** And it descended lower.

**48** And its shape became visible.

**49** And it was unlike any vessel fashioned by the hands of men.

**50** Then fear fell upon many who beheld it.

**51** Some fled.

**52** Others stood frozen.

**53** The soldiers raised their weapons.

**54** Yet no weapon was fired.

**55** For all were overcome by confusion.

**56** And suddenly the earth shook.

**57** And a sound filled the air, deep and powerful, though no source thereof could be seen.

**58** Then there descended from the vessel a great beam of blue light.

**59** And it fell upon the place where Lucky, Astra, Kaito, and the weapon stood.

**60** And those who were touched by the light felt their bodies grow weightless.

**61** The soldiers cried out.

**62** The merchants fled.

**63** The crowds scattered in terror.

**64** Yet Lucky and Astra could neither run nor resist.

**65** For an unseen force held them fast.

**66** And they began to rise from the ground.

**67** Kaito and certain of his men rose also.

**68** And the weapon was lifted with them.

**69** Then many witnessed these things and trembled greatly.

**70** Yet none understood what purpose lay behind them.

**71** And as Lucky ascended into the light, he looked down upon the city growing smaller beneath him.

**72** And he perceived that the life he had known was ending.

**73** For beyond the stars a greater conflict awaited him.

**74** A conflict not between nations only, but between powers hidden since the foundation of the world.

**75** Thus ended the days of ignorance.

**76** And thus began the journey of the Soul Pen.
