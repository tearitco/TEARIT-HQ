# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 13

### The First Mother Awakens

**1** And it came to pass after the archives had yielded up their warning, that Lucky and his company descended by the hidden coordinates unto a chamber far below the known works of New Antioch.

**2** And the passages grew older as they descended, until the walls seemed not built but grown, and the air itself carried the scent of dust, saline decay, and something like rain upon stone.

**3** Then Cassian said, "We cross now into a place no priest is meant to name."

**4** And Mara answered, "Then let us proceed quietly."

**5** Yet before long the way opened into a great subterranean cathedral whose ceiling vanished into darkness and whose floor was marked by concentric rings of dormant machinery.

**6** And at the center thereof stood a cradle larger than any transport vessel.

**7** Therefore Lucky beheld it and knew at once that this was no mere engine, but the same kind of shrine the records had shown.

**8** Then the Soul Pen warmed in his hand.

**9** And symbols shifted across its face as if it recognized the chamber.

**10** Thereupon a voice came from the walls and said, "HEIR SIGNAL CONFIRMED."

**11** And another voice answered, "GENETIC BRIDGE PRESENT."

**12** Then Astra gripped Lucky’s arm and said, "If that thing calleth thee family, I shall become immediately suspicious of both thee and it."

**13** Yet no attack came.

**14** Instead the lights awoke one by one, and the cradle unfolded like a flower made from metal, bone, and crystal.

**15** And within it lay a Mother.

**16** Not a statue.

**17** Not a corpse.

**18** But a living ancient being larger than any man had imagined.

**19** Its skin shimmered like carved pearl over starless depths.

**20** Its veins glowed faintly beneath translucent flesh.

**21** And its breath moved through the chamber like a tide.

**22** Then all who saw it fell silent.

**23** For the sight of it was like beholding the memory of a world before history.

**24** Thereafter the Mother opened one eye.

**25** And it fixed upon Lucky.

**26** Then the chamber spoke in a voice neither male nor female, saying, "Bridge child."

**27** "Found child."

**28** "At last."

**29** And Lucky said, though his voice was shaken, "Why sayest thou this unto me?"

**30** Then the Mother answered, "Because thou wert made to be found."

**31** And Cassian covered his face and whispered, "The old prayers were true."

**32** Meanwhile the systems of the chamber began to awaken around them.

**33** Rings of light spun overhead.

**34** Pipes began to pulse.

**35** Ancient reservoirs filled with a soft red glow.

**36** And within the walls slept countless connected systems waiting upon a command that had not been spoken in ages.

**37** Then Mara said, "If this be a holy place, it is the most expensive holy place I have ever seen."

**38** Yet even as she spoke, the chamber answered with new images.

**39** And the company beheld Mars in an ancient age, red and wild, before cities, before colonies, before the first flags of Earth had ever touched its soil.

**40** They saw the Mothers shaping living foundations, raising ecosystems from barren stone, and planting vast biological structures that joined mind and matter as one.

**41** Then the vision darkened.

**42** For there came a shadow upon the stars.

**43** Not a fleet.

**44** Not a firestorm.

**45** But a spreading wrongness.

**46** And where it passed, life twisted, dreams turned hostile, and thriving worlds became husks of themselves.

**47** Then the Mother within the chamber trembled.

**48** And it said, "Carcazod."

**49** Thereupon the archives showed the final war.

**50** The Mothers fleeing.

**51** The Nests being sealed.

**52** A bridge program designed to bind heir and Mother together.

**53** And a child, one among the ancient visions, standing between them.

**54** Then Lucky understood that the image of the child was also his own image in some hidden way beyond ordinary blood.

**55** Therefore he asked, "Am I that child?"

**56** And the Mother answered, "Thou art the line that was prepared."

**57** "Thou art the hand that can wake us safely."

**58** "Or fail and wake the wound."

**59** Then the chamber shook.

**60** For something deeper below had sensed the awakening.

**61** The Mother’s eyes widened.

**62** And it said, "It heard us."

**63** Then alarms cried out through the lower systems of Mars.

**64** Red lights flashed.

**65** And distant machinery answered with a rhythm that did not belong to any healthy heart.

**66** Therefore Astra seized Lucky and cried, "Whatever this thing is, it doth not awaken alone."

**67** And the Mother replied, "No."

**68** "It never did."

**69** Then the chamber opened an inner gate.

**70** And beyond it lay records, weapons, and sealed corridors leading toward the oldest guts of the colony.

**71** Therefore the company entered, knowing that the truth of the Mothers had only begun to reveal the truth of Mars.
