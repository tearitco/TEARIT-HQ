# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 15

### The Fall of New Antioch

**1** And it came to pass after the Dream had begun speaking through the colony, that New Antioch entered a season of terror such as no governor had foretold and no priest had the courage to name aloud.

**2** For the upper districts lost power in waves.

**3** The transit lines halted.

**4** The blood markets collapsed into riots.

**5** And families fled into the streets as alarms cried from every tower.

**6** Then the Queen addressed the populace through the city screens and commanded all citizens to remain calm and to await official instruction.

**7** Yet while she yet spoke, one of the screens behind her showed a shape moving beneath the city.

**8** And the transmission cut out.

**9** Thereafter panic became impossible to contain.

**10** Then the Flesh Priests emerged in greater numbers than before, carrying censers, scanners, and weapons hidden within ceremonial robes.

**11** And they proclaimed that the disturbance was proof of divine selection.

**12** But others among the people cried that the Priests had caused the ruin by their secret dealings with the old systems.

**13** Thus the streets became divided.

**14** And the Colony began to break into factions.

**15** Then Lucky and his company rose from the lower chambers with the Mother’s warnings still upon them.

**16** And they found the city in chaos.

**17** Therefore Astra said, "The kingdom is coming apart."

**18** And Mara answered, "Nay."

**19** "The lie that held it together is coming apart."

**20** Then they saw citizens changing.

**21** Some became rigid and obedient, as though listening to an inner command.

**22** Some became violent.

**23** Some spoke in the language of the Priests though they had never entered their orders.

**24** And some grew small, roach-like, and escaped into drains and vents.

**25** Then Cassian cried out, "The corruption hath spread beyond the nests!"

**26** And the Mother said, "Not beyond."

**27** "It hath spread through them."

**28** Thereafter the Queen ordered the orbital barriers sealed and the offworld ports locked, but the command came too late.

**29** For several transport craft had already departed with altered crews, carrying contagion toward the stations and beyond.

**30** Then the Black Queen confronted her ministers and demanded the truth.

**31** And one among them confessed that the colony had long exported blood, tissue, and data to Earth under approved commercial channels.

**32** Therefore the Queen understood at last that the wound was not confined to Mars.

**33** For what had been hidden here had already been shared there.

**34** Then the chamber beneath the city opened wider.

**35** And from its depths came a pulse that shook every reactor, heart, and reservoir.

**36** And many who heard it fell to their knees.

**37** For the sound was not merely noise.

**38** It was summons.

**39** Then the Mother said unto Lucky, "Choose."

**40** "Seal the lower wound and preserve the city in part, or pursue the source and risk the rest."

**41** Therefore Lucky looked upon the collapsing city, the fleeing people, the Queen’s failing authority, and the widening darkness below.

**42** And he answered, "If the root remaineth, the fruit shall keep falling."

**43** Then he descended with Astra, Mara, and Cassian toward the oldest chamber beneath Mars.

**44** And the Queen, learning of their intent, sent soldiers and priests alike to follow, for she knew that if the source were not confronted now, no wall, fleet, or decree would prevent the coming end.

**45** Thus the fall of New Antioch began.

**46** Yet the fall was not merely of buildings and streets.

**47** It was the fall of certainty.

**48** The fall of inheritance.

**49** The fall of every story told to make the colony seem eternal.

**50** And beneath that ruin waited the thing that had dreamt through ages without mercy.

**51** Carcazod.
