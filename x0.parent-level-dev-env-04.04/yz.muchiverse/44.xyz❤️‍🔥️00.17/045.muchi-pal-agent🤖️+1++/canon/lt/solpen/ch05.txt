# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 5

### The Voyage of the SS Sol

**1** And it came to pass after the Trial of the Moon was completed, and after the Alpha had fallen, that Sol gathered Lucky, Astra, and those chosen for the journey, and led them unto a great docking chamber hidden beneath the lunar surface.

**2** Now the chamber was vast beyond measure, so that a city might have stood comfortably within it, and yet there remained room to spare.

**3** And many elevators descended into its depths, carrying workers, engineers, soldiers, merchants, and travelers from every district of the Moon.

**4** For word had spread that a departure was at hand.

**5** Then Lucky looked into the abyss of the chamber and beheld a vessel unlike any he had ever seen.

**6** And its length exceeded many skyscrapers laid end to end.

**7** Its hull gleamed with silver and gold.

**8** Its engines shone with a pale blue radiance.

**9** And countless lights illuminated its surface like stars scattered across a night sky.

**10** Then Astra gazed upon it and said, "This is no ship."

**11** But Sol answered, "By the standards of older civilizations, it is merely a transport."

**12** And Astra replied, "Then I should very much like to see what they considered a warship."

**13** Yet Sol did not answer.

**14** Then there came many travelers unto the vessel.

**15** Some were merchants journeying unto Mars.

**16** Some were engineers.

**17** Some were diplomats.

**18** Others were pilgrims seeking knowledge.

**19** And among them moved strangers whose purposes were hidden.

**20** For wherever men gather, secrets gather also.

**21** Now the vessel was called the SS Sol.

**22** And though many believed it to have been named after its master, the truth of the matter was uncertain.

**23** For the ship was older than most nations of Earth.

**24** And there were records concerning its origin which even Sol would not discuss.

**25** Therefore they boarded the vessel.

**26** And as they entered, Lucky marveled greatly.

**27** For within the ship were gardens beneath artificial suns.

**28** There were rivers flowing through crystal channels.

**29** There were marketplaces and halls of commerce.

**30** There were libraries whose shelves stretched farther than the eye could follow.

**31** And there were districts where entire communities dwelt permanently aboard the vessel.

**32** Then Lucky said, "This ship is a city."

**33** And Sol answered, "A city that travels."

**34** And Astra said, "Then how many souls dwell here?"

**35** Sol replied, "On this voyage, nearly two hundred thousand."

**36** Then Astra stared at him.

**37** And she said, "You transported an army."

**38** Yet Sol answered, "Compared to what lies ahead, this is not an army."

**39** Thus the vessel departed from the Moon.

**40** And many stood before the great observation windows to watch.

**41** They beheld the lunar surface recede into darkness.

**42** And the Earth appeared beyond it, beautiful and distant.

**43** Then the engines of the SS Sol awakened.

**44** Their light filled the void.

**45** And the vessel began its journey toward Mars.

**46** During the first days of the voyage, Lucky sought to learn more concerning the Soul Pen.

**47** Therefore he spent many hours in the Training Atrium.

**48** And there he met apprentices from many worlds.

**49** Some were born upon Earth.

**50** Some upon Mars.

**51** Some within orbital colonies.

**52** And some came from places whose names were unknown to him.

**53** Yet all sought mastery of the Pen.

**54** And each possessed differing talents.

**55** One could shape walls from air.

**56** Another could repair damaged machines.

**57** Another could create light where darkness prevailed.

**58** Then Lucky perceived how little he truly understood.

**59** And humility entered his heart.

**60** Meanwhile Astra preferred not the classrooms but the combat arenas.

**61** And she tested herself continually against soldiers, security officers, and mercenaries.

**62** And her reputation spread quickly throughout the vessel.

**63** For many challenged her.

**64** Yet few defeated her.

**65** Now among the passengers there traveled a man called Jonah Reed.

**66** And he was a merchant of information.

**67** For he bought secrets and sold them at great profit.

**68** And hearing rumors concerning Lucky and Astra, he sought them out.

**69** Then he approached their table within a marketplace and said, "I have heard that ye came from the Moon Facility."

**70** But Lucky answered cautiously, saying, "Many people came from the Moon."

**71** And Jonah smiled.

**72** For he understood evasive men.

**73** Then he sat beside them uninvited.

**74** And he said, "I have also heard whispers of Mars."

**75** "And whispers are often more valuable than facts."

**76** Then Astra said, "Speak plainly."

**77** Therefore Jonah leaned closer and lowered his voice.

**78** Saying, "The Colony of New Antioch is changing."

**79** "Ships arrive and disappear."

**80** "Workers vanish."

**81** "Entire sectors are sealed."

**82** "And strange religions flourish beneath the authorities' notice."

**83** Then Lucky said, "What sort of religion?"

**84** And Jonah answered, "The sort that worships something real."

**85** Thereupon he departed.

**86** Leaving them troubled.

**87** Now as the voyage continued, there occurred certain unusual events.

**88** For several passengers reported dreams unlike ordinary dreams.

**89** And these dreams spread throughout the vessel.

**90** Many dreamed of Mars.

**91** Many dreamed of vast hearts beating beneath the surface.

**92** Many dreamed of a crimson eye opening in darkness.

**93** Yet none understood the meaning thereof.

**94** Then one night Lucky himself dreamed.

**95** And in the dream he stood upon a red plain.

**96** The sky above him was black.

**97** The stars were hidden.

**98** And before him stood a doorway larger than any mountain.

**99** Upon the doorway were symbols unknown unto mankind.

**100** Then the doorway began to open.

**101** Yet before he could behold what lay beyond, he awoke.

**102** And his heart was troubled.

**103** Therefore he sought Sol.

**104** And he found him standing alone within an observation deck.

**105** Looking toward Mars.

**106** Then Lucky told him the dream.

**107** Yet Sol was silent.

**108** And this silence troubled Lucky more than any answer might have.

**109** At length Sol spoke.

**110** Saying, "Dreams are rarely accidents."

**111** Then Lucky asked, "What was the doorway?"

**112** But Sol answered, "A question."

**113** And Lucky said, "What is the answer?"

**114** Then Sol looked upon Mars.

**115** And he replied, "That remains to be seen."

**116** Thereafter many days passed.

**117** And the red planet grew larger before them.

**118** The passengers gathered often to watch its approach.

**119** For its deserts and canyons became visible.

**120** And its cities shone beneath great domes.

**121** Then at last the vessel entered Martian space.

**122** Yet as they drew near New Antioch, an alarm sounded throughout the bridge.

**123** And officers hurried unto their stations.

**124** Therefore Sol went immediately unto the command deck.

**125** And Lucky and Astra followed after him.

**126** Then they beheld strange readings displayed upon the great screens.

**127** There were energy signatures beneath the colony.

**128** Signals of unknown origin.

**129** And patterns which none could explain.

**130** Then one officer said, "These readings were not present on previous surveys."

**131** Another answered, "They are increasing."

**132** A third said, "Something beneath the colony is awakening."

**133** Then silence fell upon the bridge.

**134** For none wished to speak the thought forming in their minds.

**135** At length Sol stepped forward.

**136** And he looked upon the data.

**137** And concern appeared upon his face.

**138** Then Astra perceived this and said, "You know what it is."

**139** But Sol answered, "No."

**140** "I know only that it should not be there."

**141** Then the vessel continued its approach.

**142** And the domes of New Antioch shone beneath them.

**143** Yet beneath those shining domes, hidden far below the sight of ordinary men, ancient things stirred.

**144** And the name of Carcazod drew ever nearer.

**145** Thus ended the voyage of the SS Sol.

**146** And thus began the mysteries of Mars.
