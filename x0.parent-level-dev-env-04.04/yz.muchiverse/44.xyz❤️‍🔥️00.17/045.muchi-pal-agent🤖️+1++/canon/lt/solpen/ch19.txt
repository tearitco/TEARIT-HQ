# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 19

### Earth Receiveth the Gift

**1** And it came to pass after the Red Plague had crossed the thresholds of Mars, that Earth began to receive shipments, transmissions, and reports which seemed at first to be mere anomalies of commerce and bureaucracy.

**2** For laboratories on Earth took in tissue samples from Mars.

**3** Hospitals received advanced blood products.

**4** Research stations obtained archived colony data.

**5** And private corporations purchased licenses for new biotech methods that promised greater endurance, greater adaptation, and greater profit.

**6** Yet beneath each blessing there lay a hidden wound.

**7** For the copied systems carried the dream.

**8** And the dream entered well-ordered cities as quietly as a fever enters a room.

**9** Then people on Earth began to dream the same red dream.

**10** They dreamed of corridors beneath their feet.

**11** They dreamed of a voice in their blood.

**12** They dreamed of Mars before they had ever seen it.

**13** And in the research districts first signs of corruption appeared among the recipients of Martian materials.

**14** Then governments demanded answers.

**15** Corporations denied responsibility.

**16** And military councils began preparing for something they could not yet define.

**17** Meanwhile Lucky and his company, still on Mars, understood that the border had failed.

**18** Therefore the Queen ordered the surviving transport fleets to preserve whatever might yet be saved.

**19** And she sealed portions of the colony behind Bloodguard barriers so that at least one world might remain standing long enough to warn the next.

**20** Yet warning was no longer enough.

**21** For the Dream had already crossed the gap.

**22** Then the Mother said, "This is how it spreads."

**23** "Not as a spear."

**24** "As a gift."

**25** And Astra answered, "A gift no one asked for."

**26** Then Cassian whispered, "And one that many accepted willingly."

**27** Thus the first cases appeared on Earth.

**28** Not as monsters in the streets.

**29** But as citizens speaking strange words in boardrooms, hospitals, ministries, and homes.

**30** And by the time fear was named, imitation had already begun.

**31** Therefore the plague was not only red.

**32** It was social.

**33** It was memetic.

**34** It was biological.

**35** And it was obedient to no wall built by ordinary men.
