# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 14

### The Dream Beneath Blood

**1** And it came to pass after the First Mother had awakened, that the colony of New Antioch was troubled in every district, and men and women throughout the domes began to dream the same dream by night and to forget the same names by day.

**2** For some dreamed of endless red oceans beneath their feet.

**3** Some dreamed of cities made of flesh and crystal.

**4** Some dreamed of a face speaking from under the blood.

**5** And some woke screaming words they had never learned.

**6** Then the Queen summoned her counselors and the Flesh Priests, and they met behind sealed doors while the public was told that certain systems required emergency maintenance.

**7** Yet no maintenance was performed.

**8** Only concealment.

**9** Only delay.

**10** And below the city the Blood Banks began to alter their rhythm.

**11** The giant hearts pulsed unevenly.

**12** The pipes trembled.

**13** And within the reservoirs the red current took on strange glimmers, as if another pattern had entered the flow.

**14** Then Cassian said, "The Dream is entering the blood."

**15** And Mara answered, "Then this city hath been dreaming longer than we knew."

**16** Thereafter they passed into a chamber of records where the oldest archives lay sealed behind membrane-like doors.

**17** And the Mother guided them there by the Soul Pen, which now shone with a steadier, deeper light than before.

**18** Then Lucky read the recovered records.

**19** And they revealed that Carcazod was not merely a beast nor merely a god, but a corruption that moved through symbolic systems, through inherited memory, through biology, and through dreams.

**20** It did not merely kill.

**21** It persuaded.

**22** It did not merely break flesh.

**23** It taught flesh to betray itself.

**24** Then Astra said, "So the nightmares were the infection."

**25** And the Mother answered, "A part thereof."

**26** "The Dream is the road."

**27** "The body is the door."

**28** "The blood is the language."

**29** "And the Pen is the counterword."

**30** Therefore Lucky looked upon his hands with new dread.

**31** For he understood that he had not merely been chosen to carry a weapon.

**32** He had been chosen to answer a thing that could learn through writing, remembering, and imitation.

**33** Then alarms sounded above them.

**34** And a voice upon the colony-wide channels announced that the lower sectors were to be sealed immediately.

**35** But seals failed before they could be completed.

**36** For citizens in the upper districts began to collapse into trance-like states, speaking with strange harmonies and moving as though guided by distant signals.

**37** Some wept.

**38** Some laughed.

**39** Some turned their faces toward walls and listened for words no one else could hear.

**40** Then the first visible corruption appeared.

**41** A laborer’s skin rippled like water and briefly showed a pattern of roaches with painted smiles before returning to human form.

**42** Another citizen’s eyes filled with red static.

**43** Another began to grow translucent at the edges.

**44** And the Priests declared the phenomenon to be panic, then contagion, then divine trial.

**45** Yet none of those names made it less real.

**46** Therefore the Queen ordered the lower wards closed, the transit routes shut, and the public screened from all mention of the First Nest.

**47** But the Dream had already escaped the chamber of memory.

**48** For it moved through blood.

**49** Through sleep.

**50** Through machines.

**51** Through confession.

**52** Through imitation.

**53** Then Lucky asked the Mother, "Can it be stopped?"

**54** And she answered, "Not by force alone."

**55** "It must be named."

**56** "It must be resisted in thought as well as in flesh."

**57** "And the barriers must be remade before the wound widens."

**58** Then she pointed deeper beneath the chamber.

**59** And said, "There lies the old seal."

**60** "Breach it not unless ye are ready for what answers."

**61** Yet at that moment the colony shook.

**62** Great tremors passed through the foundations.

**63** And the lights overhead went briefly dark.

**64** When they returned, a new message glowed on every screen.

**65** It was not a sentence.

**66** It was a command.

**67** "OPEN THE DREAM."

**68** Then the blood in the reservoirs rose as if stirred by invisible hands.

**69** And the first true panic spread through New Antioch.
