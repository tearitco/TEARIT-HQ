* 🛸 After Chapter 6, Sol disappears from the story.
* 📡 Communications fail.
* 👤 The heroes assume he abandoned them, was captured, or was operating under some larger plan.
* 🌍 Much later on Earth, Lucky encounters a man who looks exactly like Sol — his own twin brother — creating one of the major mysteries of the Testament.

So Chapter 7 becomes the transition from **"under Sol's protection"** to **"we're on our own."**

And I'll make the verses much more like actual narrative Scripture: fewer verse breaks, longer verses, multiple events per verse.

# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 7

### The Vanishing of Sol

**1** And it came to pass after the alarms had sounded throughout the lower sectors of New Antioch, and after Mara had declared that their hiding place had been discovered, that confusion arose among the rebels, for some sought to flee immediately while others desired to remain and fight, believing that their years of secrecy had come at last to an end.

**2** Then Mara commanded them to gather what records they possessed concerning the Blood Banks, the disappearances, and the Flesh Priests, for she feared that if the authorities seized these things then the truth itself would be buried alongside those who had vanished beneath the city.

**3** And while the rebels prepared themselves, Lucky looked toward Sol, expecting instruction as he had done upon the Moon, for in every great danger thus far it had been Sol who possessed the answers; yet Sol stood strangely silent, his eyes fixed upon the depths below where the great hearts continued their dreadful beating.

**4** Then Astra approached him and said, "The alarms are spreading throughout the facility, and security forces are closing the gates. If thou hast a plan, now would be a good time to reveal it."

**5** But Sol answered only, "The path before you no longer belongs to me," and having spoken these words he turned away, leaving both Astra and Lucky perplexed.

**6** Then Mara returned bearing data drives, maps, and records stolen over many years, and she said unto them, "The eastern tunnels remain open for now, but they will not remain open long; therefore we must move before the security divisions surround the district."

**7** And as they departed from the observation platform, the sound of heavy doors echoed throughout the underground complex, and the lights changed from white unto crimson, causing many among the rebels to remember former raids and former losses.

**8** Yet when they had traveled some distance through the maintenance corridors, Lucky looked behind him and perceived that Sol no longer walked among them, and at first he supposed that the man had merely fallen behind.

**9** Therefore he searched the rear of the column and questioned those nearest him, but none could say when Sol had departed, and some even swore that they had not seen him since leaving the Blood Banks.

**10** Then Astra returned from scouting ahead and said, "He is gone," and there was irritation in her voice, though beneath it lay concern, "for I have searched every corridor behind us, and there is no sign of him."

**11** And Mara answered, "No man simply vanisheth within these tunnels."

**12** But Lucky remembered the Moon, the Soul Pen, the ship, and many other impossible things, and therefore he replied, "Sol is not most men."

**13** Yet despite their searching they found neither trace nor message, and all attempts to contact him through their communicators were met with silence, as though the man had never existed.

**14** Thus the company pressed onward, though uncertainty weighed heavily upon them, and many wondered whether Sol had abandoned them or whether some darker fate had befallen him.

**15** Now after they had journeyed through the lower sectors for a considerable time, they came unto a great transit junction where many tunnels converged, and there they discovered evidence that security forces had already arrived before them.

**16** For barricades had been erected across several passages, surveillance drones hovered overhead, and armed officers bearing the insignia of Colonial Security advanced methodically through the district, searching every chamber and every maintenance room.

**17** Then Mara cursed quietly and said, "They are moving faster than before; either someone betrayed us, or the authorities have become desperate."

**18** And one of the older rebels answered, "Perhaps both are true."

**19** Therefore the company concealed themselves among abandoned machinery while scouts observed the enemy positions, and they discovered that nearly every route toward the surface had already been sealed.

**20** Then fear arose among some of the younger rebels, for they believed themselves trapped beneath the city, and several spoke openly of surrendering in hopes of mercy.

**21** Yet Mara rebuked them sharply, saying, "Mercy is not what awaits those who disappear into the lower sectors, and ye know this as well as I."

**22** Then silence fell among them, for all had heard stories of the vanished, and many had lost friends or family to the hidden machinery of New Antioch.

**23** At length Astra examined the tactical displays and the maps recovered by the rebels, and she discovered a forgotten route passing beneath the old industrial district, a route omitted from official records and known only to maintenance crews who had long since disappeared.

**24** Therefore she said unto Mara, "If this map be accurate, we may escape through these tunnels and emerge near the eastern sectors, though the journey shall not be easy and there is no certainty that the passage remains intact."

**25** Then Mara nodded and said, "An uncertain path is better than no path at all."

**26** Thus they entered the forgotten tunnels, and the deeper they traveled the older the structures became, until the walls no longer resembled Martian engineering but appeared to belong unto some far earlier age, being covered with symbols, carvings, and patterns unlike any known language of Earth.

**27** And Lucky beheld these things and remembered the strange markings found beneath the Moon Facility, and a troubling thought entered his mind: that whatever mystery surrounded Carcazod was older than Mars, older than the colonies, and perhaps older than mankind itself.

**28** Then one of the rebels shone a light upon a carved wall, and there appeared an immense eye surrounded by concentric circles and geometric forms, and beneath it were symbols none could read.

**29** Yet as Lucky gazed upon the image, the Soul Pen at his side grew warm, and for a brief moment he thought he heard distant whispers carried upon the air.

**30** Therefore he touched the Pen and the sensation departed, but he spoke of it unto no one.

**31** Now while they journeyed through those forgotten depths, security forces entered the tunnels behind them and began closing the distance, and before long the echoes of pursuit could be heard reverberating through the ancient passages.

**32** Then Mara commanded the company to increase their pace, but many among them were exhausted and wounded, and it became evident that they could not outrun their pursuers indefinitely.

**33** Therefore Lucky took up the Soul Pen and began writing symbols of his own design, though his understanding remained imperfect, and he inscribed barriers, false corridors, and walls of crystal behind the retreating company, so that the enemy would be delayed.

**34** And though several attempts failed, others succeeded beyond expectation, and entire passages transformed before the astonished eyes of the rebels, causing many to regard Lucky with newfound respect.

**35** Yet even as these things occurred, a deeper danger awakened beneath the city, for the beating of the great hearts had begun to change, and throughout the underground networks of New Antioch a new rhythm spread like a signal passing through unseen veins.

**36** And none among the company understood its meaning, yet many felt unease, as though something vast had shifted in its sleep and become aware of their presence.

**37** Thus the rebels escaped immediate capture, and thus they vanished into the forgotten undercity of Mars; but the mystery of Sol remained unresolved, the name of Carcazod grew heavier upon their thoughts, and the road before them descended not toward safety but toward revelations yet more terrible than those already witnessed.

**38** And from that day forward they heard nothing from Sol, neither by message nor by voice nor by any device fashioned by men, and though many theories arose concerning his fate, the truth remained hidden until years later upon the Earth.

**39** Therefore the company continued alone, having lost their guide, their protector, and the only man who seemed to understand the full measure of the darkness gathering beneath Mars.

**40** And so began the Days of Separation.

This chapter feels closer to **Acts, Samuel, and Kings** because many verses carry whole scenes rather than single actions, and it establishes one of the major mysteries of the entire SoulPen saga: **the disappearance of Sol.**
