# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 6

### The City of New Antioch

**1** And it came to pass after the voyage of the SS Sol, that the great vessel entered the skies of Mars and descended toward the Colony called New Antioch.

**2** Now New Antioch was counted among the greatest settlements of mankind beyond the Earth, and its fame had spread throughout many worlds.

**3** For merchants praised its wealth.

**4** Governors praised its industry.

**5** Scholars praised its technologies.

**6** Yet beneath these praises there remained whispers which few wished to discuss openly.

**7** Therefore Sol, Lucky, Astra, and their companions looked carefully upon the city as they approached.

**8** And they beheld vast domes covering districts of habitation.

**9** They saw towers rising above the red deserts.

**10** They saw factories whose smoke ascended continually into the Martian sky.

**11** And they saw countless transports moving between districts like blood flowing through veins.

**12** Then Astra said, "It appeareth prosperous."

**13** But Sol answered, "Appearances are often the first casualty of truth."

**14** Thus the SS Sol docked within the primary orbital terminal.

**15** And many travelers departed.

**16** Merchants returned unto their businesses.

**17** Pilgrims continued their journeys.

**18** Diplomats met with officials.

**19** And the crowds flowed through the great transit halls like rivers.

**20** Yet among the travelers there moved watchers.

**21** Men and women whose purpose was observation.

**22** For the rulers of New Antioch desired knowledge concerning every visitor of importance.

**23** Therefore Sol instructed his companions, saying, "Speak carefully."

**24** "Trust slowly."

**25** "And remember that not every smile is a sign of friendship."

**26** Then they entered the city.

**27** And at first all seemed orderly.

**28** The streets were clean.

**29** The buildings were maintained.

**30** The marketplaces prospered.

**31** And the public displays proclaimed messages of unity, progress, and security.

**32** Yet Lucky perceived something troubling.

**33** For though the people obeyed the laws, few appeared joyful.

**34** Their conversations were brief.

**35** Their eyes remained lowered.

**36** And many glanced about before speaking even the simplest words.

**37** Then Lucky said unto Astra, "These people are afraid."

**38** And Astra answered, "The question is of whom."

**39** As they journeyed through the city, they observed recruitment centers, security checkpoints, surveillance towers, and information terminals.

**40** And each seemed ordinary when considered alone.

**41** Yet together they formed a pattern.

**42** A pattern of control.

**43** Then Sol brought them unto a district known as Founder's Market.

**44** There merchants from many worlds conducted business.

**45** Rare artifacts were traded.

**46** Exotic foods were sold.

**47** And travelers exchanged news from distant colonies.

**48** Yet even here whispers concerning disappearances could be heard.

**49** Therefore Lucky approached a vendor and asked concerning these matters.

**50** But the vendor immediately became uneasy.

**51** And he answered, "Such questions bring trouble."

**52** Then he returned unto his work and would say no more.

**53** Now among the crowds there appeared a woman watching them from afar.

**54** She wore the garments of an ordinary worker.

**55** Yet her eyes missed little.

**56** And after observing them for some time, she departed.

**57** Thereafter the day passed.

**58** And evening came upon New Antioch.

**59** The lights of the city shone brightly beneath the domes.

**60** And many citizens returned unto their homes.

**61** Yet Lucky noticed the same woman again.

**62** This time she approached openly.

**63** Then she said quietly, "If ye seek the truth, follow me."

**64** Astra immediately placed her hand near her weapon.

**65** But the woman continued.

**66** Saying, "The authorities already know ye are here."

**67** "The question is whether ye shall learn anything before they decide what to do with you."

**68** Then Sol regarded the woman carefully.

**69** And after a moment he said, "Lead on."

**70** Thus she guided them through narrow streets.

**71** Through maintenance tunnels.

**72** Through forgotten corridors hidden beneath commercial districts.

**73** Until at length they arrived at an abandoned warehouse.

**74** And there many people were gathered.

**75** Some were miners.

**76** Some were engineers.

**77** Some were smugglers.

**78** Some were former security officers.

**79** And all shared one thing in common.

**80** They no longer trusted the rulers of New Antioch.

**81** Then the woman introduced herself.

**82** Saying, "My name is Mara."

**83** "And these are the Free Colonists."

**84** Then Lucky asked, "What is it that ye oppose?"

**85** But Mara answered, "The better question is what we fear."

**86** Thereupon she activated a hidden display.

**87** And images appeared before them.

**88** At first the images showed ordinary workers.

**89** Then they showed lists of names.

**90** Then records of missing persons.

**91** Then entire sectors marked as restricted.

**92** And the number of disappearances was very great.

**93** Then Astra said, "Where do they go?"

**94** Mara answered, "Beneath the city."

**95** Then Lucky said, "For what purpose?"

**96** But Mara shook her head.

**97** Saying, "We know only fragments."

**98** "Those who enter do not return."

**99** Then one among the rebels stepped forward.

**100** And he said, "I worked in the lower sectors."

**101** "I saw things."

**102** Yet as he spoke his hands trembled.

**103** Then Mara placed a hand upon his shoulder.

**104** And he continued.

**105** Saying, "There are machines below."

**106** "Machines unlike any I had ever seen."

**107** "And there are pipes."

**108** "Miles of pipes."

**109** "All flowing toward something deeper."

**110** Then silence filled the room.

**111** For none desired to imagine what waited at the end of such conduits.

**112** Therefore Mara spoke once more.

**113** Saying, "Come."

**114** "There is something ye must see."

**115** Thus they departed from the warehouse.

**116** And they traveled through passages long forgotten by official maps.

**117** At length they reached a concealed observation platform.

**118** And beyond the platform lay a vast underground chamber.

**119** Then Lucky looked down.

**120** And what he beheld filled him with horror.

**121** For throughout the chamber stood enormous glass reservoirs.

**122** And within them flowed rivers of blood.

**123** Countless pipes connected the reservoirs together.

**124** And the pipes stretched away into darkness.

**125** Yet this was not the most terrible thing.

**126** For among the reservoirs stood gigantic hearts.

**127** Each greater than a house.

**128** And they beat continually.

**129** Without rest.

**130** Without ceasing.

**131** Their pulses shook the chamber floor.

**132** And their rhythm echoed throughout the depths.

**133** Then Astra stepped backward.

**134** And even Sol remained silent.

**135** For the sight was dreadful.

**136** Then Mara whispered, "The Blood Banks."

**137** "The authorities call them infrastructure."

**138** "The Priests call them blessings."

**139** "We call them abominations."

**140** Then Lucky asked, "What powers them?"

**141** Yet none answered immediately.

**142** For no one truly knew.

**143** Then movement appeared below.

**144** And robed figures emerged among the machinery.

**145** Their garments were crimson.

**146** Their faces were hidden.

**147** And strange symbols adorned their robes.

**148** Then Mara said, "The Flesh Priests."

**149** "Servants of the hidden doctrine."

**150** "Servants of something they call Carcazod."

**151** Now as Lucky listened, he heard the name for the first time.

**152** Yet something about it troubled him.

**153** As though the word itself carried weight.

**154** Then one of the Priests lifted his hands.

**155** And those gathered below bowed their heads.

**156** Therefore the Priest proclaimed, saying,

**157** "Life floweth."

**158** "Strength increaseth."

**159** "The Great Design unfoldeth."

**160** Then the assembled followers answered,

**161** "All praise unto Carcazod."

**162** And hearing this, many among the rebels shuddered.

**163** For they knew not whether Carcazod was a man.

**164** A machine.

**165** A spirit.

**166** Or something far older.

**167** Then suddenly a warning light appeared upon one of the control panels.

**168** Mara looked upon it.

**169** And fear entered her face.

**170** Then she said, "We must leave."

**171** Astra asked, "Why?"

**172** Mara answered, "Because someone hath found us."

**173** At that moment alarms sounded throughout the lower sectors.

**174** Red lights flashed among the Blood Banks.

**175** Security gates began to close.

**176** And distant footsteps echoed through the tunnels.

**177** Then Sol looked toward the darkness.

**178** And he said, "Our arrival hath been noticed."

**179** Then Lucky took hold of the Soul Pen.

**180** And Astra prepared her weapon.

**181** For they understood that the mysteries of Mars would not reveal themselves willingly.

**182** Thus was uncovered the first secret of New Antioch.

**183** And thus was heard the name of Carcazod among them for the first time.

**184** Yet far below the Blood Banks, beyond the sight of rebels and priests alike, something ancient stirred in its sleep.

**185** And its dreams were spreading through the colony.

**186** For the hour of awakening drew nearer.
