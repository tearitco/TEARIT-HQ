# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 21

### The Future of Sol Pen

**1** And it came to pass after the beginning of the last age, that Lucky stood upon a broken overlook and beheld the world changing beneath the weight of what Mars had sent.

**2** And cities once certain of their permanence now fortified themselves against dream, blood, and imitation.

**3** And across the heavens the old routes of trade became routes of quarantine.

**4** Then the Mother spoke softly, saying, "The cycle hath not ended."

**5** And Lucky answered, "Then show me how it is broken."

**6** Yet she said, "It is broken only by one who will pay for it."

**7** Then there came a figure into the chamber, masked and patient, and though age and circumstance concealed him, Lucky felt the old recognition stir within him.

**8** And the figure said, "Thou knowest my voice."

**9** Then the mask withdrew.

**10** And there stood Sol.

**11** Yet not the Sol of memory alone.

**12** For his bearing was older.

**13** His silence was heavier.

**14** And in his eyes dwelt the knowledge of a war already survived and of one not yet finished.

**15** Then Lucky said, "Art thou friend or warning?"

**16** And Sol answered, "Both."

**17** "I am what thou shalt become if thou failest to choose."

**18** "And I am what remains if thou dost choose and live."

**19** Then the Mother said, "The bridge is closing."

**20** And Sol lifted the broken Shadow Pen beside the Soul Pen, and the two lights answered each other like mirrored wounds.

**21** Then Lucky understood at last that the future had not merely waited for him.

**22** It had been guiding him.

**23** And whether he became savior, successor, or sacrifice remained still unwritten.

**24** Therefore he set the Soul Pen to the air.

**25** And he wrote not an ending, but a vow.

**26** That the Dream should not rule the living.

**27** That the Mothers should not be chained forever.

**28** That Mars should not be forgotten.

**29** And that the children of Earth might yet inherit a world not wholly possessed by Carcazod.

**30** Thus the Testament was sealed for a season.

**31** Yet the Pen remained in his hand.

**32** And the future remained open.
