# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 9

### The First Nest

**1** And it came to pass after the execution in the Arena of Iron, and after the clown had cried aloud concerning the First Nest and the Mothers and the thing which slept beneath the blood, that neither Lucky nor Astra could remove those words from their thoughts, for they seemed not the ravings of a condemned fool but the warning of a man who had seen something dreadful and despaired of convincing others.

**2** Therefore they returned unto the hidden refuge of the Free Colonists, and there throughout the night they questioned Mara and the elders of the resistance concerning the First Nest; yet many shook their heads, and others claimed only fragments of rumor, while a few became visibly uncomfortable and refused to speak at all.

**3** Then Mara arose and said, "There are stories older than the colony itself, stories whispered among miners, scavengers, and tunnel crews who venture farther beneath Mars than official maps permit, and these stories speak of chambers that existed before New Antioch, before the Blood Banks, and perhaps before mankind first set foot upon this world."

**4** And one elder answered her, saying, "Those are merely legends."

**5** Yet another replied, "Most legends begin as truths that powerful men prefer forgotten."

**6** Therefore silence fell upon the assembly.

**7** Then Mara produced an ancient data slate recovered many years earlier from a collapsed excavation site, and its contents were incomplete, corrupted by age and damage, yet certain entries remained legible.

**8** And among those entries appeared repeated references unto a location designated simply as NEST-01, alongside warnings that all access had been prohibited by direct order of the earliest colonial authorities.

**9** Then Astra said, "Whenever powerful people forbid questions, the questions are usually worth asking."

**10** And Lucky agreed.

**11** Thus preparations were made.

**12** Scouts gathered supplies, navigational equipment, weapons, medical packs, and stolen access codes, while engineers modified mining drones that might travel where men could not, for no one knew what conditions awaited them below.

**13** Yet before their departure, there came unto the refuge a traveler whose arrival surprised many, for he wore the robes of a Flesh Priest and bore the crimson markings of the Blood Banks upon his garments.

**14** Immediately weapons were raised against him, and several rebels demanded his execution.

**15** Yet the man neither fled nor resisted, but knelt upon the floor and said, "If ye kill me now, then ye shall remain ignorant; but if ye hear me, perhaps ye shall live a little longer."

**16** Then Mara ordered restraint, though reluctantly.

**17** And she asked the stranger his name.

**18** Therefore he answered, "I was once called Brother Cassian, though among the Priests names matter less than obedience."

**19** Then Astra replied, "That answer alone maketh me distrust thee."

**20** Yet Cassian smiled faintly and said, "Then thy instincts still function properly."

**21** And hearing this, several among the rebels laughed despite themselves.

**22** Thereafter Cassian spoke concerning the Blood Banks, and he revealed that many of the priests understood far less than they pretended, for they served systems older than their doctrines and obeyed rituals whose original purposes had long been forgotten.

**23** And he said, "Most believe the blood sustaineth the colony. This is true. Most believe the hearts regulate energy distribution. This also is true. Yet few ask why the system was designed in such a manner, and fewer still ask what it was originally built to feed."

**24** Then Lucky felt unease within his spirit.

**25** For he remembered the giant hearts beating beneath the city, and he remembered the strange rhythm that had changed during their escape.

**26** Therefore he asked, "What doth it feed?"

**27** But Cassian answered, "That question hath consumed better men than myself."

**28** Then he revealed a map stolen from restricted archives, and upon it appeared layers of tunnels extending far below the regions known even unto colonial engineers, descending toward structures that no official records publicly acknowledged.

**29** And at the center of those structures appeared a single designation.

**30** FIRST NEST.

**31** Therefore Mara decided the matter.

**32** Saying, "If answers exist, they lie below."

**33** Thus the company departed from the refuge and entered once more into the hidden arteries of Mars, and they journeyed through maintenance passages, abandoned mining routes, forgotten service corridors, and excavation shafts sealed generations earlier, while the city continued its daily life above them unaware of what moved beneath its foundations.

**34** Now the deeper they traveled, the stranger the architecture became, for the tunnels gradually ceased resembling human construction and instead displayed impossible symmetries, curved surfaces without visible joints, and walls that seemed grown rather than built.

**35** And one engineer touched such a wall and declared that no known fabrication method could have produced it, whereupon several others examined the structure and reached the same conclusion.

**36** Then fear entered many hearts.

**37** For men prefer mysteries that can be explained.

**38** Yet these mysteries resisted explanation.

**39** At length they came unto a vast cavern whose ceiling vanished into darkness and whose floor was covered with immense shell-like formations extending farther than their lights could reveal.

**40** Therefore the company advanced cautiously.

**41** And as they drew nearer they perceived that the formations were not stone.

**42** Neither were they metal.

**43** Nor were they any substance known to the sciences of Earth.

**44** Then Cassian whispered, "The eggs."

**45** And many turned toward him.

**46** Yet the former priest appeared more frightened than any among them.

**47** Therefore Mara said, "Explain thyself."

**48** And Cassian answered, "The oldest records speak of vessels discovered beneath Mars during the first excavations. Some claimed they were machines. Others claimed they were biological. The debates ended when entire research teams vanished."

**49** Then Astra said, "And these are those vessels?"

**50** But Cassian replied, "I pray that they are merely vessels."

**51** Thus they continued deeper.

**52** And before long they discovered markings upon the walls resembling those Lucky had seen beneath the Moon Facility, though here they appeared vastly older and covered entire chambers rather than isolated surfaces.

**53** Then the Soul Pen hanging at Lucky's side began to glow faintly.

**54** At first he attempted to conceal this from the others.

**55** Yet the glow increased.

**56** And strange symbols appeared along its surface without any action of his own.

**57** Therefore he removed the Pen from its holster.

**58** And immediately the symbols upon the walls responded.

**59** Some brightened.

**60** Others shifted position.

**61** While still others revealed layers hidden beneath the visible surface.

**62** Then every person present stepped backward.

**63** For they beheld technology—or perhaps something older than technology—awakening after ages beyond calculation.

**64** Thereupon a doorway appeared where moments before there had been only solid wall.

**65** And the doorway opened silently.

**66** Revealing darkness beyond.

**67** Then Astra looked at Lucky and said, "Every time that thing doth something unexpected, my confidence in reality decreases."

**68** Yet despite her words she was the first to enter.

**69** Therefore the others followed.

**70** And beyond the doorway lay a chamber unlike any they had previously encountered.

**71** The room was circular.

**72** Its ceiling resembled a night sky filled with moving stars.

**73** Great structures rose from the floor like pillars.

**74** And at the center stood a cradle vast beyond comprehension.

**75** Then the lights of their equipment illuminated what rested within.

**76** And all who saw it became silent.

**77** For within the cradle lay a creature.

**78** Not dead.

**79** Not fully alive.

**80** But sleeping.

**81** Its form was impossible to describe completely, appearing at once mechanical and biological, ancient and unborn, familiar and alien.

**82** And countless cables, veins, and conduits extended from its body into the surrounding architecture.

**83** Then Mara whispered, "By all the worlds..."

**84** Yet no one finished her sentence.

**85** For the creature suddenly moved.

**86** Not greatly.

**87** Only slightly.

**88** Yet that slight movement caused tremors throughout the chamber.

**89** Then monitors hidden within the walls activated.

**90** Symbols appeared.

**91** Lights awakened.

**92** And forgotten systems began operating once more.

**93** Therefore Cassian fell to his knees.

**94** And he cried out, "The Mothers preserve us."

**95** Then Lucky asked, "What are the Mothers?"

**96** Yet before Cassian could answer, another voice filled the chamber.

**97** It came not from any visible speaker.

**98** Nor from any living person.

**99** But from the structure itself.

**100** And the voice said:

**101** "NEST REACTIVATION DETECTED."

**102** "GENETIC LINEAGE CONFIRMED."

**103** "HEIR SIGNAL LOCATED."

**104** Then every light in the chamber turned toward Lucky.

**105** And every sensor focused upon him.

**106** While the Soul Pen shone brighter than it ever had before.

**107** Therefore Astra stepped between Lucky and the chamber.

**108** Though she knew not what threat she opposed.

**109** Then the ancient voice spoke again.

**110** Saying:

**111** "WELCOME HOME."

**112** At these words terror seized the company.

**113** For none understood their meaning.

**114** Yet Lucky felt something worse than terror.

**115** He felt recognition.

**116** As though some distant and forgotten part of himself had heard those words before.

**117** Thus the mystery deepened.

**118** For the First Nest had been found.

**119** Yet instead of answers it had offered only greater questions.

**120** And beneath the sleeping foundations of Mars, the ancient things began to awaken.
