# The Living Testament

## Book of the Soul Pen

### Chapter 4

### The Alpha of the Moon

**1** And it came to pass that Lucky, Astra, Sol, and those who remained of the Moon Guard descended into the lower sectors of the facility, for the Alpha had begun its ascent and there was no longer any possibility of avoiding confrontation.

**2** Now the lower regions of the station had not been seen by most who dwelt above, for many secrets were hidden there, and only those entrusted with the highest authority were permitted to enter.

**3** And the deeper they traveled, the colder the halls became, and the fewer signs of ordinary life remained.

**4** The walls were scarred by claws.

**5** The lights flickered continually.

**6** And many doors hung broken upon their hinges.

**7** Then they came upon a laboratory whose entrance had been torn apart.

**8** And within were overturned workstations, shattered containment vessels, and the remains of machines whose purposes could no longer be discerned.

**9** Yet among the wreckage there remained records.

**10** Therefore Sol examined them.

**11** And Astra said unto him, "What was this place?"

**12** Then Sol answered reluctantly and said, "A mistake."

**13** But Astra replied, "That is not an answer."

**14** And Sol said, "Many years ago certain men sought to restore creatures long vanished from the Earth."

**15** "They possessed knowledge."

**16** "They possessed resources."

**17** "Yet they lacked wisdom."

**18** For they desired not merely to resurrect the ancient beasts, but to improve them according to their own understanding."

**19** Then Lucky said, "Men always seem eager to improve things they barely understand."

**20** And Sol answered, "Thus much suffering enters the world."

**21** Now as they continued onward they discovered signs of battle.

**22** For many guards had made their stand in the corridors below.

**23** Yet their defenses had failed.

**24** Barricades lay broken.

**25** Weapons lay abandoned.

**26** And the marks of violence covered every surface.

**27** Then one among the engineers found a survivor hidden beneath a collapsed support beam.

**28** And they lifted the beam from him and brought him before Sol.

**29** The man was wounded.

**30** His face was pale.

**31** Yet he still breathed.

**32** Then Sol knelt beside him and asked, "What happened here?"

**33** And the man answered with trembling lips.

**34** Saying, "The others escaped first."

**35** "The smaller ones."

**36** "But then the Alpha awakened."

**37** At these words many exchanged fearful glances.

**38** And the wounded man continued, saying, "It remembered."

**39** Then Sol's expression darkened.

**40** For these words troubled him greatly.

**41** Yet Lucky perceived this and said, "What meaneth that?"

**42** And Sol answered, "Nothing good."

**43** But before further explanation could be given, the station shook violently.

**44** And a roar arose from somewhere in the darkness ahead.

**45** The sound was deeper than before.

**46** More powerful.

**47** More ancient.

**48** And all who heard it felt fear within their hearts.

**49** Then the wounded man seized Sol's arm.

**50** And he whispered, "It knoweth we are here."

**51** Thereafter his strength failed him.

**52** And he slept.

**53** Therefore the company pressed onward.

**54** At length they entered a chamber vast beyond expectation.

**55** For it extended deep into the lunar bedrock.

**56** And great machines surrounded its edges.

**57** While broken containment structures stood in its center.

**58** Then Astra beheld tracks crossing the chamber floor.

**59** And each footprint exceeded the size of a man.

**60** Therefore she said, "This creature is larger than the others."

**61** But Sol answered, "Much larger."

**62** Then suddenly all lights within the chamber failed.

**63** And darkness covered the place.

**64** Some raised their weapons.

**65** Others held their breath.

**66** Yet none could see what approached.

**67** Then there appeared two points of blue light within the darkness.

**68** They were like eyes.

**69** And they watched the company without blinking.

**70** Then the lights moved.

**71** And all understood that the creature had changed position.

**72** Yet none had seen it move.

**73** Therefore fear fell upon many.

**74** Suddenly emergency illumination activated.

**75** And pale light flooded the chamber.

**76** Then they beheld the Alpha.

**77** Its height exceeded that of any beast known upon Earth.

**78** Its flesh was transparent.

**79** Its skeleton shone like crystal.

**80** Strange symbols flowed continuously beneath its skin.

**81** And within its chest there burned a radiant core of blue fire.

**82** Thus it appeared less a creature and more a living equation written upon reality itself.

**83** Then several guards opened fire.

**84** Yet the Alpha advanced unhindered.

**85** Bullets shattered against invisible fields surrounding its body.

**86** And the beast answered with a roar that shook the chamber.

**87** Then many were thrown from their feet.

**88** Astra fired again.

**89** Yet the creature continued forward.

**90** Then Lucky cried unto Sol, saying, "How are we supposed to kill that?"

**91** But Sol answered, "You are asking the wrong question."

**92** Then Lucky replied, "What question should I ask?"

**93** And Sol said, "What is keeping it alive?"

**94** Thereupon Lucky looked again upon the creature.

**95** And he perceived the blue fire burning within its chest.

**96** Then he remembered the symbols he had seen moving beneath its flesh.

**97** And he understood that the Alpha was sustained not merely by biology, but by information.

**98** Therefore he took up the Soul Pen.

**99** And he began to write.

**100** Yet his first symbols failed.

**101** And the second pattern dissolved before completion.

**102** For the language of the Pen was difficult.

**103** And fear clouded his thoughts.

**104** Then Astra stepped before him.

**105** And she said, "I shall keep it occupied."

**106** Therefore she advanced toward the Alpha.

**107** Though all others retreated.

**108** And the beast charged against her.

**109** Yet Astra stood firm.

**110** Then she leapt aside at the last moment.

**111** And the Alpha crashed into a containment tower.

**112** The structure collapsed.

**113** Sparks filled the chamber.

**114** And for a brief moment the symbols within the creature became visible.

**115** Then Lucky perceived a pattern.

**116** And suddenly understanding entered his mind.

**117** Not complete understanding.

**118** But enough.

**119** Therefore he raised the Soul Pen.

**120** And he wrote a new sequence in the air.

**121** The symbols burned with blue light.

**122** And they arranged themselves around the Alpha.

**123** The creature roared.

**124** For it perceived danger.

**125** Then the symbols entered its body.

**126** And the radiant core within its chest flickered.

**127** Once.

**128** Twice.

**129** Then many fractures appeared throughout its form.

**130** The flowing symbols beneath its skin became unstable.

**131** The beast staggered.

**132** And the invisible fields surrounding it failed.

**133** Then the Moon Guards unleashed all remaining firepower.

**134** And Astra struck the creature repeatedly.

**135** While Lucky continued writing.

**136** At last the blue core shattered.

**137** And a great light burst forth from within the Alpha.

**138** The chamber shook.

**139** The machines failed.

**140** And the beast gave one final roar.

**141** Then it collapsed.

**142** And its body dissolved into countless fragments of luminous data.

**143** Which rose upward like sparks ascending from a fire.

**144** Thereafter silence filled the chamber.

**145** None spoke for a time.

**146** For all were astonished.

**147** Then Astra laughed and sat upon a broken machine.

**148** And she said, "Next time perhaps begin with the part about rewriting reality."

**149** But Sol did not answer.

**150** For he was looking upon one of the shattered containment structures.

**151** Then Lucky followed his gaze.

**152** And he beheld a symbol carved deep into the metal.

**153** A symbol older than the facility.

**154** Older than the experiments.

**155** Older than the Moon Colony itself.

**156** And though neither Lucky nor Astra understood it, Sol did.

**157** Therefore concern appeared upon his face once more.

**158** Then Lucky asked him, saying, "What is it?"

**159** And Sol answered, "A warning."

**160** And Lucky said, "From whom?"

**161** Then Sol remained silent for a long while.

**162** At length he answered.

**163** Saying, "From those who came before."

**164** Then Astra said, "Before whom?"

**165** And Sol replied, "Before us all."

**166** Thus ended the Trial of the Moon.

**167** And the Alpha was no more.

**168** Yet greater mysteries awaited beyond the red world called Mars.

**169** For even then events were unfolding within the Colony of New Antioch.

**170** And the name of Carcazod was beginning to spread among the stars.
