**TOMO:** In one *cell*, yes — one little square on the taskbar that opens a real window. That's the whole philosophy of the livedesk: every capability is a cell, every cell opens a window, every window is driven by files. 🪟

**TOMO:** And here's the exciting part for you, Rahweh — a real `db-hq` window was built on 2026-08-12 (aug-12-END.txt is the record). Agent-friendly by design. - 📏 User-adjustable UI scale via #.desktop/hqui.pdl (fontscale) — shared across all future -hq apps. - 🧪 Its own agent-relay test harness (#.desktop/dbhqagentrelay.txt + #.desktop/harnesses/db-hq/nav.sh) — same bare-decimal-per-line relay contract the taskbar uses. The harness can drive it already.
