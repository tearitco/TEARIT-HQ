    li x12, <V>

0→OFF; any other `<V>` is not matched (honest: a stray literal "ON"
in a hand-written PAL is not claimed as a block). Purely additive -
the renderer's strncmp prefix parser ignores unknown prefixes, so
pre-patch renderers are byte-unaffected.

real `.scratch-block` class (background + border, colors modeled on
